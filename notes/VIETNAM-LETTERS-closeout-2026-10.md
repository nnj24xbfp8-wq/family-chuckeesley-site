# Vietnam letters — close-out (October 2026)

Mining of the `dad###` scan set (`src/assets/family/originals/vietnam-letters/`) is **complete**. This note replaces the working table `vietnam-scan-catalog.md`, keeping only what's still open. Everything else in that file was resolved and is recorded in the published entries' `source` fields.

**Count:** 260 scans (by base name, after removing the IMG_6241 copy); 224 attached to entries. The 36 unattached scans fall into the groups below. None is an unread letter.

## Final pass (1 Oct 2026)

- **Published** `charlie-to-terrie-1970-12-04-security-platoon-da-nang` (dad210 + dad211, converted from .jp2). Complete, one sheet, both sides. It opens the December 1970 rear-area run: a sixty-day posting to the XXIV Corps security platoon. The day in the date header is overwritten and could be 4 or 9. **Check the envelope.**
- **Attached** dad208 (closing: "discussed when I call / Love Charles") to `charlie-to-terrie-training-physical-and-mental-tests` as page 2. The pairing is probable, matched by pencil, hand and scan series.
- **Reconstructed, not published:** dad250 → dad251 → dad252 are consecutive pages of one letter. The join is exact: dad251 ends "the army is one hell of a strain to put a", and dad252 begins "relationship through." dad252 closes: *"Well I'm intoxicated as I said but I'll mail this letter as is. Please answer it and expect a more sober & serious letter to follow. Love Charlie."* That is almost certainly the drunken letter the **18 December 1970** letter (dad253, the next scan) apologizes for, so the date is c. 15–17 Dec 1970. Page 1 is missing. dad246 (Dec 1970, "I leave here Feb 4… I'll finish the letter tomorrow") is on the same paper series and may be another page of it. **Decision (Chuck, 1 Oct 2026): keep private.** Do not publish, summarize, or attach these scans to any entry, including the 18 December letter.
