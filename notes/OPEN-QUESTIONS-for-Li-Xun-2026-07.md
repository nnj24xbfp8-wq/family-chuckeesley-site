## English summary (for Chuck)

1. **Shang Yaoli 尚耀礼 (大舅) — birth year still unknown.** Any year at all?
2. **Li Zhongchu 李仲初 (grandfather) — Year of the Monkey.** We've provisionally set **1920**; please confirm the actual year. (Old record wrongly said 1921.)
3. **Shang Yaozhen 尚耀真 (grandmother) — Year of the Ox.** We've provisionally set **1925**, but this collides with her brother **Yaofu Shang 尚耀福 (recorded 1925–2014)** — they can't both be 1925. Her sister **Yaoxiang 尚耀香** is recorded as **1911**. So please confirm (a) Yaozhen's actual year, (b) Yaofu's actual year, and (c) the birth order of the four siblings — the archive currently calls Yaozhen the "eldest daughter," but Yaoxiang (1911) looks older.
4. **The "woman holding a baby, with a young girl" photo (now dated ~1974–75).** Li Xun's note says the woman holding the baby is **Yaoxiang, not Yaozhen**, and that one child was **born in 1972** — so it can't be Lijie/Junjun. **Who are the two children, and is ~1974–75 the right date?**
5. **Zhou Ling 周玲 (Lijie's father, b. 1955) — the note says this name is wrong.** What are the correct characters? The archive currently has both **周玲** and **周凌** in different places — which is right (or is it something else)?
