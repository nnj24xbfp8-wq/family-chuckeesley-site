Found on a shelf at the **Stanford Alumni Center** in August 2026 by [Chuck](/family/charles-eric-eesley/), Briana and Bryson — the senior-class pages of the 1948 *Quad*, under the banner **"Class of '48 — PRE-MEDICINE."**

![A page from the 1948 Stanford Quad yearbook, senior class section headed Class of 48 Pre-Medicine, with a column of names and a grid of portrait photographs. The listing reads ROBERT E. WILDERMETH, Off campus, Marietta, Ohio, between WILLIAM J. WHALEN of Palo Alto and MARGARET C. WINSTON of Berkeley.](../../assets/family/originals/robert-earl-wildermuth-stanford-quad-1948-yearbook.jpeg)

The entry:

> **ROBERT E. WILDERMETH**
> *Off campus*
> Marietta, Ohio

**The surname is misspelled** — *Wildermeth*, not Wildermuth. A German name got one vowel wrong in a California yearbook, which is a small instance of the thing that happens to this surname repeatedly across the record: *Wildermuth, Wildermeth, Wildermoth, Wilder Muth*.

## Which face is his

The portraits sit in a grid to the right of the name column, four across. The bottom row of this group corresponds to the four names listed beside it, left to right:

| Name in column | Portrait |
|---|---|
| William J. Whalen · *Village* · Palo Alto | 1st — dark-haired man |
| **Robert E. Wildermeth · *Off campus* · Marietta, Ohio** | **2nd — fair-haired young man, dark suit, light tie** |
| Margaret C. Winston · *Lagunita* · Berkeley | 3rd — the woman |
| Peter Y. Yee · *Off campus* · Reno, Nev. | 4th |

The mapping is checkable rather than guessed: **Margaret Winston is the only woman in the group and she is the only woman in the row**, and Peter Yee falls fourth in both lists. With two independent anchors at positions three and four, Robert Earl is the second portrait.

He was **24** when this was printed, three years off a B-24 navigator's seat over Biak, Leyte and Mindoro.

## "Off campus" — and now we know where

The single most useful word in the entry is *"Off campus."* Robert Earl had married **[Dorothy Marie Davis](/family/dorothy-davis-wildermuth/)** on 20 April 1946, months before arriving on the GI Bill; married veterans did not live in Encina or Toyon. His [registrar's card](/archive/robert-earl-wildermuth-stanford-transcript/) supplies the actual addresses — **Box 276, Pine Lane, Los Altos**, and a **S. Stanford Avenue address in Menlo Park** — the first Bay Area addresses this archive has ever held for the family.

## Pre-Medicine

The banner is a small piece of biography by itself. He was listed under **Pre-Medicine**, applied to medical school in his senior year, and was turned down. He took the **BA in Biological Sciences**, conferred 1 October 1948, and went into industry, then back into the Air Force. Twenty-four years later he finished a **B.S. in Medical Record Administration** at the University of Central Florida — the medical career reached at a slant, from the records side.

*Sixty-seven years after this page was printed, his grandson joined the Stanford faculty. Bryson and Briana were in the room when the page was found.*
