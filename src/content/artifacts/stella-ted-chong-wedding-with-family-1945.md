An indoor wedding-day photograph from **[Stella Sunn](/family/stella/) and [Ted Chong](/family/ted-chong/)'s wedding, c. April 1945** &mdash; a frame distinct from the [Cassady Avenue group photo](/archive/stella-ted-chong-wedding-cassady-group-1945/), the [porch group](/archive/stella-ted-chong-wedding-day-cassady-porch-1945/), and the [bride-and-groom portrait](/archive/stella-ted-chong-wedding-portrait-1945/) already in the archive. Stella, in her white gown and veil, is seated on a staircase with her mother, **[Mabel Lee Sunn](/family/mabel-lee-sunn/)**, behind her (family identification), and her father, [Koon Hung Sunn](/family/koon-hung-sunn/), beside them (both identified by the family). It is a rare frame of Stella with her own Sunn-side parents, distinct from the Eesley-household group photos taken the same day.

It belongs to the same April 1945 wedding cluster as the other Chong-wedding photographs, developed the week of the KISCO stamp (10 April 1945) that dates the group photo.
