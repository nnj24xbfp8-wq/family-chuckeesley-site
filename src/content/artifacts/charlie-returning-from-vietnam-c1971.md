[Charlie Eesley](/family/charles-eesley/) home from his Vietnam tour, photographed at the Eesley family's house in Marietta sometime after his **14 June 1971 ETS** from the U.S. Army. The handwritten caption at the bottom of the print, written years later by Charlie's wife [Terrie](/family/terrie-lee-eesley/) in red felt-tip, reads:

> *RETURNING FROM VIETNAM &mdash; CHARLIE CIRCA 1972 EESLEY*

The year has been written over &mdash; the *2* on top of what appears to be an earlier *1*. Charlie's tour ended in June 1971. The photograph could date from that summer or fall (Terrie's first reading); or from a later moment in late 1971 or early 1972 if her overwriting was correcting upward. Either way the frame sits in the first six-to-eighteen months after his return.

## What the photograph shows

Charlie stands in front of a tall wood bookshelf packed with **leather-bound volumes** &mdash; the run reads as legal references or the family's reference set (the kind of bookshelf the Sixth Street house carried in its public rooms). He is wearing the **U.S. Army officer's full-length wool overcoat** (the standard Army Green overcoat of the late-Vietnam-era uniform regulation, double-breasted with epaulettes), with a dark tie visible beneath. He has his glasses on, his hair is short, and he is **smiling broadly, turned slightly to look off to his right** at someone outside the frame &mdash; possibly the photographer or the person whose hand is visible at the very left edge of the frame holding a drink, raising it toward him in welcome.

On the shelf to his right, a small **picture frame** is visible, containing what reads as a family portrait. Charlie holds something in his left hand &mdash; possibly a souvenir from the tour, possibly a letter opener from the shelf.

The whole composition is a **homecoming**: the officer's coat, the bookshelves of his parents' house, the drink being raised to him from off-camera, the easy smile. After [602 days in Vietnam](/docs/letters-from-vietnam/), the [malaria episode of September 1970](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1970-09-21-just-diagnosed/), the [Da Nang clerk's job after Camp Eagle](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1971-02-10-danang/), the [Hawaii R&R in April 1971](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1971-03-29-second-rr-booking/), and the long ride home &mdash; this is the frame on the other side of all of it.
