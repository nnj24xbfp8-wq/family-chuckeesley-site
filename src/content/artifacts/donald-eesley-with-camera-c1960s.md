[Donald Stuart "Don" Eesley](/family/don-eesley/) &mdash; [Will Eesley](/family/wilbur-eesley/)'s brother and [Chuck](/family/charles-eric-eesley/)'s great-uncle &mdash; caught in the act of taking a photograph, raising a 35mm SLR to his eye with both hands. Grey-haired and in a short-sleeved shirt with a tie, he reads as a man in his fifties or sixties, which places the frame somewhere in the **1960s** (Don was born in 1908 and died in 1975). Other figures stand blurred in the background, and a chair is just visible at the left &mdash; an outdoor family gathering of the kind the Eesleys kept photographing across the generations.

It is a fitting likeness of him: a candid of the photographer photographing, in a family whose visual record &mdash; from [Maggie's four-generation photo deck](/family/margaret-maggie-eesley/) forward &mdash; is the reason this archive has pictures at all. His earlier likenesses here are the [April 1932 studio portrait](/family/don-eesley/) at about twenty-three, the [three brothers c. 1912](/archive/eesley-three-brothers-c1912/), and the [late-1940s Bexley family group portrait](/archive/eesley-family-group-portrait-late-1940s/); this catches him a full generation on, mid-life, doing the thing that made the record.
