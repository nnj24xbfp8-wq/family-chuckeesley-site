A six-person family group from [Lijie](/family/lijie-zhou/)'s maternal side at the Qingdao seafront, c. early 1970s — a pavilion resembling the Zhanqiao pier's Huilan Pavilion is visible at the left. All six were identified by [Li Xun](/family/xun-li/) (Lijie's mother) in 2026.

**Back row (standing), left to right:**

- **[Shang Yaoxiang](/family/yaoxiang-shang/)** (b. 1911) — Yaozhen's elder sister, in a dark traditional jacket.
- **[Shang Lingke](/family/lingke-shang/)** — Yaofu Shang's eldest daughter, in glasses.
- **[Yunhua Li](/family/yunhua-li/)** — Zhongchu Li's sister and Yaofu Shang's wife, at right.

**Front row (crouching), left to right:**

- **[Li Xun](/family/xun-li/)** (b. 1956, Lijie's mother) — at left.
- **[Bo Li](/family/bo-li/)** — Li Xun's younger sister, crouching and holding the little girl.
- **[Wang Xiaoxiang](/family/wang-xiaoxiang/)** — the little girl in Bo Li's arms; **Shang Lingke's daughter** (and so Lijie's second cousin).

## 中文

**尚&mdash;李两家于青岛海滨合影，约1970年代初**

出自[Lijie](/family/lijie-zhou/)母系的一张六人合影，摄于青岛海滨（画面左侧可见形似栈桥回澜阁的亭子），约1970年代初 &mdash; 六人全部由[李恂](/family/xun-li/)（Lijie的母亲）于2026年指认。

**后排（站立），自左至右：**

- **[尚耀香](/family/yaoxiang-shang/)**（1911年生）&mdash; 尚耀真的姐姐，着深色传统上衣。
- **[尚玲珂](/family/lingke-shang/)** &mdash; 尚耀福的长女，戴眼镜。
- **[李蕴华](/family/yunhua-li/)** &mdash; 李仲初的姐妹、尚耀福之妻，居右。

**前排（蹲坐），自左至右：**

- **[李恂](/family/xun-li/)**（1956年生，Lijie的母亲）&mdash; 居左。
- **[李勃](/family/bo-li/)** &mdash; 李恂的妹妹，蹲着抱小女孩。
- **[王肖向](/family/wang-xiaoxiang/)** &mdash; 李勃怀中的小女孩；[尚玲珂](/family/lingke-shang/)之女（即Lijie的同辈表亲）。
