Charles "Charlie" Eesley and Terrie Lee in their wedding portrait, **15 August 1971**, at the First Congregational Church in Marietta, Ohio &mdash; Charlie in a black tuxedo with a yellow boutonnière, Terrie in a high-necked lace gown and veil, holding a bouquet of yellow and orange flowers.

This photograph is the resolution of the whole [Vietnam letters](/docs/letters-from-vietnam/) correspondence. Across the spring of 1971 the letters circle one question over and over &mdash; whether Terrie would come to Hawaii, whether her parents would allow it, whether they would marry around the R&R, what it would cost. Charlie came home from Vietnam in June 1971; two months later they were married at the church in Marietta. The countdown that paces the late letters &mdash; *136 days, 101, 88, 81, 43* &mdash; ends here.
