**[Helen "Big Helen" (Alspach) Eesley](/family/helen-bernadine-alspach-eesley/)** with her son **[Thomas Leonard "Tommy" Eesley](/family/tommy-eesley/)** on a columned porch, dated **4 September 1938** on the print. Big Helen is about thirty-six here; Tommy, born December 1930, is about seven or eight. It is a rare mother-and-son frame on [Leonard](/family/leonard-david-eesley/)'s branch, from the same years as the [c. 1932 photograph of Leonard holding a toddler Tommy](/archive/leonard-and-son-tommy-c1932/).

> *Source: Eesley family archive, from Roberta Burnes Walker's keeping; dated 4 September 1938 on the print.*
