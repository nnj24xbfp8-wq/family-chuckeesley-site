[Lijie](/family/lijie-zhou/)'s **paternal grandparents** together in later life at home in [Qingdao](/places/qingdao/): **[Sun Yunzhe](/family/yunzhe-sun/)** (at left, in a green embroidered top; d. 2023) and her husband **[Zhou Zhaoxiang](/family/zhaoxiang-zhou/)** (at right, in a checked shirt; 1930&ndash;2022, born in Jiaozhou). It is the clearest photograph of Sun Yunzhe in the archive and the source of her portrait here.

The pairing completes the grandparent set for Lijie's line: her maternal grandmother [Shang Yaozhen](/family/yaozhen-shang/) and maternal grandfather [Li Zhongchu](/family/zhongchu-li/) on one side, and Zhou Zhaoxiang and Sun Yunzhe on the other.

> *Source: Zhou family photographs, held by Lijie and Chuck; identified by Chuck, 2026.*

## 中文

**周兆祥与孙云哲 &mdash; Lijie的祖父母，青岛**

[Lijie](/family/lijie-zhou/)的**祖父母**晚年在[青岛](/places/qingdao/)家中的合影：**[孙云哲](/family/yunzhe-sun/)**（左，身着绿色绣花上衣；2023年辞世）与其夫**[周兆祥](/family/zhaoxiang-zhou/)**（右，身着格子衬衫；1930&ndash;2022，生于胶州）。这是本档案中孙云哲最清晰的一张照片，也是其肖像的来源。

这一对合影补齐了Lijie这一脉的祖辈：一侧是外祖母[尚耀真](/family/yaozhen-shang/)与外祖父[李仲初](/family/zhongchu-li/)，另一侧即周兆祥与孙云哲。
