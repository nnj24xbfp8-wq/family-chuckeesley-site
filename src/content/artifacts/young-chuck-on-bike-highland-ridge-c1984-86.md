[Chuck](/family/charles-eric-eesley/) on a wet **blacktop loop driveway** under bare deciduous trees, in a **gray quilted jacket over a red striped shirt** and red sweatpants, balancing on the same **red BMX-style bike with training wheels** that appears in [the indoor Hillcrest frame](/archive/young-chuck-on-bike-hillcrest-c1984-86/). The lighting reads as early spring or late autumn &mdash; the trees leafless, the asphalt still damp, the grass at the edge yellow-green coming back. A pale 1980s sedan sits at the edge of the loop at far left. He is grinning at the camera.

## Where the loop is

The blacktop loop driveway and the wooded edge place the photograph at [Highland Ridge Road, Lowell, Ohio](/places/highland-ridge-road-marietta/) &mdash; the next house the family settled into after the [Hillcrest years](/family/charles-eesley/), and the one [Will the architect](/family/wilbur-eesley/) designed and built. The loop is the same driveway visible in the [Highland Ridge house exteriors elsewhere in the archive](/places/highland-ridge-road-marietta/) and in the [red-bridge winter frame](/archive/highland-ridge-red-bridge-winter/).

## The two bikes-with-training-wheels frames

This photograph pairs with [the indoor Hillcrest BMX shot](/archive/young-chuck-on-bike-hillcrest-c1984-86/) as the **two earliest training-wheels frames** of Chuck on a bike in the archive &mdash; the prehistory of [the post-2015 charity bike rides](/docs/charity-bike-rides/). The training wheels are the only ones in the corpus.
