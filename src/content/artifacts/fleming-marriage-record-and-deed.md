Two primary records that [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/) pulled from county courthouses and filed together under **FLEM-12**. Between them they document the two events that set up **[Thomas Bailey Fleming](/family/thomas-bailey-fleming/)**'s adult life: the marriage, and the farm.

## 1. The marriage, 28 August 1851

![A typed abstract sheet headed MARRIAGE RECORD, Document #92, FLEM-12, citing 1851 Thomas B. Fleming, Book 1, Page 66, Wood County Record of Marriages, Wood County Court House, Parkersburg, West Virginia; date married 1851 August 28, minister Francis Guthrie, names of persons married Thomas B. Fleming and Martha Turner.](../../assets/family/originals/thomas-b-fleming-martha-turner-marriage-record-1851.jpeg)

> **MARRIAGE RECORD** — Document #92, FLEM-12
> 1851 THOMAS B. FLEMING, Book 1; Page 66. Wood County Record of Marriages, Wood County Court House, Parkersburg, West Virginia
>
> **Date Married:** 1851 – August 28 · **Minister:** Francis Guthrie · **Names of persons married:** Thomas B. Fleming and Martha Turner

Small but load-bearing. The groom is entered as **Thomas B. Fleming** — the **B is Bailey**, his mother [Synthia Bailey](/family/synthia-bailey/)'s surname carried as his middle name, and the reason the Bailey line is traceable at all through the Flemings. The record gives the date, the county, and the officiating minister, **Francis Guthrie**.

## 2. The deed, 15 September 1854

![A photocopied Doddridge County deed book page, number 482, in cursive: a deed of 15 September 1854 from Lewis Fleming and Mary his wife to Thomas B. Fleming for one hundred dollars, conveying fifty acres on Burnt Cabin, a drain of Arnold's Creek, followed by the clerk's recording note and a second instrument of 1855.](../../assets/family/originals/lewis-fleming-to-thomas-b-fleming-deed-1854.jpeg)

Deed book page **482**, marginal heading *"Lewis Fleming / Deed for 50 acres of ground / Thomas B. Fleming."* The opening reads:

> *"This Deed made the 15th day of September in the year 1854 between **Lewis Fleming and Mary his wife** of the first part and **Thomas B. Fleming** of the other part witnesseth that in consideration of **One hundred Dollars** to them in hand paid the receipt whereof is hereby acknowledged the said Lewis Fleming and Mary his wife do grant unto the said Thomas B. Fleming all of a certain tract or parcel of land lying on **Burnt Cabin**, a drain of **Arnold's Creek**, in the County of Doddridge … containing **Fifty acres**…"*

A father selling his son fifty acres of his own farm for a hundred dollars. The boundary call begins at a chestnut oak *"corner to Lewis Fleming's farm"* — the son's land cut directly off the father's.

**"Mary his wife"** is the confirmation. [Lewis](/family/lewis-fleming/) married [Synthia Bailey](/family/synthia-bailey/) in 1827; she died about 1849, and he then married the widow **Mary Lake**. A dower release in a 1854 deed names the wife of the moment — so this instrument independently corroborates both Synthia's death before 1854 and the second marriage the family record had already reported.

The deed was acknowledged before a justice of Doddridge County and **admitted to record 8 May 1855**.

### The second instrument on the same page

Directly beneath, dated **8 May 1855** — the same day the first deed was recorded — Thomas B. Fleming conveys **the same fifty acres** to a trustee, in trust to secure a debt of **seventy-five dollars**. It is a deed of trust: a mortgage in the Virginia form.

Eight months after buying the farm from his father for a hundred dollars, he borrowed seventy-five against it. He was twenty-five, three years married, with the first children coming. *(The cursive on this portion is faint in the photocopy; the trustee's and creditor's names are legible but not certain, and are not asserted here.)*

## What the two records fix

- **Marriage:** 28 August 1851, Wood County, minister Francis Guthrie — confirming the date in the [biographical sketch](/archive/thomas-bailey-fleming-biographical-sketch/) from the county register itself.
- **The farm:** fifty acres on Burnt Cabin, a drain of Arnold's Creek, Doddridge County, bought from his father 15 September 1854 for $100 and recorded 8 May 1855. Per the sketch, roughly **ten miles west of West Union**.
- **Lewis's second wife:** named in a legal instrument, in 1854.
