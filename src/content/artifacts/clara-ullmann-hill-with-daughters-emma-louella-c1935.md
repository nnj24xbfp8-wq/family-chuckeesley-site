Three women outside a white clapboard house, c. 1935 &mdash; and, importantly, **the first confirmed photograph of [Clara Victoria Ullmann Hill](/family/clara-victoria-ullmann/) (1885&ndash;1941) in this archive.** Clara, Chuck's maternal great-great-grandmother, stands at the center in a printed dress and glasses, flanked by two of her four children.

## Left to right

- **[Emma Vearl (Hill) Dailey](/family/emma-vearl-hill-dailey/)** (1907&ndash;1989) &mdash; Clara's second daughter (labeled by the family).
- **[Clara Victoria Ullmann Hill](/family/clara-victoria-ullmann/)** (1885&ndash;1941) &mdash; the mother; wife of [Harvey Hendershot Hill](/family/harvey-hendershot-hill/), mother of Chuck's great-grandmother [Bessie](/family/bessie-hill-davis/).
- **[Louella "Lovella" (Hill) Beardmore](/family/louella-hill-beardmore/)** (1917&ndash;2011) &mdash; Clara's youngest daughter, in a formal light gown with a corsage.

## Dating — probably Louella's graduation

The date rests on the daughters' ages and one nice detail: **Louella graduated Somerset High School in 1935**, and her formal dress and corsage here read like a graduation occasion. That fits the rest &mdash; Emma at about twenty-eight, Louella at seventeen or eighteen, and Clara around fifty. It comfortably precedes Clara's death in **October 1941**, and gives the archive a face for a great-great-grandmother who until now existed only in the record and in the [Mt. Perry group](/archive/hill-davis-family-mt-perry-porch-c1940s/) alongside her husband and mother-in-law.

> *Source: Eesley family archive, maternal Hill line; all three figures identified by Chuck Eesley, 2026 (Emma family-labeled; Clara and Louella confirmed). Louella's 1935 Somerset High graduation per her [obituary record](/family/louella-hill-beardmore/).*
