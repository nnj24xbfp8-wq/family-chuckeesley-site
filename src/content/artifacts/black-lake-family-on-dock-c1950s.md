A summer-day photograph at the [Black Lake cottage](/places/black-lake-michigan/), c. 1950&ndash;1952 &mdash; the [Will Eesley](/family/wilbur-eesley/) household at the lake with three of their young children. The dock extends out into the still water; a floating swim-platform sits in the middle distance and the lake fades silver into the far shore under a sky of soft cumulus.

## Cast

Per Chuck Eesley, June 2026 (tentative on the two girls):

- **[Will Eesley](/family/wilbur-eesley/)** &mdash; Chuck's paternal grandfather; the architect of the [Highland Ridge house](/places/highland-ridge-road-marietta/), then in his late thirties. Seated on the dock leaning back, sun on his face.
- **[Margaret "Peggy" McMaster Eesley](/family/margaret-mcmaster-eesley/)** &mdash; Will's wife, Chuck's paternal grandmother. Dark curly hair, dark short-sleeved top, sitting close beside Will, head turned toward the camera.
- **[Charlie](/family/charles-eesley/)** &mdash; Chuck's father, b. 24 May 1947, about three to five years old here. Small boy in a patterned shirt sitting on the edge of the dock with his bare feet hanging over the water.
- **[Jeanne Eesley](/family/jeanne-eesley-kamiab/)** (probably) &mdash; Will and Peggy's eldest, then about seven. Standing on the dock in a polka-dot or print two-piece bathing suit, one hand raised.
- **[Anne Eesley](/family/anne-eesley/)** (probably) &mdash; the next sister, then a toddler. Sitting in the shallow water at the edge of the beach in a small striped swimsuit.

The figure further out on the dock walking toward the swim platform is most plausibly Jeanne or Anne in a second moment of the same afternoon (or possibly a fourth child &mdash; uncertain).

The image is the earliest photograph of Will and Peggy's young family in this archive's Black Lake set &mdash; the next-generation summer at the cottage Will's father Charles Leonard had built two decades earlier, captured a few years before [the cottage burned down](/places/black-lake-michigan/) and the Eesley summers at the lake ended.
