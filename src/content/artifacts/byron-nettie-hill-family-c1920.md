A formal studio portrait of **[Ida Jeanetta "Nettie" (Ullman) Hill](/family/ida-jeanetta-ullman-hill/)** and her husband **[Byron E. Hill](/family/byron-hill/)** with their children, **c. 1919–1922**. Nettie stands at left in a dark dress with a white collar; Byron at right, a slim young man in wire-rimmed glasses and a dark suit. In front are **three of their children** — an older boy in a bow tie at center, a younger boy in a sailor collar at right, and a baby in a wicker bassinet.

Nettie married Byron on 20 March 1912, so the eldest child here (about seven or eight) and the babe-in-arms place the frame at roughly **1919–1922** — consistent with Nettie, born June 1892, appearing to be in her late twenties. Byron would die in 1932, when these children were still young.

The **children's names are not yet recorded** in this archive. The 1920 U.S. Census for Byron E. Hill's Ohio household would list each of them by name and age — the natural next step to identify the three young faces in this frame.

> *Source: Ullman/Hill family papers, transmitted by Chuck Eesley 2026; identifications of Nettie and Byron from Chuck's face-tagging. Children unnamed pending census research.*
