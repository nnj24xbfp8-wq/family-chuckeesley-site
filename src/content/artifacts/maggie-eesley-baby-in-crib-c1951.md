[Maggie Eesley](/family/margaret-maggie-eesley/) as an infant, propped up in a wooden crib with a hanging toy at the left, looking straight at the camera. The youngest of [Will](/family/wilbur-eesley/) and [Peggy](/family/margaret-mcmaster-eesley/) Eesley's four children, born c. 1951 &mdash; which dates the frame to her first year.

It is a small closing of a loop: the archivist behind this site's whole paternal-side image layer &mdash; the [four-generation photo deck](/family/margaret-maggie-eesley/) that so many of these portraits come through &mdash; here at the very start, on the other side of the camera.
