[Stephanie Kamiab](/family/stephanie-kamiab/) &mdash; daughter of [Jeanne Eesley Kamiab](/family/jeanne-eesley-kamiab/) and Chuck's first cousin &mdash; feeding **baby Chuck** a bottle on the couch at the [Highland Ridge Road house](/places/highland-ridge-road-marietta/) near Lowell, Ohio, c. 1980.

Behind them hangs the large **Chinese mountain-and-river landscape mural** that decorated the Highland Ridge living room &mdash; almost certainly a souvenir of [Will Eesley](/family/wilbur-eesley/)'s Japan-and-Hong-Kong travels, and part of the East-Asian design vocabulary he brought home to the house he designed and built. The mural is one of the recurring backdrops of Chuck's early childhood at Highland Ridge; here it frames a cousin looking after the family's newest member.
