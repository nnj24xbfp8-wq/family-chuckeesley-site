**An outdoor family snapshot with [Catharina Boeshar](/family/catharina-boeshar-wildermuth/) as the seated matriarch**, taken against the brick-and-stone wall of a house — presumably in Marietta. Per Chuck Eesley's July 2026 face-tagging, the four people are:

- **Seated, center** — **[Catharina Boeshar](/family/catharina-boeshar-wildermuth/)** (b. 1840, d. 1933), the matriarch, elderly here in a dark high-necked dress with a long striped skirt over her lap. This is the **clearest single image of her face** in the archive.
- **Standing, left** — her youngest son **[Edward Frederick Wildermuth](/family/edward-frederick-wildermuth/)** (b. 1873, d. 1964), in a dark suit and tie.
- **Standing, right** — his wife **[Arminda Jane Hayes](/family/arminda-jane-hayes-wildermuth/)** (b. 1886, d. 1967), in a light dress. The family knew her as Jane.
- **Front, left, on a tricycle** — their young son **[William Wildermuth](/family/william-edward-wildermuth/)**, a boy of about four or five.

## Dating

The photograph postdates **Edward Frederick and Arminda's marriage on 15 April 1918**, and the boy on the tricycle looks about four or five — which places the sitting in the **early 1920s**, with Catharina (b. 1840) in her early eighties. She would live to 1933; Edward would live to 1964.

## A note on the record

The family GEDCOM does **not** list any children for Edward Frederick and Arminda's marriage, so their son William is documented here by **this photograph and the family's own identification** rather than by the descendant tree — a reminder that the tree is incomplete for this branch. His dates and later life are open questions on [his page](/family/william-edward-wildermuth/).
