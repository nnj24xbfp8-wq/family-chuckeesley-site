**An outdoor family snapshot with [Catharina Boeshar](/family/catharina-boeshar-wildermuth/) as the seated matriarch**, taken against the brick-and-stone wall of a house — presumably in Marietta. This is a **second, closer likeness of Catharina** to go with the [c. 1896 family group portrait](/archive/william-wildermuth-family-group-portrait/): here she is elderly, seated front and center in a dark high-necked dress with a long striped skirt over her lap, hands folded — the clearest single image of her face in the archive.

Per Chuck Eesley's face-tagging, the other three are her son [**Edward Frederick Wildermuth**](/family/edward-frederick-wildermuth/) (standing, left), his wife [**Arminda Jane Hayes**](/family/arminda-jane-hayes-wildermuth/) (standing, right, in a light dress), and a young boy named **William** on a tricycle at front-left.

## Open questions — identities and date to confirm

Two things about this photograph are unresolved and should be confirmed before it is treated as settled:

- **The standing man looks younger than Edward Frederick (b. 1873) would be** in a photograph where Catharina (b. 1840) is this elderly. If Catharina is in her seventies or eighties here, Edward would be in his forties or fifties — older than the man appears. The younger couple could instead be **one of Catharina's grandsons and his wife**, with the boy their son and Catharina his great-grandmother.
- **The tricycle boy's parentage is unsupported by the tree.** The family GEDCOM records **no children** for Edward Frederick and Arminda's 1918 marriage, so "William, their son" rests on the photograph rather than the documentary record. If the couple are instead a grandchild's family, the boy belongs to that line.

If the couple *are* Edward Frederick and Arminda, the tricycle boy dates the sitting to the **early 1920s** (after their 1918 marriage), with Catharina in her early eighties. If they are a younger grandchild's family, the photograph could be a decade or more earlier. The date above is a placeholder pending that call.
