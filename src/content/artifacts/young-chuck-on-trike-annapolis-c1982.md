A summer-afternoon snapshot of [Chuck](/family/charles-eric-eesley/) as a blond toddler in a red anchor-print sleeveless romper and leather sandals, sitting on a **red Radio Flyer tricycle** in the driveway of the family house in **Annapolis, Maryland**. He is looking past the camera in the focused little-kid stare of a child on a moving thing. Behind him: a yellow 1970s American sedan in the driveway, a lush green front lawn, and the **two-story red-clapboard colonial with a brick first floor and a covered front porch** that was the family's Maryland home in the years before the 1983 return to Marietta. On the front-porch step a tall figure in shorts is at work on something &mdash; almost certainly [Charlie](/family/charles-eesley/) on a weekend project, but the figure is partly turned away.

## What it documents

This is the **earliest documentary frame of Chuck on the Annapolis house** in the archive &mdash; the structural anchor for [Charlie's "Annapolis years" section](/family/charles-eesley/) (1971&ndash;1983, Sears employment, the family's first decade of married life). It places Chuck in late toddlerhood &mdash; c. 1981 to 1983 &mdash; in the front yard of the house the family would leave when they moved back to Marietta to [137 Hillcrest Drive](/family/charles-eesley/) and the [108 Hillcrest neighborhood of the Wolfes](/places/108-hillcrest-drive-marietta/).

The Annapolis years and the c. 1972 [Charlie studio portrait](/archive/charlie-eesley-formal-studio-portrait/) (whose location was open between Marietta and the Maryland area) sit on the same family-geography thread &mdash; the Eesley east-coast decade between [Vietnam](/places/saigon/) and [Marietta](/places/108-hillcrest-drive-marietta/).
