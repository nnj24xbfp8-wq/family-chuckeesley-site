Charlie Eesley on the balcony of a Waikiki high-rise hotel in Honolulu during the **Hawaii R&R of April 1971** &mdash; the meeting he and Terrie had spent the entire spring of [Vietnam letters](/docs/letters-from-vietnam/) planning. He stands at the railing in a short-sleeved shirt, the Waikiki skyline and the green of the Ala Moana behind him, flown in from Vietnam for the one week the Army's R&R calendar allowed.

This is the trip the letters build toward: the [29 March 1971 booking letter](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1971-03-29-second-rr-booking/) (*"Please get your ticket under (Mrs. Eesley) to save money… arrival the 12th of April and leave on the 19th"*), the [24 February cost letter](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1971-02-24-five-days-in-and-rr-cost/), and the [15 February letter](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1971-02-15-danang-awaiting-orders/) working out the budget (*"we could always eat hot dogs and stay in bed for the week to save money"*). Terrie stands on the [same balcony](/archive/terrie-waikiki-hawaii-1971/) in her own photograph; a few days later she is [home with the pineapple souvenir bag](/archive/terrie-home-from-hawaii-1971/). Charlie would rotate home from Vietnam that June and [marry Terrie in August](/archive/eesley-wedding-1971-portrait/).
