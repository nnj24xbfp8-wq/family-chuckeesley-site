The formal **wedding portrait** of [Stella Sunn Eesley](/family/stella/) and [Ted Chong](/family/ted-chong/) &mdash; **c. April 1945**, contemporaneous with the [10 April 1945 KISCO-stamped cherry-blossom photograph](/archive/ted-chong-navy-with-stella-cherry-blossoms-april-1945/) at the Washington D.C. Tidal Basin on Stella's page. This is the canonical formal frame from the wedding day.

## What the portrait shows

A full-length studio portrait against a backdrop of palm fronds and gladiolus flower arrangements. **Stella** stands at left in a long white satin wedding gown with a fitted bodice, a sweetheart neckline trimmed with lace, long sleeves, and a wide cathedral-length veil falling from a flower-trimmed coronet. She carries a large cascading bouquet of lilies and other white flowers, the cascade falling nearly to her knee. **Ted** stands at right in his U.S. Navy white dress uniform &mdash; double-breasted with brass buttons, a service ribbon bar above the left breast pocket, white belt, and white shoes. The composition is the classic 1940s wartime wedding portrait of an American serviceman and his bride; the palm-frond backdrop and gladiolus arrangements suggest the studio of a hotel-ballroom photographer or a downtown wedding-photography studio.

## Why the date

The portrait is **undated** in family memory. The KISCO Photo Service stamp on the back of the [contemporary cherry-blossom photo](/archive/ted-chong-navy-with-stella-cherry-blossoms-april-1945/) reads **APR 10 1945**, and a labeled group portrait from the same wedding day batch ([7366, with handwritten cast on the back](/archive/stella-ted-chong-wedding-cassady-group-1945/)) carries the same KISCO APR 10 1945 stamp. The wedding sits **c. April 1945** &mdash; four months before the end of the Pacific war.

## What it documents

[Stella](/family/stella/) was a **Chinese-American** wartime nurse who met [Ted Chong](/family/ted-chong/), a U.S. Navy officer, during the war years. Their wedding c. April 1945 is the **structural moment of the Chong-side branch** of this archive &mdash; the marriage that produced their two daughters [Sue Lynn](/family/sue-lynn-chong/) and [Glenn](/family/glenn-chong/), and the line that runs into [Stella's later New Jersey life](/family/stella/) after Ted's death.

The portrait sits alongside the [10 April 1945 cherry-blossom photograph](/archive/ted-chong-navy-with-stella-cherry-blossoms-april-1945/), the [Cassady Avenue wedding day group with Will and Helen Eesley](/archive/stella-ted-chong-wedding-cassady-group-1945/), and the [larger nine-person wedding day porch group at 230 N. Cassady](/archive/stella-ted-chong-wedding-day-cassady-porch-1945/) &mdash; together the **four-frame Stella-and-Ted wedding set** the archive now holds from the April 1945 batch.

> *Source: Eesley family archive; surfaced June 2026 by [Roberta Burnes Walker](/family/roberta-burnes/) as part of the photograph batch that included Stella's wedding day at 230 N. Cassady Avenue, Bexley.*
