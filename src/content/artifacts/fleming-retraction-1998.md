**Eight months after printing the [1998 pedigree charts](/archive/pedigree-charts-1998/) and writing the [thirteen-page Family Legacy](/docs/john-fleming-family-legacy/), he threw out the top of it.**

![A typed letter dated 27 November 1998 opening with the words STOP THE PRESSES, CIRCLE THE WAGONS, PULL UP A CHAIR and POUR A TALL COOL ONE, announcing a gross error in the Fleming family lineage and concluding that John Fleming Jr. and John Fleming III should be removed and James Fleming inserted in their place.](../../assets/family/originals/fleming-retraction-letter-1998-nov.jpeg)

The letter opens:

> ***"STOP THE PRESSES ! CIRCLE THE WAGONS !! PULL UP A CHAIR !!! and POUR A TALL COOL ONE !!!! Gross error in our Fleming family lineage but recoverable. I just learned that our oldest of Fleming ancestors were not 4 guys named John."***

## What he retracted

He explains how he got there. Twenty-odd years earlier he had found a **James Fleming** he believed was a direct ancestor and gathered a great deal on him. Then, around 1992, **two other Fleming researchers convinced him it was four John Flemings instead**, so he spent years documenting Johns of Maryland, Virginia and West Virginia back to 1708 — the line that appears on the March 1998 charts.

And then this, which is the sentence a careful researcher writes:

> *"There was one thing that bothered me a little that I could never quite prove but I accepted what I had found."*

What changed his mind was a book: ***"A Fleming Family with Colonial Ancestors in Virginia, Maryland and Pennsylvania,"*** by **William Allen and William Alpheous Fleming**, a father-and-son pair of genealogists, printed in **1941**. He describes it as thoroughly sourced from wills, marriage banns and courthouse records, and says plainly: *"I accept this book as true and correct."* Its findings, he writes, demonstrate **"conclusively that our John IV (my designation) was not the son of John III of Pennsylvania."**

He also turns on his own two sources. One, **Mike Roberts of Akron, Ohio**, other researchers told him was *"grossly flawed."* The other was **Josephine Fleming** — the cousin whose three taped hours gave this archive its [log-cabin recollections](/family/verona-sheppard-fleming/) — and about her he is unsentimental:

> *"Josephine Fleming had nothing in writing and had done no research but only recalled hearing her parents talk about it. She was 80 years old so a lot of her remembrances are more than a little clouded."*

The instruction at the end is explicit:

> *"John Fleming Jr. and John Fleming III should be removed from my previous family history and **James Fleming should be inserted in their place**."*

## What this archive is changing

**Everything above [John Fleming IV](/family/john-fleming-iv/) is withdrawn.** That means the whole Maryland-and-Ireland pedigree published here from the March 1998 charts — **John Fleming III**, **John Fleming Jr.** the Cabin John tobacco planter, **John Fleming Sr.** the weaver of **Ballyclare, County Antrim**, **Jean**, and by extension the **Whites** and **Hopkinses** who married into that branch — rests on a line its own author repudiated before the year was out.

**What survives:** John Fleming IV himself, as **Lewis Fleming's father**. Nothing in this letter touches that, and it is independently supported by [his 1853 death record](/archive/john-fleming-death-record-1853/), whose informant was his son **Eli B. Fleming**, and by the [1827 consent note](/docs/john-fleming-family-legacy/) signed *John Fleming*.

> **And this was not his last word either.** Five months later, in [April 1999](/archive/fleming-email-shelf-1999/), he abandoned the search — *"I've decided to turn my back on our John Fleming b. 1781… **Still leaning towards Samuel or Patrick**"* — and turned to the Baileys and Bartletts instead. **The James Fleming of this letter was a way-station, not a destination.**

**What replaces it is not yet known here.** He says James Fleming was the progenitor and that he enclosed *"a 10 generation Pedigree Chart and a rundown on James Fleming."* **Neither enclosure has been photographed.** From [his September 1998 letter](/archive/fleming-dear-cousins-1998/) we know he then thought **James Fleming Jr.** was the son of a James born 1716 — but that was written while he still held the four-Johns view, so it cannot simply be carried across.

> **This is the correct outcome, not a setback.** He tested a lineage he had published, found a better-sourced authority, and reversed himself in writing at seventy-four — having already circulated the wrong version to his cousins. The archive's job is to follow him, including when he corrects himself.

*A note on this archive's own error:* pages here presented the Ballyclare and Maryland generations on 15 August 2026 as his settled finding. They were his finding in **March** 1998. This letter is from **November** 1998, and it was in the same folder. The relevant pages now carry the retraction.
