A group of ten on the small covered porch of a white clapboard cottage, photographed from beyond a wooden picket fence and gate, **c. 1938–1941** &mdash; the Hill family home at **Mt. Perry**, in Perry County, Ohio, northeast of Somerset and about twenty-five miles beyond Zanesville. This is the maternal **Hill–Davis** side of the family &mdash; a different world from the columned, stucco [Bexley house on N. Cassady Avenue](/archive/stella-ted-chong-wedding-day-cassady-porch-1945/) it was once mistaken for: a modest vernacular Victorian cottage with jigsaw-cut porch trim and turned posts, the kind of house that anchored a rural Ohio family.

## The full cast, left to right

- **[Homer Edward Davis](/family/homer-davis/)** (1900&ndash;1982) &mdash; in white shirt and tie at the far left. Chuck's maternal great-grandfather; father of Dorothy, who would marry [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/) in 1946.
- **[Theodore "Ted" Roosevelt Hill](/family/theodore-roosevelt-hill/)** (b. 1912) &mdash; Bessie's younger brother, the one Hill sibling who later left Ohio for Texas.
- **Christine (Miller) Hill** &mdash; Ted's wife. *(No archive page yet.)*
- **[Louella "Lovella" (Hill) Beardmore](/family/louella-hill-beardmore/)** (b. 1917) &mdash; Bessie's youngest sister, married Charles Beardmore in 1938.
- **[Dorothy Marie Davis](/family/dorothy-davis-wildermuth/)** (b. 1925) &mdash; Homer and Bessie's daughter, Chuck's maternal grandmother, a young teenager here.
- **[Bessie Marie (Hill) Davis](/family/bessie-hill-davis/)** (1905&ndash;1950) &mdash; Homer's wife, Harvey and Clara's daughter, Chuck's maternal great-grandmother.
- **[Betty (Davis) Wolfe](/family/betty-davis/)** (b. 1926) &mdash; Dorothy's younger sister; "Aunt Betty" of the 108 Hillcrest Drive holiday dinners.
- **[Janetta "Jeanette" (Smith) Hill](/family/janetta-smith/)** (b. 1864) &mdash; Harvey's *mother*, the eldest person in the frame at about seventy-five; Chuck's maternal great-great-great-grandmother.
- **[Clara Victoria Ullmann Hill](/family/clara-victoria-ullmann/)** (1885&ndash;1941) &mdash; Harvey's wife, the matriarch; Chuck's maternal great-great-grandmother.
- **[Harvey Hendershot Hill](/family/harvey-hendershot-hill/)** (1882&ndash;1951) &mdash; the patriarch, the older man in the dark suit at the far right. The two Davis–Hill fathers-in-law bracket the frame: Homer at one end, Harvey at the other.

## Why Mt. Perry, and why c. 1938–1941

Harvey Hendershot Hill was born in Monroe County in 1882 and **died in nearby Bremen** (Fairfield County, just across the line from Mt. Perry) in February 1951 &mdash; placing the Hill household firmly in this Perry/Fairfield corner of Ohio. The date now brackets tightly: his wife **[Clara Ullmann Hill](/family/clara-victoria-ullmann/) died 15 October 1941**, and she is in the frame &mdash; a firm upper bound &mdash; while [Louella](/family/louella-hill-beardmore/) appears under her married Beardmore name (from 1938) and the Davis girls, [Dorothy](/family/dorothy-davis-wildermuth/) (b. 1925) and [Betty](/family/betty-davis/) (b. 1926), read as young teenagers. Together those put the gathering at roughly **1938–1941**, on the porch of the Hill home before Clara's death broke the older generation of the family.

The frame spans **four generations** of the maternal Hill line at once: [Janetta (Smith) Hill](/family/janetta-smith/) (b. 1864); her son [Harvey](/family/harvey-hendershot-hill/) and his wife [Clara](/family/clara-victoria-ullmann/); their daughter [Bessie](/family/bessie-hill-davis/) with her husband [Homer Davis](/family/homer-davis/); and Bessie's daughters [Dorothy](/family/dorothy-davis-wildermuth/) and [Betty](/family/betty-davis/) &mdash; the great-great-great-grandmother through to the grandmother of Chuck's own generation, on one small Mt. Perry porch.
