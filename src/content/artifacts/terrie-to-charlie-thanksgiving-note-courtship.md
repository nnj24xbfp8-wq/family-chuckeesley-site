A short handwritten note from [Terrie](/family/terrie-lee-eesley/) to [Charlie](/family/charles-eesley/) from their courtship, kept among his photographs and scanned on the same sheet as their [wedding portrait](/archive/charlie-and-terrie-wedding-portrait-c1972/):

> *Charlie,*
>
> *Thanks for a really terrific weekend! Be good, and I really want ya' to come for Thanksgiving. Try harder than your last visit.*
>
> *Until then,*
> *Yours,*
> *Terry*

It is one of the few surviving pieces in **Terrie's own hand to Charlie** — the reverse direction of the [Letters from Vietnam](/docs/letters-from-vietnam/), which are almost all Charlie writing home to her. The voice is the courtship at its lightest: a thank-you for a weekend, a nudge to come home for the holiday, the teasing *"try harder than your last visit,"* and the sign-off *"Yours, Terry."* This is the tone underneath the two years of wartime correspondence that followed — the relationship the letters were working to protect.
