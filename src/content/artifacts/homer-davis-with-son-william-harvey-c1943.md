**[Homer Edward Davis](/family/homer-davis/)** (1900&ndash;1982), in shirtsleeves and suspenders, standing in a yard and holding his young son **[William Harvey Davis Sr.](/family/william-harvey-davis-sr/)** (1941&ndash;2022). William, born August 1941, is a toddler of about a year and a half to two here, which places the photograph around **1943** &mdash; Homer at about forty-two.

William Harvey was the youngest of Homer and [Bessie](/family/bessie-hill-davis/)'s four children and the only son, after daughters [Mary Louise](/family/mary-louise-davis-murdock/), [Dorothy](/family/dorothy-davis-wildermuth/), and [Betty](/family/betty-davis/). This frame &mdash; a confirmed father-and-son image &mdash; also settles the identity of the child in the companion [c. 1944&ndash;45 photograph](/family/homer-davis/) of Homer with a small boy on his knee: the son is William Harvey. It is the first photograph of William Harvey Davis Sr. in the archive, and now his page portrait.
