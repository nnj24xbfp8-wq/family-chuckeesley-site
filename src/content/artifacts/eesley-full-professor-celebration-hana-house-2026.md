Chuck Eesley and [Lijie Zhou](/family/lijie-zhou/) at Stanford's Hana House for the celebration of Chuck's promotion to **full Professor**, 2026. The promotion to Professor in the Department of Management Science &amp; Engineering was effective in the **fall of 2025**; the celebration followed in 2026.

It is the companion frame to the [tenure celebration in the same garden five years earlier](/archive/eesley-tenure-celebration-hana-house-2021/) &mdash; the same oak, the same water-steps and stone lantern at Hana House, the two academic milestones bracketed by the one place and the one person beside him in both.
