A second frame from the family's **[Black Lake, Michigan](/archive/black-lake-group-with-leonard-1928/)** summers of the late 1920s &mdash; a group of about nine posed against the cottage wall &mdash; distinct from the [1928 group with Leonard](/archive/black-lake-group-with-leonard-1928/) already in the archive.

## Confirmed (from the labeled scan)

- Back row: **[Will Eesley](/family/wilbur-eesley/)** (left) and **[Donald "Don" Eesley](/family/don-eesley/)** (center).
- Front row, left: **[Dale Dudley Eesley](/family/dale-eesley/)** (1906&ndash;1939) &mdash; another frame of the brother who would drown at Black Lake in July 1939; one of the few photographs of him alive.

## Proposed identifications for the rest

These are **educated guesses**, grounded in the documented cast of the companion [1928 Black Lake frame](/archive/black-lake-group-with-leonard-1928/) &mdash; which independently records Lillie Dale, Thelma, and little Helen Burnes at the same gathering &mdash; not yet confirmed by a label:

- **Older woman, back right** (glasses, cloche hat): most likely **[Lillie Dale Chenoweth Eesley](/family/lillie-dale-chenoweth/)** (b. 1877), the matriarch and mother of the brothers &mdash; present in the companion frame. Her strong resemblance to [Aunt Ota](/family/scioto-mafry-chenoweth/) in the 1929 Sault Ste. Marie photo is exactly what one expects: the two were sisters.
- **Young woman, front center** (cloche hat): most likely **[Thelma (Haughn) Eesley](/family/thelma/)**, Dale's wife (married November 1927), seated near her husband &mdash; the companion frame places her between Dale and Lyle.
- **Little girl, front**: most likely **[Helen "Little Helen" (Eesley) Burnes](/family/helen-burnes/)** (b. January 1924), about four years old &mdash; also in the companion frame.

**Still genuinely open:** the **boy** of about seven or eight (no Eesley grandchild was that age in 1928 &mdash; likely a Chenoweth- or Smith-side cousin, or a family friend); the **young woman at front right** (possibly [Big Helen](/family/helen-bernadine-alspach-eesley/), Leonard's wife, if she attended); and a partly-hidden figure who could be **[Lyle](/family/lyle-eesley/)** or **[Leonard](/family/leonard-david-eesley/)**, both recorded at the companion frame but not visible enough to tag here.

> *Source: Eesley family archive, from Roberta Burnes Walker's keeping; Will, Don, and Dale confirmed from the labeled scan (Chuck Eesley, 2026); the remaining identifications proposed here by inference from the companion 1928 frame's cast, pending confirmation.*
