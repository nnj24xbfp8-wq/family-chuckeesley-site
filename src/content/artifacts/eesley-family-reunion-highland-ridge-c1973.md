A color group photograph of the extended [Charles Leonard Eesley](/family/charles-leonard-eesley/) family &mdash; five of his children with their spouses, grandchildren, and a great-grandchild &mdash; at the [Highland Ridge Road house](/places/highland-ridge-road-marietta/) near Lowell, Ohio, **c. 1973**. It is the **companion color frame** to the [black-and-white reunion group](/archive/highland-ridge-family-group-portrait-c-1980/) already in the archive, taken the same day. [Peggy McMaster Eesley](/family/margaret-mcmaster-eesley/) is not in the frame &mdash; she was behind the camera.

## The cast

Five of Charles Leonard Eesley's children and their families:

- His sons **[Will](/family/wilbur-eesley/)**, **[Don](/family/don-eesley/)**, and **[Leonard](/family/leonard-david-eesley/)** &mdash; Leonard is the elder man in the frame; his daughters **[Mary Eesley Bean](/family/mary-eesley-bean/)** and **[Helen (Eesley) Burnes](/family/helen-burnes/)**
- **[Peggy Youman Eesley](/family/margaret-youman-eesley/)** &mdash; Don's wife
- Grandchildren **[Charlie](/family/charles-eesley/)** and his wife **[Terrie](/family/terrie-lee-eesley/)**; **[Jeanne](/family/jeanne-eesley-kamiab/)**; **[Roberta](/family/roberta-burnes/)** and **[Katie Burnes](/family/katie-burnes/)**
- **[Mafry Hyatt](/family/mafry-smith-hyatt/)** &mdash; of the Chenoweth side
- Great-granddaughter **[Stephanie Kamiab](/family/stephanie-kamiab/)** &mdash; a young girl of about five or six here, not a carried toddler (she was already talking by late 1970, so born c. 1967&ndash;68), which fits the c. 1973 date

**Still to identify:** a young woman in the left-hand (Burnes) cluster, and a boy of about eight in a striped shirt &mdash; most likely a young grandson of Helen Burnes (a child of one of her married daughters), i.e., one of the great-grandchildren on the Burnes side.

<aside class="not-prose my-8 border-l-4 border-stone-400 bg-stone-50/70 dark:bg-stone-900/30 px-6 py-5 rounded-r">

**On the dating.** The patriarch [Charles Leonard Eesley](/family/charles-leonard-eesley/) is *not* in this photograph &mdash; the elder man is his son [Leonard](/family/leonard-david-eesley/). Charles Leonard died in October 1972, so his absence is consistent with a **c. 1973** gathering. The date is bracketed on the near side by [Terrie](/family/terrie-lee-eesley/)'s presence &mdash; she entered the family at the August 1971 wedding &mdash; and squared with [Stephanie Kamiab](/family/stephanie-kamiab/)'s age: born c. 1967&ndash;68 (already talking by late 1970), she reads as a girl of about five or six here rather than a babe in arms, consistent with c. 1973. The mistaken "c. 1980" filing on the black-and-white companion frame should be reconciled to this same c. 1973 gathering.

</aside>
