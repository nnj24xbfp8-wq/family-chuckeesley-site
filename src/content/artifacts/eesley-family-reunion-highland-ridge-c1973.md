A color group photograph from one of the annual Highland Ridge family reunions that [Wilbur "Will" Eesley](/family/wilbur-eesley/) began hosting after his father [Charles Leonard Eesley](/family/charles-leonard-eesley/)'s death in October 1972 &mdash; five of Charles Leonard's children with their spouses, grandchildren, and a great-grandchild, gathered at the [Highland Ridge Road house](/places/highland-ridge-road-marietta/) near Lowell, Ohio, **c. 1973**. It is a **separate, slightly later frame** from the [black-and-white founding reunion group](/archive/highland-ridge-family-group-portrait-c-1980/) (c. 1971&ndash;1972) already in the archive &mdash; a different day in the same tradition, with a different subset of the family present. [Peggy McMaster Eesley](/family/margaret-mcmaster-eesley/) is not in this frame &mdash; she was behind the camera.

## The cast

Five of Charles Leonard Eesley's children and their families:

- His sons **[Will](/family/wilbur-eesley/)**, **[Don](/family/don-eesley/)**, and **[Leonard](/family/leonard-david-eesley/)** &mdash; Leonard is the elder man in the frame; his daughters **[Mary Eesley Bean](/family/mary-eesley-bean/)** and **[Helen (Eesley) Burnes](/family/helen-burnes/)**
- **[Peggy Youman Eesley](/family/margaret-youman-eesley/)** &mdash; Don's wife
- Grandchildren **[Charlie](/family/charles-eesley/)** and his wife **[Terrie](/family/terrie-lee-eesley/)**; **[Jeanne](/family/jeanne-eesley-kamiab/)**; **[Roberta](/family/roberta-burnes/)** and **[Katie Burnes](/family/katie-burnes/)**
- **[Mafry Hyatt](/family/mafry-smith-hyatt/)** &mdash; of the Chenoweth side
- Great-granddaughter **[Stephanie Kamiab](/family/stephanie-kamiab/)** &mdash; a young girl of about five or six here, not a carried toddler (she was already talking by late 1970, so born c. 1967&ndash;68), which fits the c. 1973 date

**Still to identify:** a young woman in the left-hand (Burnes) cluster, and a boy of about eight in a striped shirt &mdash; most likely a young grandson of Helen Burnes (a child of one of her married daughters), i.e., one of the great-grandchildren on the Burnes side.

<aside class="not-prose my-8 border-l-4 border-stone-400 bg-stone-50/70 dark:bg-stone-900/30 px-6 py-5 rounded-r">

**On the dating.** The patriarch [Charles Leonard Eesley](/family/charles-leonard-eesley/) is *not* in this photograph &mdash; the elder man is his son [Leonard](/family/leonard-david-eesley/). Charles Leonard died in October 1972, and per [Roberta Burnes](/family/roberta-burnes/)'s recollection [Will](/family/wilbur-eesley/)'s annual Highland Ridge reunions began right after &mdash; so a **c. 1973** date fits. Two anchors place it: [Terrie](/family/terrie-lee-eesley/), who entered the family at the August 1971 wedding, is present; and [Stephanie Kamiab](/family/stephanie-kamiab/), born c. 1967&ndash;68 (already talking by late 1970), reads here as a girl of about five or six rather than a babe in arms. This is a later, separate frame from the [c. 1971&ndash;1972 black-and-white founding reunion](/archive/highland-ridge-family-group-portrait-c-1980/), whose earlier date rests on Roberta's own testimony that her sister Katie (b. 1965) "can't be much more than seven" in it.

</aside>
