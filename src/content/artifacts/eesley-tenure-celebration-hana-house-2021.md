Chuck Eesley and [Lijie Zhou](/family/lijie-zhou/) at Stanford's Hana House for the celebration of Chuck's tenure, 2021. He had been promoted to **Associate Professor with tenure** in Stanford's Department of Management Science &amp; Engineering in **2017**; the in-person celebration came later, delayed by the COVID-19 pandemic.

Hana House sits in a Japanese-style garden on the Stanford campus &mdash; the stone lantern, the water-steps, and the brick terraces behind them here &mdash; a setting that quietly rhymes with the East-Asian design thread that runs through the family, from [Will Eesley](/family/wilbur-eesley/)'s Japan-and-Hong-Kong-influenced Highland Ridge house to Lijie's roots in Qingdao. Its companion is the [full-professor celebration in the same garden five years later](/archive/eesley-full-professor-celebration-hana-house-2026/).
