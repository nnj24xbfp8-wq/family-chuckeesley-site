A close color group of five, in old age &mdash; **[Charles Leonard Eesley](/family/charles-leonard-eesley/)'s three surviving sons together with the two Helens**, c. early 1970s. Left to right:

- **[Will Eesley](/family/wilbur-eesley/)** (1910&ndash;1986)
- **[Helen "Little Helen" (Eesley) Burnes](/family/helen-burnes/)** (1924&ndash;2000) &mdash; Charles Leonard's youngest daughter
- **[Donald "Don" Eesley](/family/don-eesley/)** (1908&ndash;1975)
- **[Helen "Big Helen" (Alspach) Eesley](/family/helen-bernadine-alspach-eesley/)** (1902&ndash;1986) &mdash; Leonard's wife
- **[Leonard David Eesley](/family/leonard-david-eesley/)** (1904&ndash;1976) &mdash; the eldest brother

## Dating

The frame catches the family in the years just before it began to thin: **[Don](/family/don-eesley/) died in May 1975 and [Leonard](/family/leonard-david-eesley/) in January 1976**, so with both of them present and elderly here, the photograph is from the **early 1970s** &mdash; roughly the same era as the [c. 1971&ndash;1972 Highland Ridge reunion](/archive/highland-ridge-family-group-portrait-c-1980/), and a rare *color* record of the surviving sibling generation. It is also a nice mirror of the [1929 Sault Ste. Marie photograph](/archive/don-aunt-ota-helen-sault-ste-marie-1929/): Don and Big Helen appear in both, forty-odd years apart. (One further figure in the frame is not yet named.)
