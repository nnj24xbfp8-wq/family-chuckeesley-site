**Four-person Wildermuth studio portrait, c. early-to-mid 1920s.** Per Chuck's July 2026 face-tagging of the print, the four people are:

- **Standing, left** — **[Edward Frederick Wildermuth](/family/edward-frederick-wildermuth/)** (b. 17 February 1873, d. 7 July 1964), a son of the immigrant [Johann Michael Wildermuth](/family/johann-michael-wildermuth/) and [Catharina Boeshar](/family/catharina-boeshar-wildermuth/), and a younger brother of [William Clifford Wildermuth](/family/william-wildermuth/). In a dark suit and tie.
- **Standing, right** — **[Arminda Jane Hayes](/family/arminda-jane-hayes-wildermuth/)**, Edward Frederick's wife (they married 15 April 1918), in a white blouse. The family knew her as Jane.
- **Seated, right** — **[Catharina Boeshar](/family/catharina-boeshar-wildermuth/)**, the family matriarch — Johann Michael Wildermuth's wife and Edward Frederick's mother — in a long dark dress. This is the archive's clearest identified likeness of her (she also appears, holding a granddaughter, in the [c. 1896 family group portrait](/archive/william-wildermuth-family-group-portrait/)).
- **Front, left, on a tricycle** — a young boy, **[William Wildermuth](/family/william-edward-wildermuth/)**, most plausibly Edward Frederick and Jane's son (not to be confused with his uncle [William Clifford Wildermuth](/family/william-wildermuth/) or his cousin William, William Clifford's son). Note: the family GEDCOM records **no children** for Edward and Arminda's 1918 marriage, so his parentage rests on this photograph rather than the documentary tree — see his [page](/family/william-edward-wildermuth/) for the caveat.

## Dating

The photograph has to postdate **Edward Frederick and Arminda Jane Hayes's marriage on 15 April 1918**, and the boy on the tricycle looks about three to five — which places the sitting in the **early-to-mid 1920s**. That is consistent with Catharina Boeshar (b. 1840, d. 1933) as the seated matriarch: she would have been in her early-to-mid eighties, still living in Marietta. An earlier guess of "c. 1910s" is therefore too early and has been corrected.

## A correction (July 2026)

An earlier version of this entry identified the photograph as **William Clifford Wildermuth's household — William Clifford with his wife Flora Schlicher and their sons William and Earl Adam** — and treated it as the archive's first image of Chuck's great-grandfather Earl Adam as a young man. **Chuck's face-tagging corrects that: the photograph is Edward Frederick Wildermuth's family with the matriarch Catharina Boeshar.** Earl Adam Wildermuth and Flora Schlicher are **not** in this picture. The two households are close kin — Edward Frederick and William Clifford were brothers, both sons of Johann Michael and Catharina — which is the likely source of the earlier mistaken reading.
