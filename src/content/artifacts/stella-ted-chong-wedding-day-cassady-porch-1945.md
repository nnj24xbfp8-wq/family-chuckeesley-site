A nine-person group photograph on the **front porch and walkway of [230 N. Cassady Avenue, Bexley](/family/charles-leonard-eesley/)** &mdash; the [Charles Leonard and Lillie Dale Eesley](/family/charles-leonard-eesley/) family house &mdash; on **Stella and Ted Chong's wedding day, c. April 1945**. The largest single group frame from the wedding day batch the archive holds.

## What the photograph shows

The group stands in two informal rows in front of the white-columned front porch of the Bexley house. A small child in a dark hat is held up at left; the adults form a long line across the frame. **Stella** stands at right of center in a dark coat with large white buttons (the same coat she wears in the [labeled five-person group](/archive/stella-ted-chong-wedding-cassady-group-1945/)); **Ted Chong** is at far right in his **U.S. Navy double-breasted dark dress uniform**.

Other figures visible from left to right: a woman in a dark coat (older, glasses); a younger woman holding up the dark-hatted child; a man in suit and tie; a woman in a pale dress; a woman in a dark patterned dress; a young woman in a dark sweater dress (likely Helen Eesley, recognizable from the [labeled smaller group](/archive/stella-ted-chong-wedding-cassady-group-1945/)); a man in a dark fedora and three-piece suit (likely Will Eesley); Stella; Ted. A younger woman seated in front holds an infant.

Cast is partially identifiable from the labeled smaller-group photograph from the same day, but the full nine-person cast is not yet locked in.

## Why this is the wedding day

The Bexley house location, Stella's dark coat with the white buttons, and Ted's Navy dress uniform all match the [labeled five-person group photograph](/archive/stella-ted-chong-wedding-cassady-group-1945/) at the same address whose back carries the **KISCO Certified Photo Service stamp APR 10 1945** and the handwritten caption *"about 1942-5."* The two photographs are from the same wedding day.

It is plausibly the **fullest** documentary frame of Eesley family + Chong family + extended-family gathering this archive holds &mdash; the Bexley household that had carried [Aunt Ota's 1930 deathbed](/family/scioto-mafry-chenoweth/) and would soon carry [Lyle's August 1942 Cabanatuan death](/family/lyle-eesley/) was, in April 1945, the venue of Stella's wedding day.
