A formal group photograph of about sixty students in uniform caps and Mao-era tunics, banner overhead. The banner reads **青岛三十六中毛泽东思想文艺宣传队 71年** — the **Qingdao No. 36 Middle School Mao Zedong Thought Literature-and-Art Propaganda Troupe, 1971**.

The photograph comes from [Lijie](/family/lijie-zhou/)'s family, so **one of her relatives was a member of the troupe.** By age, the likeliest is a young **[Li Xun](/family/xun-li/)** (Lijie's mother, b. 1956, so about fifteen in 1971), though it could be another relative of that generation. The specific person is **not yet identified in the frame** — to be pointed out by Lijie's mother.
