A formal group photograph of about sixty students in uniform caps and Mao-era tunics, banner overhead. The banner reads **青岛三十六中毛泽东思想文艺宣传队 71年** — the **Qingdao No. 36 Middle School Mao Zedong Thought Literature-and-Art Propaganda Troupe, 1971**.

The photograph comes from [Lijie](/family/lijie-zhou/)'s family: **[Li Xun](/family/xun-li/)** (Lijie's mother, b. 1956, about fifteen in 1971) was a member of the troupe. She identified herself in the photograph in 2026 — **third row, sixth from the right.**

> *Source: Zhou–Li family photographs, from Lijie's family; Li Xun identified herself in the frame (third row, sixth from the right), 2026.*

## 中文

**青岛三十六中毛泽东思想文艺宣传队（1971年）**

一张约六十名学生的正式合影，学生们头戴统一帽子、身着毛泽东时代的服装，上方悬挂横幅。横幅上写着**"青岛三十六中毛泽东思想文艺宣传队 71年"**。

照片来自[Lijie](/family/lijie-zhou/)的家庭：[**李恂**](/family/xun-li/)（Lijie的母亲，1956年生，1971年约十五岁）是宣传队的一员。她于2026年在照片中指认了自己 &mdash; **第三排，右起第六位**。
