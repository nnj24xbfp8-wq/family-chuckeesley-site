The full wedding party on the steps of the First Congregational Church in Marietta, Ohio, **15 August 1971** &mdash; Charlie and Terrie at the center, flanked by groomsmen in dark suits, bridesmaids in white-bodiced, dark-skirted gowns with wide-brimmed hats and bouquets, and a young flower girl. Guests gather at the right; the church's brick façade and columned portico frame the group.

A companion to the [couple portrait](/archive/eesley-wedding-1971-portrait/) and the [ceremony photograph](/archive/eesley-wedding-1971-ceremony/) from the same day.
