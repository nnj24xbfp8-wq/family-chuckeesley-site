Terrie on the balcony of the Waikiki hotel during the **April 1971 Hawaii R&R**, smiling for the camera in a floral shirt-dress, the high-rises and lagoons of Waikiki spread out behind her under a hazy Pacific sky. She flew from Maryland &mdash; where she was finishing at the University of Maryland &mdash; to meet Charlie on his one week of R&R from Vietnam, on the reduced *"Mrs. Eesley"* fare the [letters had arranged](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1971-03-29-second-rr-booking/).

It is the same balcony where [Charlie stands](/archive/charlie-waikiki-hawaii-1971/) in his photograph, and &mdash; tellingly &mdash; the **same floral dress** she is wearing in the photograph of her [arriving back home from the trip](/archive/terrie-home-from-hawaii-1971/), the detail that ties the two ends of the journey together. The trip is the midpoint of the [Vietnam-letters](/docs/letters-from-vietnam/) story, four months before the [August 1971 wedding](/archive/eesley-wedding-1971-portrait/).
