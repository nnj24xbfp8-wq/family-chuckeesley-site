The printed memorial card from the December 1977 funeral of **[Earl Adam Wildermuth](/family/earl-a-wildermuth/)** &mdash; [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/)'s father and Chuck's great-grandfather on the maternal side. The card records:

- **Born:** October 6, 1899
- **Passed away:** December 6, 1977
- **Funeral services:** McClure-Schafer Funeral Home, December 9, 1977, 1&nbsp;p.m.
- **Officiating:** Jess Nutter
- **Interment:** Valley Cemetery

The facing panel prints Alfred, Lord Tennyson's poem *&ldquo;Crossing the Bar.&rdquo;*

The card **confirms the birth and death dates** carried on Earl Adam's page &mdash; 6 October 1899 and 6 December 1977. Two details are worth reconciling with the family: the card gives the **interment as Valley Cemetery** and the funeral home as McClure-Schafer, whereas his page currently records burial at **Reno, Washington County, Ohio**; and his page's summary mentions a **stroke on 8 December 1977**, which falls *after* the card's 6 December death date, so one of those two dates is off by a few days. Both are flagged here for confirmation against the funeral record rather than silently changed.
