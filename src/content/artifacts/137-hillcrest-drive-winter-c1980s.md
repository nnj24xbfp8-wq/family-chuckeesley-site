The Eesley family's house at **[137 Hillcrest Drive](/places/137-hillcrest-drive-marietta/)** in Marietta, photographed from the front yard in winter &mdash; patchy snow on the grass, bare trees, and a **Christmas wreath on the front door**. A single-story mid-century house with a low-pitched roof, a band of clerestory windows along the front wing, and a brick chimney at the right. A small figure &mdash; **likely young [Chuck](/family/charles-eric-eesley/)** &mdash; stands in the yard near the front walk.

This is the house Chuck grew up in after the family's [1983 move back to Marietta from Annapolis](/family/charles-eesley/), a few doors down the same street from his great-aunt and -uncle [Betty](/family/betty-davis/) and [Norm Wolfe](/family/norman-graham-wolfe/) at [108 Hillcrest](/places/108-hillcrest-drive-marietta/), before the family moved on to [Highland Ridge Road](/places/highland-ridge-road-marietta/). It is the first exterior frame of the Hillcrest childhood house in this archive.
