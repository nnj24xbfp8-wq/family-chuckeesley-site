Two more sheets from [the printed chart set](/archive/wildermuth-fleming-ancestor-chart-set/), in the same boxed format — **"Ancestors of Johann Georg Wolf"** and **"Ancestors of Anna Marie Werner."** They cover the Württemberg generation the [1988 Ahnentafel](/archive/ahnentafel-wildermuth-grossaspach-1988/) already reached, which makes them look redundant.

They are not. **Because they print dates in English words rather than German figures, they catch three errors this archive had published** — all of them the archive's own reading mistakes, not Robert Earl's.

## What they correct

**1. Balthus Wolf was born at Reichenberg, not Großaspach.** The archive had him born in the village he died in. The chart gives **Reichenberg** — a village that stayed independent until it was **absorbed into Oppenweiler in 1938**, and which lies next door to Aspach. The Ahnentafel's *"Balthus Wolf, Großaspach"* was recording residence, which is what that form does throughout. He married in.

**2. The Wolf marriage was 27 January 1733, not 27 July.** The Ahnentafel writes **∞ 27.1.1733** in the German day-first order. This archive read it as July. The chart spells it: **"January 27, 1732/33."** *(The double year is the old-style/new-style convention that attaches to January dates, not a disagreement about which year.)*

**3. Magdalena Übele was born in 1711, not 1717 — and was not a child bride.** The Ahnentafel's photograph shows **\* 10.8.171?** with the final digit unreadable, and this archive filled the gap with 1717. The chart prints **"August 10, 1711."**

That single digit had been doing a lot of damage. Combined with the July/January error, it produced a published claim that she married *"seventeen days short of her sixteenth birthday."* On the corrected dates she was **twenty-one** at her wedding and **fifty-seven** at her death, not fifty-two. Both her page and her husband's have been rewritten.

## What they confirm

**Anna Barbara Phillipp, b. 9 October 1712, d. 26 March 1743** — exactly as the Ahnentafel has it. *(Robert Earl's own "Little Histories" sketch says she died 26 March **1742** at thirty-one; on the 1743 date she was thirty. The two chart-and-Ahnentafel sources agree on 1743 against his earlier prose.)*

**Johann George Werner, married 11 February 1737/38** — matching the Ahnentafel's ∞ 11.2.1738, and still with no birth or death anywhere. He remains the line-end on the Werner side, as Robert Earl said in 1989: *"His birth and death dates are unknown."* Three separate sources now, and none of them knows.

## One conflict left open

**Anna Maria Werner's death year.** The chart says **27 February 1811**. The Ahnentafel, compiled in Germany *from the Großaspach parish registers*, says **27 February 1817**. Same day, same month, six years apart.

**The archive keeps 1817.** The Ahnentafel is the nearer source — Karl Lachenmaier reading the church books directly — and these charts are database output at one remove. Recorded here as a variant, not adopted.

Also unresolved, and unchanged by these sheets: **Johann Georg Wolf's birthplace**, written **"Rohroch"** here, **Rohrbach** on the Ahnentafel, against the **Röhrach** of the rest of the family. Probably all one village. Not yet proven to be.
