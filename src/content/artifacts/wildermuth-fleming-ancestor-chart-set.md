## Four whole branches the archive did not have

![A printed pedigree chart headed Ancestors of Catherina Boeshar, giving her birth at Brontenbach in the Rhineland-Pfalz in 1840 and her death at Marietta in 1933, with her parents Christian Boeshar and Maria Margaretha Jung and grandparents Abraham Boeshar, Maria Lehman, Johann Jung and Katharina Hofman.](../../assets/family/originals/ancestor-chart-catherina-boeshar.jpeg)

**The Boeshars.** [Catharina](/family/catharina-boeshar-wildermuth/) was the daughter of **Christian Boeshar** (b. 30 Nov 1810, d. 8 Dec 1846) and **Maria Margaretha Jung** (b. 27 Feb 1814, d. Fronhofen), of **Brontenbach, Rhineland-Pfalz**; her grandparents were **Abraham Boeshar** and **Maria Lehman**, **Johann Jung** and **Katharina Hofman**. The archive had her birthplace as Breitenbach, Pfalz, and no parents at all.

**The Schlichers.** [Flora](/family/flora-schlicher-wildermuth/)'s father **Theobald David Schlicher** (1835–1912) came from **Adenbach**, son of **Johann Peter Schlicher** (b. 1797 Adenbach, d. 1861 Marietta) and **Maria Magdalina Weyrich** (b. 1807 Marth), with **Johann Friedrich Schlicher**, **Maria Elizabeth Burgarth**, **Theobald Weyrich** and **Magdalina Burgey** behind them. Robert Earl's 1989 memoir says he was still trying to find the Schlicher hometown; these charts name it.

**The Schmidts.** Flora's mother's line: **Eva Barbara Schmidt** (b. 29 Dec 1837 Bavaria, d. 24 Oct 1915), daughter of **Johann Jacob Schmidt** (b. June 1818 Bavaria, d. 1899 Marietta) and **Eva Bourtine** (b. June 1815 Bavaria, d. 1888 Marietta).

**The Dunbars and Kelleys.** [Verona Belle](/family/verona-sheppard-fleming/)'s parents were **Seth Dunbar** (b. 1837, **Readyville, Kennebec, Maine**) and **Martha Kelley** (b. 1844, Barbour County). Behind Seth: **John V. Dunbar**, b. 1804 in **Maine**, died in Wood County, West Virginia. Behind Martha: **Johnson Kelley** (1810–1886), **Ebenezer Kelley** (b. 1763 Somerset County, m. 1790 Allegheny Co.), **Rachel Johnson** (b. 1769 New Jersey), **Robert Johnson**, **Mary**, and **Sarah Yoke**. A Maine family and a New Jersey family, both arriving in the West Virginia hills.

## One conflict resolved, one opened

![A printed pedigree chart headed Ancestors of Lewis D. Fleming, giving his birth in 1807 in the Eastern Sector of Harrison County, his marriage in 1827 and death about 1862 in Taylor County, with his parents John Fleming and Clarissa Roe and the Roe and Meeks families of New Jersey behind them.](../../assets/family/originals/ancestor-chart-lewis-d-fleming.jpeg)

**Resolved: Lewis Fleming died about 1862, not 1849.** The Sadye Irene Fleming chart in the same batch gave 1849, which the [1854 deed](/archive/fleming-marriage-record-and-deed/) disproves outright, since Lewis was conveying land that year. This chart says **"Abt. 1862 in Taylor County, West Virginia"** — matching the archive. The 1849 was Synthia's death year misplaced.

**Opened: who was Lewis's father?** These charts say **John Fleming**, born **11 October 1781 in Hampshire County, Virginia**, died **3 November 1853 at Arnold's Creek**. [Lewis's page](/family/lewis-fleming/) says **Edward Fleming**, of the Simpson Creek farm in Harrison County, a Revolutionary soldier per Joy Gilchrist's *The Squires Family*. Those are two different men, and the archive cannot hold both. **Unresolved.**

His mother is given as **Clarissa Roe** (b. 18 June 1762, Essex Co., New Jersey), daughter of **John Roe** (b. c. 1758, Morris County, New Jersey; m. 27 March 1779 at Westfield, Essex County) and **Susannah Meeks** (b. c. 1759, Westfield). *That arithmetic does not work — a father born in 1758 cannot have a daughter born in 1762 — so one of the two dates is wrong on the chart.*

## Where the charts back the archive

- **Thomas Bailey married Nancy Bartlett on 21 October 1803** in Monongalia County — agreeing with [Family Group Record 0019](/archive/bailey-family-group-record-0019/) against FamilySearch's 11 October.
- **Johann Christian married at Rietenau on 25 April 1819** — the corrected date, printed here, so he had absorbed Lachenmaier's Ahnentafel into his database.
- **Hans Adam Wildermuth and Rosina** as Andreas's parents, and **Johann Michael Lammle and Catherina** as Margaretha's — matching the [Rielingshausen pastor's annotations](/archive/rielingshausen-parish-correspondence/).
- **Synthia Bailey died 1849**, **Flora Schlicher 18 November 1919**, **William Clifford 10 September 1943**, **Catharina Boeshar 17 October 1933** — all as held.
