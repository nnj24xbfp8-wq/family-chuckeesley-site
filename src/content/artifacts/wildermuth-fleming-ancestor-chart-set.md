## What a better photograph changed

The sheets were first read here from small, washed-out photographs, and several of the entries below turn on a single numeral or a short word. Re-read at full resolution, **four things that this page previously recorded as unknown or unresolved are now legible** — and one thing it asserted turns out to have been a misreading of its own.

### The Schlicher sheet carries exact dates, and answers two open questions

The "Ancestors of Theobald David Schlicher" sheet is the one this archive leans on hardest, because [Johann Peter Schlicher](/family/johann-peter-schlicher/) has no other source at all. It gives more than was previously read off it:

| | Previously read here | On the sheet |
|---|---|---|
| Johann Peter Schlicher born | "1797" | **29 November 1797**, Adenbach |
| Johann Peter married | *(not recorded)* | **28 February 1827, at "Marth," Rhineland Pfalz** |
| Johann Peter died | "1861" | **11 March 1861**, Marietta |
| Maria Magdalina Weyrich born | "1807", place unresolved | **27 December 1807 at "Marth," Rhineland Pfalz** |
| Maria Magdalina died | *(nothing — not placed in America at all)* | **28 November 1879, Marietta** |

**Two consequences.** [Johann Peter's page](/family/johann-peter-schlicher/) asked whether he lived to see his son's wedding of 20 January 1861: **he did**, by seven weeks, and he died in the same year. And [Maria Magdalina](/family/maria-magdalina-weyrich/) — of whom this archive could previously say only that nothing placed her in America — **died in Marietta, Ohio in 1879**, eighteen years after her husband. She crossed too.

**"Marth" survives the better photograph**, so it is not a misreading of a smudge: it is written twice, as her birthplace and as the place of their 1827 marriage, and this time with a region attached — ***Rhineland Pfalz***. That narrows the search for it to the Palatinate, which is where her husband's Adenbach is.

*All of this is still chart authority and nothing more.* No register, no burial record, no passenger list. But an exact birth date is a thing a parish register can be searched against, which "1797" was not.

### Correction: the Sadye chart never gave Lewis Fleming a death of 1849

This page previously said that *"the Sadye Irene Fleming chart in the same batch gave 1849"* for [Lewis Fleming](/family/lewis-fleming/)'s death, and reasoned that the 1849 must be **[Synthia Bailey](/family/synthia-bailey/)'s** death year, misplaced.

**The reasoning was right and the reading was wrong.** On the sheet itself, Lewis D. Fleming is *"d: Abt. 1862 in Taylor County, West Virginia"* — the same as every other chart in the batch — and **1849 is printed in the box directly below his, as Synthia's own death**, in the Western Sector of Harrison County. There was never a conflict between the two charts. The 1849 was in the right box all along; the low-resolution photograph put it in the wrong one.

![A printed pedigree chart headed Ancestors of Sadye Irene Fleming, running four generations: Sadye born 15 July 1901 in Wood County, married 31 December 1921 at Marietta, died 4 August 1975; her parents James Wesley Fleming, born 20 December 1857 at Arnold's Creek, Doddridge, and Verona Belle Kelley slash Dunbar slash Sheppard, born 11 June 1869, died 27 October 1922; and behind them Thomas Bailey Fleming and Martha Turner, Seth Dunbar and Martha Kelley, with Lewis D. Fleming born 1807 and died about 1862 in Taylor County, Synthia Bailey died 1849 in the Western Sector of Harrison County, John Turner, John V. Dunbar, Ann, Johnson Kelley and Sarah Yoke.](../../assets/family/originals/ancestors-of-sadye-irene-fleming-chart.jpeg)

*Two other things on this sheet are worth noting. Verona is entered under **three surnames at once** — "Verona Belle Kelley/Dunbar/Sheppard" — which is the [maiden-name problem](/family/verona-sheppard-fleming/) this archive later had to sort out, visible in his own database as unresolved. And **Martha Turner's death is given as 11 June 1904**, the date the [Wood County register](/archive/thomas-martha-fleming-wood-county-records/) gives, against the typescript's 1903 — so his database had the register's date and his narrative did not.*

## One branch the archive did not have

*This section originally claimed the charts added four whole branches. That was wrong, and checking each name against the existing person pages is what showed it. On three of the four branches the archive was **already ahead of the charts** — it holds pages for those people, with better dates, from better sources. Only the Schlicher line is genuinely new. The corrected account follows.*

### The Schlichers — genuinely new, and it closes an open question

[Flora](/family/flora-schlicher-wildermuth/)'s father **[Theobald David Schlicher](/family/theobald-david-schlicher/)** (1835–1912) came from **Adenbach**, son of **[Johann Peter Schlicher](/family/johann-peter-schlicher/)** (b. 1797 Adenbach, d. 1861 Marietta) and **[Maria Magdalina Weyrich](/family/maria-magdalina-weyrich/)** (b. 1807 "Marth"), with **Johann Friedrich Schlicher**, **Maria Elizabeth Burgarth**, **Theobald Weyrich** and **Magdalina Burgey** behind them.

This matters more than any other line on the fourteen sheets. Robert Earl's [1989 memoir](/docs/robert-earl-wildermuth-memoir/) ends by hoping to find *"the home towns of grandma Catherina Boeshar and hopefully that of **Johann Peter Schlicher**"* — and until these charts surfaced, this archive did not even know **who Johann Peter Schlicher was**, recording the relationship as unresolved. **The charts make him Theobald's father.**

**With the caveat that cuts against everything else on this page.** On the three branches below, the charts lose to documents. Here there *is* no document — no register, no passenger list, no Marietta burial — so the charts win by default rather than on merit. That is a much weaker position, and the two new pages say so on their face. The Adenbach parish registers, or the Evangelical books of the mother parish at **Odenbach**, are what would settle it.

Nor is the appealing version of the story supported: the charts are undated beyond "c. early 1990s," so whether Robert Earl cracked this *after* the 1989 memoir, or had it all along from a correspondent, cannot be told from the sheet.

If it holds: **Adenbach is in the Landkreis Kusel** — the same district as [Catharina Boeshar](/family/catharina-boeshar-wildermuth/)'s **Breitenbach**, at the far end of it. The two home towns in that one memoir sentence would be in one district.

Two new person pages come from this sheet, and only this sheet.

### The Boeshars — the archive was already further along

![A printed pedigree chart headed Ancestors of Catherina Boeshar, giving her birth in the Rhineland-Pfalz in 1840 and her death at Marietta in 1933, with her parents Christian Boeshar and Maria Margaretha Jung and grandparents Abraham Boeshar, Maria Lehman, Johann Jung and Katharina Hofman.](../../assets/family/originals/ancestor-chart-catherina-boeshar.jpeg)

Every person on this chart **already has a page**: [Christian Boeshar](/family/christian-boeshar/), [Maria Margaretha Jung](/family/maria-margaretha-jung/), [Abraham Boeshar](/family/abraham-boeshar/), [Maria Lehman](/family/maria-lehman/), and — under their fuller forms — [Johannes Jung](/family/johannes-jung/) and [Maria Catharina Hoffmann](/family/maria-catharina-hoffmann/). The archive's dates come from FamilySearch and from the **German-script birth certificate** Robert Earl had translated for the [Little Histories](/docs/robert-earl-wildermuth-little-histories/), which is a primary record. The chart is a secondary summary and loses to it.

| | Chart | Archive |
|---|---|---|
| Birthplace | "Brontenbach" | **Breitenbach**, Kusel district — a real village, confirmed by the christening entry *"Breitenbach, BA. Homburg"* |
| Christian Boeshar born | 30 Nov 1810 | **27 Oct 1810** |
| Christian Boeshar died | 8 Dec 1846 | **5 May 1843**, buried 7 May |

Note the last row: the chart's *"8 Dec 1846"* sits one digit away from the couple's **marriage date, 8 December 1836**. A marriage date that migrated into the death field is the most economical explanation.

### The Schmidts — corroboration, with two small conflicts

![A printed pedigree chart headed Ancestors of Eva Barbara Schmidt, giving Eva born 29 December 1837 in Bavaria, married 20 January 1861 at Marietta and died 24 October 1915; her father Johann Jacob Schmidt born June 1818 in Bavaria, married 26 January 1861 at Marietta and died 25 January 1899 at Marietta; and her mother Eva Bourtine born June 1815 in Bavaria and died 7 June 1888 at Marietta.](../../assets/family/originals/ancestor-chart-eva-barbara-schmidt.jpeg)

**Eva Barbara Schmidt** (b. 29 Dec 1837 Bavaria, d. 24 Oct 1915) as daughter of **[John Jacob Schmidt](/family/john-jacob-schmidt/)** and **[Eva Bourtine](/family/eva-barbara-bourtine/)** — all already held, and the charts agree on Eva's dates exactly.

*One oddity on the sheet: **Johann Jacob's own marriage is entered as 26 January 1861** — six days after his daughter's, and twenty-four years after her birth. Either it is a second marriage the archive does not hold, or the date is a stray. Recorded, not adopted.* Worth something — though both the charts and the family-history sheet come out of the same Wildermuth research corpus, so this is agreement rather than genuinely independent confirmation. The chart's **John Jacob b. June 1818** (archive: 1817) and **Eva Bourtine d. 1888** (archive: c. 1898, from the obituary's *"preceded him by one year"*) are recorded on those pages as variants, not adopted.

[John V. Dunbar](/family/john-v-dunbar/) b. 1804 Maine, [Johnson Kelley](/family/johnson-kelley/) 1810–1886 and [Ebenezer Kelley](/family/ebenezer-kelley/) b. 1763 Somerset County all match. The archive additionally carries this line back well past the chart — to **John Patrick Kelley** of County Wicklow (b. 1725) and beyond him to **John Aaron Kelley** (b. 1702, County Kildare), the deepest documented Kelley ancestor.

**The lesson for the rest of the charts:** they are Robert Earl's database output, not his sources. Where they meet a document, the document wins.

## One conflict resolved, one opened

![A printed pedigree chart headed Ancestors of Lewis D. Fleming, giving his birth in 1807 in the Eastern Sector of Harrison County, his marriage in 1827 and death about 1862 in Taylor County, with his parents John Fleming and Clarissa Roe and the Roe and Meeks families of New Jersey behind them.](../../assets/family/originals/ancestor-chart-lewis-d-fleming.jpeg)

**Resolved: Lewis Fleming died about 1862, not 1849.** The Sadye Irene Fleming chart in the same batch gave 1849, which the [1854 deed](/archive/fleming-marriage-record-and-deed/) disproves outright, since Lewis was conveying land that year. This chart says **"Abt. 1862 in Taylor County, West Virginia"** — matching the archive. The 1849 was Synthia's death year misplaced.

**Opened: who was Lewis's father?** These charts say **John Fleming**, born **11 October 1781 in Hampshire County, Virginia**, died **3 November 1853 at Arnold's Creek**. [Lewis's page](/family/lewis-fleming/) says **Edward Fleming**, of the Simpson Creek farm in Harrison County, a Revolutionary soldier per Joy Gilchrist's *The Squires Family*. Those are two different men, and the archive cannot hold both. **Unresolved.**

His mother is given as **Clarissa Roe** (b. 18 June 1762, Essex Co., New Jersey), daughter of **John Roe** (b. c. 1758, Morris County, New Jersey; m. 27 March 1779 at Westfield, Essex County) and **Susannah Meeks** (b. c. 1759, Westfield). *That arithmetic does not work — a father born in 1758 cannot have a daughter born in 1762 — so one of the two dates is wrong on the chart.*

**And the Thomas Bailey Fleming sheet settles Susannah Meeks's date.** [Clarissa's page](/family/clarissa-roe/) records her mother's birth as **1770** from the [1998 printed chart](/archive/pedigree-charts-1998/) — *"which would make her nine at her own marriage and twelve at Clarissa's birth"* — and guesses that *"something nearer 1760 is meant."* This sheet, from the same corpus, gives **abt. 1759 in Westfield**. The guess was right, and the 1770 on the other chart is the outlier.

*It also gives two death dates the archive did not hold: **John Roe, 29 September 1829, in the Eastern Sector** of Harrison County, and **Susannah, "Abt. 1829" at Westfield, Essex County, New Jersey**. Take the second with caution — the [1820 deed](/archive/roe-rosier-deed-1820/) has Susannah in Harrison County, Virginia, examined privily by the justices in May 1821, so a New Jersey death eight years later would mean a return east. The likeliest reading is that her husband's 1829 migrated into her death field, the same way Christian Boeshar's marriage date migrated into his death field on the Boeshar sheet above. **John Roe's exact date is the useful new thing here; hers is not.***

![A printed pedigree chart headed Ancestors of Thomas Bailey Fleming, giving Thomas born February 1830 in the Western Sector of Harrison County, married 18 August 1851 in Wood County and died in Wood County; his father Lewis D. Bailey born 26 May 1732, and Hannah Thornberry born 1728 and died 1831 in the Eastern Sector of Harrison County.](../../assets/family/originals/ancestor-chart-thomas-bailey-fleming.jpeg)

![A printed pedigree chart headed Ancestors of Synthia Bailey, giving Synthia born in Preston County at an unknown date, married 14 March 1827 in the Western Sector of Harrison County and died 1849 there; her father Thomas Bailey born about 1781 and married 21 October 1803 in Monongalia County; her mother Hannah Thornberry born 1728 and died 1831 in the Eastern Sector of Harrison County; and her grandfather J.B.Y. Bailey born 26 May 1732.](../../assets/family/originals/ancestor-chart-synthia-bailey.jpeg)

**One impossible pair, printed twice.** Both Bailey sheets give **[Hannah Thornsberry](/family/hannah-thornsberry/)** as born **1728** and dead in **1831** — a hundred and three years, and a woman of fifty-three at the birth of her son [Thomas](/family/thomas-bailey/) in 1781. The archive carries the dates as he printed them, flagged; they are two of the impossible dates the FamilySearch corrections list is meant to clear.

![A printed pedigree chart headed Ancestors of Martha Turner, nearly empty, giving Martha born March 1832 in Wood County, married 18 August 1851 in Wood County and died 11 June 1904 in Wood County, with her father recorded only as John Turner and no mother named.](../../assets/family/originals/ancestor-chart-martha-turner.jpeg)

## Where the charts back the archive

- **Thomas Bailey married Nancy Bartlett on 21 October 1803** in Monongalia County — agreeing with [Family Group Record 0019](/archive/bailey-family-group-record-0019/) against FamilySearch's 11 October.
- **Johann Christian married at Rietenau on 25 April 1819** — the corrected date, printed here, so he had absorbed Lachenmaier's Ahnentafel into his database.

## The two Württemberg sheets

The German end of the pedigree is where the charts are thinnest, and where the archive has since gone furthest past them.

![A printed pedigree chart headed Ancestors of Andreus Wildermuth, giving Andreus born 10 November 1745 at Rielingshausen in the Duchy of Wuerttemberg, married 19 July 1768 there, and died 1 June 1768 there, with his father Hans Adam Wildermuth and mother Rosina, question-marked, both with all dates unknown.](../../assets/family/originals/ancestor-chart-andreus-wildermuth.jpeg)

**The Andreus sheet prints an impossible death.** It has him **married on 19 July 1768** and **dead on 1 June 1768** — seven weeks before his own wedding. [His page](/family/andreus-wildermuth/) carries **1 June 1771** from better sources, so the chart's 1768 is a year keyed from the marriage line one row up. The sheet also gives his birth as **10 November 1745** where the archive has **30 November**; a transposed numeral, and the archive's date comes from the German work rather than from this database. Behind him the sheet has only two names and no dates at all: **Hans Adam Wildermuth** and a **Rosina (?)**, both of Rielingshausen.

![A printed pedigree chart headed Ancestors of Margaretha Lammle, giving Margaretha born 7 March 1743 slash 44 at Rielingshausen, married 19 July 1768 there and died 8 May 1778 there, with her parents Johann Michael Lammle and Catherina, both of Rielingshausen and without dates.](../../assets/family/originals/ancestor-chart-margaretha-lammle.jpeg)

His wife's sheet is cleaner and agrees with the archive on the death — **8 May 1778** — but dates her birth **"7 March 1743/44"**, the double year of old-style reckoning, where [her page](/family/margaretha-laemmle/) has **1746**. The marriage date, **19 July 1768**, is the one thing the two sheets agree on exactly.

## The sisters' sheets, and a marriage date that loses to a document

The last two charts are the fullest in the batch: complete four-generation pedigrees for his sisters **[Norma Jean](/family/norma-jean-wildermuth-gault/)** (b. 14 September 1930, m. 2 February 1947) and **[Betty Joan](/family/betty-joan-wildermuth-haddox/)** (b. 17 July 1935, m. 18 May 1956), both at Marietta. Everything above them is the Wildermuth–Schlicher–Fleming–Kelley frame this archive is built on, and on the generations it can check independently the sheets hold up: [Catharina Boeshar](/family/catharina-boeshar-wildermuth/) 15 June 1840 – 17 October 1933, [William Clifford](/family/william-wildermuth/) 17 September 1866 – 10 September 1943, [Flora](/family/flora-schlicher-wildermuth/) 24 August 1870 – 18 November 1919, [Earl Adam](/family/earl-a-wildermuth/) 6 October 1899 – 6 December 1977, all exactly as the archive has them.

**With one clear exception.** Both sheets give [Johann Michael Wildermuth](/family/johann-michael-wildermuth/)'s marriage as **17 September 1864**. The [marriage certificate](/archive/wildermuth-marriage-and-census-records/), Volume 3 page 505 of the Washington County record, certifies the wedding on **7 September 1862** — and his son William Clifford was born on **17 September** 1866. A day from the son's birth and a year of its own: the database has scrambled it, and **the document wins**. *(He also enters Earl Adam's death at Marietta where the archive has Newark, Licking County, with burial at Reno.)*

![A printed four-generation pedigree chart headed Ancestors of Norma Jean Wildermuth, giving Norma Jean born 14 September 1930 at Marietta and married 2 February 1947, her parents Earl Adam Wildermuth and Sadye Irene Fleming, and behind them William Clifford Wildermuth and Flora Schlicker, Johann Michael Wildermuth born 23 August 1830 at Grossasbach with a marriage entered as 17 September 1864 and death in February 1903, Catherina Boeshar of Breitenbach, Theobald David Schlicher and Eva Barbara Schmidt, and on the Fleming side James Wesley Fleming, Verona Belle Kelley slash Dunbar slash Sheppard, Thomas Bailey Fleming, Martha Turner, Seth Dunbar and Martha Kelley.](../../assets/family/originals/ancestor-chart-norma-jean-wildermuth.jpeg)

![A printed four-generation pedigree chart headed Ancestors of Betty Joan Wildermuth, giving Betty Joan born 17 July 1935 at Marietta and married 18 May 1956, with the identical ancestry above her as the Norma Jean sheet: Earl Adam Wildermuth and Sadye Irene Fleming, William Clifford Wildermuth and Flora Schlicker, Johann Michael Wildermuth and Catherina Boeshar, Theobald David Schlicher and Eva Barbara Schmidt, James Wesley Fleming and Verona Belle Kelley slash Dunbar slash Sheppard, and behind them Thomas Bailey Fleming, Martha Turner, Seth Dunbar and Martha Kelley.](../../assets/family/originals/ancestor-chart-betty-joan-wildermuth.jpeg)
- **Hans Adam Wildermuth and Rosina** as Andreas's parents, and **Johann Michael Lammle and Catherina** as Margaretha's — matching the [Rielingshausen pastor's annotations](/archive/rielingshausen-parish-correspondence/).
- **Synthia Bailey died 1849**, **Flora Schlicher 18 November 1919**, **William Clifford 10 September 1943**, **Catharina Boeshar 17 October 1933** — all as held.
