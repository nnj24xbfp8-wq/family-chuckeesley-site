Three figures on a waterfront at **Sault Ste. Marie in 1929**, with the far shore and its buildings across the St. Marys River behind them. Seated at left is **[Donald Stuart "Don" Eesley](/family/don-eesley/)** (b. 1908), about twenty-one; at center, in glasses and a cloche hat, his aunt **[Sciota "Ota" Chenoweth Smith](/family/scioto-mafry-chenoweth/)** &mdash; his mother [Lillie Dale](/family/lillie-dale-chenoweth/)'s sister, the physician of the older Chenoweth generation and mother of [Mafry Hyatt](/family/mafry-smith-hyatt/). The photograph belongs to the same Michigan-summers period as the family's [Black Lake gatherings](/archive/black-lake-group-with-leonard-1928/) of the late 1920s. Standing at right is **[Helen Bernadine "Big Helen" (Alspach) Eesley](/family/helen-bernadine-alspach-eesley/)** &mdash; Leonard's wife, in her late twenties here.

## Which Helen

The family label names the standing woman simply **"Helen,"** and she is confirmed (per Chuck) as **"Big Helen" &mdash; [Helen Bernadine (Alspach) Eesley](/family/helen-bernadine-alspach-eesley/)**, Leonard's wife. She is *not* Don's youngest sister **[Helen (Eesley) Burnes](/family/helen-burnes/)**, who &mdash; born January 1924 &mdash; was only **five years old in 1929**. The two Helens of this generation are easy to conflate; the age settles it. This is also the **earliest photograph of Big Helen** now in the archive, and gives her a face on her page for the first time.

> *Source: Eesley family archive, family-labeled photograph ("Don, Aunt Ota and Helen, 1929, Sault St. Marie"); all three figures confirmed by Chuck Eesley, 2026 (the "Helen" resolved as Big Helen Alspach, not Helen Burnes, by the age evidence).*
