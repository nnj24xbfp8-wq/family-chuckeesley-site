A candid outdoor photograph of **[Leonard David Eesley](/family/leonard-david-eesley/)** &mdash; in a beret and a soft sweater, a slim mustache, about twenty-eight &mdash; seated on a sunny bank and cradling his small son **[Thomas Leonard "Tommy" Eesley](/family/tommy-eesley/)** against his chest. Tommy, born **1 December 1930**, is a toddler of about a year and a half here, which places the frame around **1932**.

It is a rare early father-and-son frame on the [Leonard](/family/leonard-david-eesley/) branch &mdash; Charles Leonard's eldest son with his own firstborn, the "nephew Tommy" who also appears beside his grandfather in the [Charles Leonard group portrait](/archive/charles-leonard-and-sons-late-1930s/) and in Aunt Maggie's Black Lake deck slides of the early 1930s. The setting &mdash; a sandy, sunlit bank &mdash; is consistent with the family's Black Lake, Michigan summers of those years.

> *Source: Eesley family archive, from Roberta Burnes Walker's keeping; identified by Chuck Eesley, 2026.*
