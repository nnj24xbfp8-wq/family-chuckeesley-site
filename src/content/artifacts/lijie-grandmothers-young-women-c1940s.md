Two young women in traditional long dresses, c. 1940s, from [Lijie](/family/lijie-zhou/)'s side of the family. The family's tentative read is that this shows **one of Lijie's grandmothers as a young woman, with a sister** — but which grandmother, and the second woman's identity, are **not yet confirmed**.

Now that [Sun Yunzhe](/family/yunzhe-sun/) and [Shang Yaozhen](/family/yaozhen-shang/) both have confirmed later-life faces in the archive (from the [mid-1980s Qingdao photographs](/archive/lijie-grandparents-early-childhood-qingdao-c1980s/)), a side-by-side comparison with Lijie may be able to settle whether either young woman here is one of them.
