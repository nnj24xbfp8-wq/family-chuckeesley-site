Two young women in traditional long dresses, c. 1940s — identified by the family as **two of [Lijie](/family/lijie-zhou/)'s grandmothers**: **[Shang Yaozhen](/family/yaozhen-shang/)** (maternal grandmother, 1921&ndash;2013) and **[Sun Yunzhe](/family/yunzhe-sun/)** (paternal grandmother, d. 2023). These are the **earliest images of either woman** in the archive, and the only known photograph of Sun Yunzhe.

**Two open questions** for the family to settle: **which woman is which** (the two are not yet individually matched), and **the occasion** — the two grandmothers came from different families on opposite sides of Lijie's line (the Shang/Li side and the Sun/Zhou side), whose children would not marry until 1982, so a shared photograph of them as young women is a notable frame worth explaining.
