A color photograph dated **6 June 2009**, from [Lijie](/family/lijie-zhou/)'s maternal side: a younger woman seated between two elders on a red sofa, beneath a framed 囍 (double-happiness) artwork — a Chinese home interior. **Best guess (working identification, to be confirmed by [Li Xun](/family/xun-li/)):** [Lijie](/family/lijie-zhou/) with her **paternal grandparents** — **[Sun Yunzhe](/family/yunzhe-sun/)** (center) and **[Zhou Zhaoxiang](/family/zhaoxiang-zhou/)** (right, in green) — who together match the couple identified elsewhere in the archive. The 2009 date fits (Lijie about 26; her grandparents in their late seventies).

## 中文

**Lijie母系三代同堂 &mdash; 青岛，2009年**

一张日期标注为**2009年6月6日**的彩色照片，出自[Lijie](/family/lijie-zhou/)母系：一位年轻女性与两位长辈同坐红色沙发前，身后墙上挂有"囍"字画框，为中式家居陈设。**初步推测（待[李恂](/family/xun-li/)确认）：** [周丽婕](/family/lijie-zhou/)与其**祖父母**——**[孙云哲](/family/yunzhe-sun/)**（中）与**[周兆祥](/family/zhaoxiang-zhou/)**（右，着绿色外套）合影。2009年时Lijie约26岁，祖父母约近八旬，与画面相符。
