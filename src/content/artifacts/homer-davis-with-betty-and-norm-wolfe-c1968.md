A color group of four beside a row of parked cars, **c. late 1960s** (by the cars). Left to right: **[Homer Edward Davis](/family/homer-davis/)** (1900&ndash;1982) in glasses and a grey shirt; his daughter **[Betty Jean (Davis) Wolfe](/family/betty-davis/)** (1926&ndash;2015); a small girl in front &mdash; one of the family's grandchildren, not yet named; and Betty's husband **[Norman Graham "Norm" Wolfe](/family/norman-graham-wolfe/)** (1926&ndash;2015).

Homer is about sixty-eight here, some years into the Florida-and-visiting chapter of his life. It is the **first photograph of Norm Wolfe in the archive** &mdash; the Marietta High School quarterback who married Betty in 1945 and, with her, hosted the [108 Hillcrest Drive](/places/108-hillcrest-drive-marietta/) holiday dinners of Chuck's childhood &mdash; and now his page portrait.

> *Source: Eesley family archive; Homer, Betty, and Norm identified by Chuck Eesley, 2026; the small girl (a grandchild) not yet named.*
