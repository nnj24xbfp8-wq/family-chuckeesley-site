Stuttgart is not a Wildermuth place in the way [Rielingshausen](/places/rielingshausen-town-square/) is &mdash; nobody in the family came *from* Stuttgart. But it is the city that made the family's origin villages legible to everyone back in Ohio. When [Robert Earl "Bob" Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/) &mdash; the [Marbach](/places/marbach-wildermuthstrasse/) chronicler and the family's genealogist for the Wildermuth and Fleming lines &mdash; wrote his captions on the November 1992 prints, he measured everything from Stuttgart: Marbach is *"about 25 miles north of Stuttgart,"* he noted on the back of the Wildermuthstraße photograph. The capital of Baden-Württemberg was the anchor point of the whole ancestral trip.

## The Schlossplatz fountains

The photograph he kept from the city itself is of one of the two ornate fountains on **Schlossplatz** &mdash; Castle Square &mdash; the great civic plaza in front of the Neues Schloss. The two matching fountains were installed in **1863 to honor King Wilhelm I of Württemberg**, set on either side of the Jubiläumssäule (the Jubilee Column) at the center of the square. Each is a tiered basin with cherubs and lion-head spouts around the pedestal, exactly the fountain R.E.W. framed with the palace behind and, in his eye, swans on the water.

His caption calls the spot *"KonigsPlatz."* The fountains are actually on the adjoining **Schlossplatz**; Königsplatz is the neighboring square running off Königstraße. It is a small slip of the kind his captions occasionally make &mdash; preserved here in his own words, with the correction noted, the same way [Marbach](/places/marbach-wildermuthstrasse/) keeps his verbatim note about Ottilie Wildermuth alongside the dates Chuck's later research added.

## Then and now

In 2018, [Chuck](/family/charles-eric-eesley/) returned to Baden-Württemberg and retraced the route &mdash; Rielingshausen, Marbach, and Stuttgart &mdash; photographing the same places his grandfather had. His frame of the Schlossplatz fountain closes the pairing: the same ornate tiered basin, the same palace square, twenty-six years apart, one generation of the Wildermuth line photographing what the previous one had gone to find.

> *Source: Robert Earl Wildermuth's November 1992 captioned print, scanned 2018-03-30; Chuck Eesley's 2018 Baden-Württemberg photographs. Fountain history: [Schlossplatz (Stuttgart), Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schlossplatz_(Stuttgart)).*
