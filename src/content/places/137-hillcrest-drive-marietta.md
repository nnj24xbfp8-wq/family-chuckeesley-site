137 Hillcrest Drive, Marietta, Ohio is the house [Charlie](/family/charles-eesley/) and [Terrie](/family/terrie-lee-eesley/) Eesley moved into when the family [came back to Marietta from Annapolis in 1983](/family/charles-eesley/), and the house [Chuck](/family/charles-eric-eesley/) grew up in through his early childhood before the family moved on to [Highland Ridge Road](/places/highland-ridge-road-marietta/). Chuck was three when they arrived.

## Down the street from the Wolfes

The address matters mostly for where it sits: a few doors from [108 Hillcrest Drive](/places/108-hillcrest-drive-marietta/), the home of Chuck's great-aunt [Betty (Davis) Wolfe](/family/betty-davis/) and her husband [Norm Wolfe](/family/norman-graham-wolfe/). Betty was the older sister of Chuck's maternal grandmother Dottie; her house was the holiday-host and sick-day-caregiver anchor of Chuck's childhood. That the Eesleys lived on the same short street meant the two households — the child's own home and the great-aunt's — were within a walk of each other for the years the family was on Hillcrest.

## The Marietta-address geography

137 Hillcrest joins the small cluster of family addresses across both sides of Chuck's family in and around Marietta:

- **137 Hillcrest Drive** (this page) — Chuck's own childhood house, 1983 into the mid-1980s.
- **[108 Hillcrest Drive](/places/108-hillcrest-drive-marietta/)** — the Wolfes' house, a few doors down.
- **[Highland Ridge Road](/places/highland-ridge-road-marietta/)** — the house Will the architect designed and built, where the family lived for most of the rest of Chuck's childhood.
- **[651 Sixth Street](/places/651-sixth-street-marietta/)** — Will and Peggy's first Marietta house, where Charlie grew up.
- **[424 N 7th Street](/places/424-n-seventh-street-marietta/)** — Grandma Peggy's widow's house.
- **[413 Harmar Street](/places/413-harmar-street-marietta/)** — Robert Earl Wildermuth's childhood house on the maternal side.
