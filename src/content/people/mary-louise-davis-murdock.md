**Mary Louise Davis** was the **eldest of the three Davis sisters** &mdash; the children of [Homer Edward Davis](/family/homer-davis/), the Marietta-area truck driver, and [Bessie Marie (Hill) Davis](/family/bessie-hill-davis/), who would die of lung cancer at 44 in 1950 while her youngest daughter Betty cared for her. Mary was born **17 January 1924**, thirteen months before [Dorothy Marie](/family/dorothy-davis-wildermuth/) (Chuck's grandmother), and about two years before [Betty Jean](/family/betty-davis/) (Chuck's great-aunt Wolfe).

She appears in the family photographic record from very early. The [c. 1929 photograph of the three Davis sisters](/archive/davis-sisters-three-mary-dorothy-betty-c-1929/) places her on the left at age five, dressed in a knee-length dress with bobbed hair. The [c. 1930 *"Why so glum?"* portrait](/archive/davis-sisters-1930-why-so-glum/) names her age six. The [c. 1931 sisters-with-puppies snapshot](/archive/davis-sisters-puppies-1931/) catches her barefoot in the yard. The [c. 1935 outdoor group portrait](/archive/davis-sisters-group-portrait-c-1935/) places her in the back row near center at about eleven, with the other two sisters scattered through the same labeled cast. Together these five childhood pictures &mdash; Mary, Dorothy, and Betty over six years &mdash; are the earliest visual record of the maternal-side Davis household this archive holds.

## The Unionville house fire

Mary was a small child when the [Davis family home in Unionville, Ohio caught fire and burned to the ground while her parents were away](/family/dorothy-davis-wildermuth/) &mdash; the story her brother-in-law [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/) preserved in the [1990 Wildermuth/Fleming Heritage](/docs/wildermuth-fleming-heritage-1990/). The three sisters moved into a small undamaged shed while their parents lived in a poorly insulated one-room garage through the cold winter that followed. Mary was the oldest of the three Davis girls through that recovery &mdash; eight or nine at the time of the fire by Robert Earl's dating &mdash; and would have been the one looking out for the younger two.

She and Dorothy started school together at the **Unionville Grade School** in southern Washington County before both later transferred to Marietta High School.

## Marriage to a Murdock

Mary's married surname surfaces in print only at the end of her sisters' obituaries. **[Dorothy's 2010 obituary](/family/dorothy-davis-wildermuth/)** spells the name **Murdoch**. **[Betty's 2015 obituary](/docs/betty-jean-wolfe-obituary-2015/)** spells the same name **Murdock**. Newspaper transcription accounts for most of the discrepancy &mdash; both spellings appear in 20th-century American records as variants of the same Scottish-derived surname.

Mary's husband's first name and the date and location of the marriage are not in this archive. He is not the Cpl. **[Jesse Murdoch](/family/jesse-murdoch/)** from Tyler, Texas who was tail gunner on Robert Earl's B-24 crew &mdash; that is a separate Murdoch, in the maternal-line crew record, presumably unrelated.

## Death before 2010

Mary predeceased both of her younger sisters. She is named in [Dorothy's 2010 obituary](/family/dorothy-davis-wildermuth/) as the one sister "preceded in death", and in [Betty's 2015 obituary](/docs/betty-jean-wolfe-obituary-2015/) again the same way. The exact date and place are not in this archive. The Davis-side records were transmitted forward primarily through the surviving sisters &mdash; Dorothy in Atlanta, Betty in St. Petersburg &mdash; so Mary's later-life record sits in whatever files her children kept.

If she had children, they would be Chuck's **first cousins once removed on the Davis side**. They are not surfaced in this archive yet.
