Through her mother she is a granddaughter of [Charles Leonard Eesley](/family/charles-leonard-eesley/) and [Lillie Dale (Chenoweth) Eesley](/family/lillie-dale-chenoweth/), which places her on the paternal Eesley line as Chuck's **first cousin once removed**.
