Thomas Eesley was born about **1700 at Banbury, Oxfordshire**, and died **1 June 1750 at Oxford St Aldate**, in the city of Oxford about twenty miles south of the family's Banbury base. He was the husband of **Sarah** (her maiden name not yet recovered) and the father of at least two sons recorded at Banbury: **Jethro** (b. 1730) and **[William Eesley](/family/william-eesley-1731/)** (1731–1777) — the William whose son [Joseph](/family/joseph-eesley/) begins the documented descent that runs through the miller [John Eesley](/family/john-eesley-1800/) to the Eesleys who crossed to Canada, Michigan, and Ohio.

He sits at the top of the Banbury Eesley cluster whose baptisms fill the St Mary's, Banbury register through the 1730s. Per the family's FamilySearch tree his own father was **Hugh Easley (b. 1675)**, with a mother **Hannah** — the earliest Eesley name the line reaches so far, though documented there only by name.

The record carries two small uncertainties worth noting: the tree also lists a **baptism at Shoreditch, Middlesex (London), on 30 August 1710** against this same Thomas, which does not sit easily with a Banbury birth about 1700 — a likely conflation, or a hint that the family moved between Oxfordshire and London in this generation. Both his and his son William's death/residence entries point to **Oxford St Aldate**, so the family's center may have shifted from Banbury toward the city of Oxford in the mid-eighteenth century before returning to the Banbury–Hanwell area by Joseph's generation.

> *Source: [Dale Eesley / FamilySearch — Thomas Eesley (GN35-C53)](https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/GN35-C53); St Mary's, Banbury parish baptism register, 1730s (Oxfordshire Church of England Baptisms, 1538–1812).*
