Ann Dunbar is **Chuck's maternal four-times-great-grandmother** &mdash; the ancestor the [Wildermuth &amp; Bain book](/docs/wildermuth-family-of-marietta-book/) singles out by name, heading her register entry *"Dunbar, Ann"* with the bracketed note **"[Terrie Lee (Wildermuth) Bain's great (times 3) grandmother]."** That editorial aside is the book's own confirmation that this Maine-born woman sits on Chuck's direct maternal line.

She was born in **1812 in Maine** and married **[John V. Dunbar](/family/john-v-dunbar/)** (b. ca 1804). The couple raised their family first at **Readfield, Kennebec County, Maine**, then moved to **Bull Run, Wood County, (West) Virginia** between the 1850 and 1860 censuses. Ann was still living in Wood County at the **1870 census**, age 58.

Her **maiden name has not yet been recovered** &mdash; the register lists her only as *"Dunbar, Ann (née ?)."* Recovering it, and her parents back in Maine, is the open end of this branch.

## Her children

Ann and John's nine children were **Vernum Augusteen** (1834), **[Seth G.](/family/seth-grosvenor-dunbar/)** (1837 &mdash; Chuck's direct ancestor), **Josephine C.** (1839), **Virginia W.** (1841), **James G.** (1843), **George H.** (1845), **Mary C.** (1847), **Abbee D.** (1849), and **Thomas I.** (1851). Through Seth &mdash; and his daughter [Verona Belle (Dunbar) Fleming](/family/verona-sheppard-fleming/) &mdash; the line runs to [Sadye](/family/sadye-fleming-wildermuth/), [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/), [Terrie](/family/terrie-lee-eesley/), and Chuck.

> *Source: [Wildermuth Family of Marietta, Ohio](/docs/wildermuth-family-of-marietta-book/) (Wildermuth & Bain), the "Dunbar, Ann (née ?)" register entry, p. 109, quoting the 1850 Kennebec County, Maine, and 1860/1870 Wood County, West Virginia censuses.*
