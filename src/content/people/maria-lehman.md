Maria Lehman is, so far, **a name without a record**. In the family tree she is the wife of **[Abraham Boeshar](/family/abraham-boeshar/)** and the mother of **[Christian Boeshar](/family/christian-boeshar/)** (1810–1843), the Breitenbach miner — but no birth, death, or place is recorded for her. She was presumably a Breitenbach-area woman of the late 18th century, in the same **Kusel district of the Rhineland-Palatinate** (the **Pfalz**) as the rest of this branch.

She is **Chuck's five-times-great-grandmother** on the maternal Wildermuth side, through Christian → [Catharina Boeshar](/family/catharina-boeshar-wildermuth/) → [William Clifford Wildermuth](/family/william-wildermuth/) → Earl Adam → Robert Earl → Terrie → Chuck. Her dates, like her husband's, wait in the Breitenbach parish registers.
