**Nancy Bartlett** married **[Thomas Bailey](/family/thomas-bailey/)** on **21 October 1803** in **Monongalia County, Virginia**. She is Chuck's maternal **5×-great-grandmother** on the Fleming line.

Her daughter **[Synthia Bailey](/family/synthia-bailey/)** married **[Lewis B Fleming](/family/lewis-fleming/)** in 1827, carrying this line into the Fleming and then the Wildermuth families.

Her father is recorded as **[Thomas Bartlett](/family/thomas-bartlett/)**; her mother appears in the record **only as "Sarah"**, with no surname or dates given. Lewis Fleming's page notes that "the Baileys and Bartletts were old Virginia families."

> *Source: the [Bailey Family Group Record](/archive/bailey-family-group-record-0019/), compiled by [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/), c. 1990.*
