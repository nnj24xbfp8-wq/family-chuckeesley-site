Joseph "Joe" Kamiab married **[Jeanne Eesley](/family/jeanne-eesley-kamiab/)** — eldest of Will and Peggy Eesley's four children and Chuck's paternal aunt — **in Marietta in June 1965**. Her brother [Charlie Eesley](/family/charles-leonard-eesley/) names the engagement and wedding in his [12th-grade autobiography](/docs/charles-eesley-12th-grade-autobiography-1965/), and Joe appears in the [Will and Peggy Eesley family portrait with Joe, c. 1965](/archive/eesley-family-with-joe-kamiab-1965/) that captures the whole family around that wedding.

He is the father of **[Stephanie "Stephie" Kamiab](/family/stephanie-kamiab/)**, Chuck's first cousin on the paternal Eesley side.
