John Jacob Schmidt is the Bavarian farmer who carried the Schmidt line to Ohio &mdash; and, through his daughter Eva, a direct ancestor on Chuck's maternal Schlicher&ndash;Wildermuth line: John Jacob &rarr; [Eva (Schmidt) Schlicher](/family/eva-schmidt-schlicher/) &rarr; [Flora Schlicher Wildermuth](/family/flora-schlicher-wildermuth/) &rarr; [Earl Adam Wildermuth](/family/earl-a-wildermuth/) &rarr; [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/) &rarr; [Terrie](/family/terrie-lee-eesley/) &rarr; Chuck.

## From Bavaria to a farm near Lowell

He was born in **June 1817 in Bavaria** (the exact day is not recorded) and married **[Eva Barbara Bourtine](/family/eva-barbara-bourtine/)**. To that marriage were born **eight children &mdash; four boys and four girls** &mdash; and in naming the sons John kept the old German custom of giving each boy the name **John (Johann)** with a different middle name. The family emigrated from Bavaria in the late 1840s: the older children were born in Bavaria, the youngest three in Washington County, Ohio. John became **a respected farmer near Lowell, Washington County, Ohio**, in the German farming community along the Muskingum above Marietta.

His eight children:

- **Johann Andrew Schmidt** (b. 1835, Bavaria)
- **[Eva Schmidt](/family/eva-schmidt-schlicher/)** (b. 29 December 1837, Bavaria; d. 24 October 1915) &mdash; married **[Theobald David Schlicher](/family/theobald-david-schlicher/)** on 20 January 1861 at Marietta; Chuck's direct ancestor
- **John George Schmidt** (b. 1841, Bavaria)
- **Prist Schmidt** (b. 1843, Bavaria)
- **Catherine Schmidt** (b. 1847, Bavaria)
- **John Laurence Schmidt** (b. 1849, Washington County, Ohio)
- **John Jacob Schmidt Jr.** (b. 1853, Washington County, Ohio)
- **Adeline Schmidt** (b. 1855, Washington County, Ohio)

## Death, 1899

His wife **Eva Barbara preceded him in death by one year**; after she died, John moved in with his son-in-law and daughter, **Theobald and Eva (Schmidt) Schlicher**, on **Greene Street in Marietta**. He died there on **25 January 1899**, age 81, of *"diseases incident to that advanced period of his life"* &mdash; asthma and old age. His obituary ran in the **26 January 1899 *Daily Register*** of Marietta (on microfilm in the Marietta College Library genealogy section), naming him *"a well known and highly respected German resident"* and a prominent farmer in the German community; it recorded that he left two sons and two daughters &mdash; the daughters being **Mrs. Schlicher** and **Mrs. Lena Schaefer** of Marietta &mdash; and that **his sons then resided in the West**.
