**周伟** &mdash; [周兆帧](/family/zhaozheng-zhou/)与[Su Ying Xian](/family/su-ying-xian/)之女，与[周燕](/family/yan-zhou/)为双胞胎，**1970年生**。妹[周亮](/family/liang-zhou/)生于1976年。

她出生并成长于父亲被分配去的阳信。**1991年全家调回青岛**时她二十岁 &mdash; 这次调动是堂兄[周玲](/family/ling-zhou/)（丽婕之父）多年奔走才办成的。

## English

**Zhou Wei** is a daughter of **[Zhou Zhaozheng](/family/zhaozheng-zhou/)** and **[Su Ying Xian](/family/su-ying-xian/)**, and one of the twins born in **1970**. Their younger sister **[Zhou Liang](/family/liang-zhou/)** followed in 1976.

She was born and raised in **Yangxin County**, in the far north of Shandong, where the state had posted her father and would not let him leave. She was twenty when the family's transfer back to **Qingdao came through in 1991** &mdash; the transfer her father's nephew **[Zhou Ling](/family/ling-zhou/)**, Lijie's father, spent years arranging.

The whole story is at **[Twenty-four years](/docs/twenty-four-years/)**.

> *Details withheld &mdash; living. She is recorded here under the name the family uses, and with the birth year only, at the sisters' request.*
