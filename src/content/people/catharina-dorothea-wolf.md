Catharina Dorothea Wolf was born **22 July 1766 in [Großaspach](/places/grossaspach/)**, Württemberg, and married **[Adam Wildermuth](/family/adam-wildermuth/)** there on **25 October 1791**. She was the mother of the family's nine recorded children — among them **[Johann Christian Wildermuth](/family/johann-christian-wildermuth/)** (b. 1794), the ancestor through whom the line reaches **[Johann Michael](/family/johann-michael-wildermuth/)**, the 1847 emigrant to Marietta, and on down to the American Wildermuths.

Adam died young, in **1800**, when Catharina was only **34**; she outlived him by thirty-five years, dying **3 April 1835**.

Her parents were **[Johann Georg Wolf](/family/johann-georg-wolf/)** and **[Anna Maria Werner](/family/anna-maria-werner/)** (b. 1739) — and through her mother the Wildermuth descent joins the **Werner–Phillipp** family, back to **[Johann Georg Werner](/family/johann-georg-werner/)** and **[Anna Barbara Phillipp](/family/barbara-phillipp/)** (1712–1742) of the Aspach villages.
