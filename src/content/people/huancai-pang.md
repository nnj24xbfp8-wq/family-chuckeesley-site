**庞焕彩**（姓氏确定；名字汉字待家族确认）&mdash; 周丽婕的曾祖母，[周茂礼](/family/maoli-zhou/)之妻，育有三子女：[周兆祥](/family/zhaoxiang-zhou/)（周丽婕之祖父）、[周兆帧](/family/zhaozheng-zhou/)、[周秀珍](/family/xiuzhen-zhou/)。**1908年生于胶州，1992年4月6日卒于青岛** &mdash; 丈夫[周茂礼](/family/maoli-zhou/)于1935年早逝，她此后独自守家逾半个世纪。她伴这一代经历了二十世纪中叶山东省的政治动荡岁月。娘家庞氏事迹尚待补全。

## English

Pang Huancai is the mother of the three Zhou siblings — [Zhaoxiang](/family/zhaoxiang-zhou/), [Zhaozheng](/family/zhaozheng-zhou/), and [Xiuzhen](/family/xiuzhen-zhou/) — and the partner who raised that generation through the upheaval years of mid-20th-century Shandong. The family tree records her as **born in 1908 in Jiaozhou** and dying **6 April 1992 in Qingdao** — meaning she outlived her husband [Maoli](/family/maoli-zhou/), who died in 1935, by **fifty-seven years**, holding the family together across nearly the whole span of the century. Her own Pang-family origin is not yet recorded here.
