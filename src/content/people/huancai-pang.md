**庞焕彩**（姓氏确定；名字汉字待家族确认）&mdash; 周丽婕的曾祖母，[周茂礼](/family/maoli-zhou/)之妻，育有三子女：[周兆祥](/family/zhaoxiang-zhou/)（周丽婕之祖父）、[周兆帧](/family/zhaozheng-zhou/)、[周秀珍](/family/xiuzhen-zhou/)。**1908年生于胶州，1992年4月6日卒于青岛** &mdash; 丈夫[周茂礼](/family/maoli-zhou/)于1935年早逝，她此后独自守家逾半个世纪。她伴这一代经历了二十世纪中叶山东省的政治动荡岁月。娘家庞氏事迹尚待补全。

## 守寡持家 — 据周玲手记

2026年8月，Lijie之父[周玲](/family/ling-zhou/)手书[两页回忆](/docs/zhou-ling-uncle-recollection/)，其中记下了这位祖母的一生。

丈夫去世时，她独自抚养三个孩子，**又没有工作**。幼子[周兆帧](/family/zhaozheng-zhou/)才一岁多，长子[周兆祥](/family/zhaoxiang-zhou/)与女儿[周秀珍](/family/xiuzhen-zhou/)只得**去当学徒工**挣钱养家。

> *"我听爸爸说奶奶经常去菜市场捡菜叶或捡人不要的蔬菜来家吃维持生活。"*

**三个孩子中，唯有幼子读到大学毕业。** 1966年他毕业于青岛医学院，却被国家分配到外地 &mdash; 先是青海省，后因家庭困难改派山东最贫困的惠民地区阳信县中医院，一去不返。

**她终身未能等到他调回。** 晚年全家皆在青岛，独他一人在外。整天叨念，常年流泪，**眼睛也看不见了**。

> *"她说她惟一的心事就是想让叔叔回到青岛来。"*

托人办理调动多年，始终未成。她1992年去世。

> *注：除姓氏外人名汉字为推测，待家族确认。*

## English

Pang Huancai is the mother of the three Zhou siblings — [Zhaoxiang](/family/zhaoxiang-zhou/), [Zhaozheng](/family/zhaozheng-zhou/), and [Xiuzhen](/family/xiuzhen-zhou/) — and the one who raised that generation through the upheaval years of mid-20th-century Shandong. The family tree records her as **born in 1908 in Jiaozhou** and dying **6 April 1992 in Qingdao**, decades after her husband [Maoli](/family/maoli-zhou/). Her own Pang-family origin is not yet recorded here.

## Widowed, and what she did about it

In August 2026 her grandson **[Zhou Ling](/family/ling-zhou/)** — Lijie's father — wrote out [two pages by hand](/docs/zhou-ling-uncle-recollection/) about this household. It is the only substantial account of her in the archive.

When her husband died she was left with **three children and no work of her own**. The youngest, [Zhaozheng](/family/zhaozheng-zhou/), was just over a year old. Her eldest son [Zhaoxiang](/family/zhaoxiang-zhou/) and her daughter [Xiuzhen](/family/xiuzhen-zhou/) were sent out **as apprentice workers** to bring money in.

> *"My father told me that my grandmother would often go to the **vegetable market to pick up cabbage leaves, or vegetables other people had thrown away**, and bring them home to eat so the family could get by."*

**One of the three got an education, and it was the baby.** Zhaozheng graduated from Qingdao Medical College in 1966 — and the state then assigned him away: first Qinghai, then, after he appealed on grounds of his family's hardship, the poorest prefecture in Shandong. He never came back.

## Her one remaining wish

Everyone else was in Qingdao. She grew old talking about it every day.

> *"Missing her youngest son, she cried often, and her **eyes failed until she could no longer see**. She said the one thing still on her mind was to have my uncle come back to Qingdao."*

Her son spent years asking people to arrange the transfer, and it never came through — her grandson went with him to deliver gifts to the officials handling it. She died in 1992.

The woman who fed three children on other people's discarded vegetables got one of them through medical school, and then could not get him back into the same city.
