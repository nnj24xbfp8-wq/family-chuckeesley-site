Byron E. Hill married **[Ida Jeanetta "Nettie" (Ullman) Hill](/family/ida-jeanetta-ullman-hill/)** on **20 March 1912**. He was born **11 October 1887** and died **17 March 1932**, age 44 — leaving Nettie a widow for the last forty-five years of her life.

His is the surname behind one of the archive's neat coincidences: Nettie's older sister [Clara Ullmann](/family/clara-victoria-ullmann/) married [Harvey Hendershot Hill](/family/harvey-hendershot-hill/), so both Ullmann sisters became "Mrs. Hill" — but Byron came from a **separate Hill family**, not Harvey's, so the matching surname is chance, not a double-brother marriage.

He and Nettie had **several children**, seen with their parents in the [c. 1920 family portrait](/archive/byron-nettie-hill-family-c1920/) — two boys and a baby — though the archive does not yet record the children's names (the 1920 U.S. Census for Byron's Ohio household would supply them). In the portrait Byron stands at right, a slim young man in wire-rimmed glasses and a dark suit.

He is **Chuck's great-great-granduncle** by marriage on the Ullman/Hill side.

> *Source: [Eesley/Wildermuth family GEDCOM](/docs/dale-eesley-familysearch-tree/) — Byron E. Hill (b. 11 October 1887, d. 17 March 1932), m. Ida Jeanetta Ullman 20 March 1912; the c. 1920 family portrait, family papers transmitted by Chuck Eesley 2026.*
