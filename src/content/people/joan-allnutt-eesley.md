Joan (Allnutt) Eesley was the **first wife of [Daniel Edburn "Dan" Eesley](/family/daniel-edburn-eesley/)** (1931–2018) and the mother of their two daughters — **[Kate (Eesley) Grey](/family/kate-eesley-grey/)** and **[Beth (Eesley) MacLean](/family/beth-eesley-maclean/)**. Through her daughters she is a direct ancestor of the living Bay Area line of the [R. Albert Eesley](/family/albert-robert-eesley-jr/) branch that reconnected with Chuck in Palo Alto in 2019.

Dan later married [Susan Thompson Lucas](/family/susan-thompson-lucas-eesley/). Joan's own birth and family background are not yet recorded here.
