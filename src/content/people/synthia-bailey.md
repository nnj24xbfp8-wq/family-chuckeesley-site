**Synthia Bailey** &mdash; recorded variously as *Synthia* and *Cynthia* &mdash; is the wife of [Lewis Fleming](/family/lewis-fleming/) and Chuck's maternal **4×-great-grandmother** on the Fleming line.

She was a daughter of **[Thomas Bailey](/family/thomas-bailey/)** and **[Nancy Bartlett](/family/nancy-bartlett/)**, of Harrison County, Virginia (now West Virginia). On **14 March 1827**, with the written consent of both her parents and Lewis's father Edward, she married Lewis B Fleming, Cylis Hickerson officiating. Soon after the marriage the couple moved west to the **Arnold's Creek** area in the Western District of Harrison County — the district that became **Doddridge County** in 1863, and the settlement where her grandson [James Wesley Fleming](/family/wesley-fleming/) would be born in 1855.

Her eldest son, **[Thomas Bailey Fleming](/family/thomas-bailey-fleming/)** (b. February 1830), carries her father's name in his own — the clearest surviving trace of the Bailey family inside the Fleming line.

Synthia died about **1849**; Lewis afterward married the widow Mary Lake.

## Source

Her parentage, marriage date, and siblings come from the **[Bailey Family Group Record](/archive/bailey-family-group-record-0019/)** (LDS form 0019) compiled and submitted by **[Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/)** from Casselberry, Florida, c. 1990 — the same research pass that produced the [Wildermuth/Fleming heritage material](/docs/wildermuth-fleming-heritage-1990/). The record lists her four siblings: **Thornsberry Bailey Jr.** (b. August 1804, d. 25 May 1857, Preston County), **Sarah** (m. William Vannort), **Elizabeth** (b. Taylor County; m. Dempsey Welch), and **James** (b. Taylor County).
