**李凤廷**（姓氏确定；名字汉字已由Lijie之母于2026年6月确认）&mdash; 周丽婕的外曾祖父，李氏一脉。[邱荣敬](/family/rongjing-qiu/)之夫，育有四子女：[李仲初](/family/zhongchu-li/)（周丽婕之外祖父）、[李蕴华](/family/yunhua-li/)、[李城武](/family/chengwu-li/)、[李蕴英](/family/yunying-li/)。家谱记其**卒于青岛**；生年及早年事迹待补。其女[李蕴华](/family/yunhua-li/)与其子[李仲初](/family/zhongchu-li/)分别与尚家的[尚耀福](/family/yaofu-shang/)、[尚耀真](/family/yaozhen-shang/)成婚 &mdash; 李、尚两家在同代结成[双重姻亲](/family/yaofu-shang/)。

> *注：除姓氏外人名汉字为推测，已由Lijie之母于2026年6月确认。*

## English

Li Fengting is named in the family tree as the father of [Zhongchu Li](/family/zhongchu-li/) (Lijie's grandfather) and his three siblings &mdash; [Yunhua Li](/family/yunhua-li/), [Chengwu Li](/family/chengwu-li/), and [Yunying Li](/family/yunying-li/); it records him as **dying in Qingdao**, though his birth year and earlier life are not yet documented. Two of his children married into the Shang family in the same generation: his daughter [Yunhua](/family/yunhua-li/) married [Yaofu Shang](/family/yaofu-shang/), and his son [Zhongchu](/family/zhongchu-li/) married Yaofu's sister [Yaozhen Shang](/family/yaozhen-shang/) (Lijie's grandmother) — the [Li–Shang double marriage](/family/yaofu-shang/) that ties the two Qingdao families together twice. See [The Qingdao Families — Zhou, Li, and Shang](/docs/qingdao-families-zhou-li-shang/) for the full picture.
