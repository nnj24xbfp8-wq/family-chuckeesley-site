Thomas Bailey Fleming is the **Fleming generation that closes the line** between the Arnold's Creek settler [Lewis Fleming](/family/lewis-fleming/) and Sadye's father. Dale Eesley's FamilySearch tree makes the descent explicit: Thomas is the son of **[Lewis B Fleming](/family/lewis-fleming/)** and Cynthia (Bailey) Fleming, and the father of **[James Wesley Fleming](/family/wesley-fleming/)** — so what had been the archive's working guess is now documented.

He was born **February 1830** at **Arnold's Creek**, in the Doddridge County hill country his father had settled, and on **28 August 1851** married **Martha Turner**. His father [Lewis](/family/lewis-fleming/) sold him fifty acres at **Burnt Cabin Slope** before moving east to Taylor County — the land Thomas farmed and raised his family on.

That family was large — the GEDCOM records **thirteen children**, the eldest being **[James Wesley Fleming](/family/wesley-fleming/)** (b. 20 December 1855), Chuck's maternal great-great-grandfather. Among the younger sons the Civil War is written into the names themselves: **Ulysses Grant Fleming** and **George McClellan Fleming**, both born in 1864 and named for Union generals — the same Union sympathy that had sent Thomas's uncle John into the Union Army while another Fleming son wore Confederate gray. The West Virginia Flemings were, on this branch, a Union family.

He is **Chuck's maternal 3×-great-grandfather** — Thomas Bailey → [James Wesley Fleming](/family/wesley-fleming/) → [Sadye](/family/sadye-fleming-wildermuth/) → [Robert Earl](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/) → Terrie → Chuck.
