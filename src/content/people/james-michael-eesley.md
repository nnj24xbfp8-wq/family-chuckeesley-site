James Michael "Mike" Eesley was born **26 October 1938** &mdash; the younger son of **[Leonard David Eesley](/family/leonard-david-eesley/)** (1904&ndash;1976) and **[Helen Bernadine Alspach](/family/helen-bernadine-alspach-eesley/)** (1902&ndash;1986), and the younger brother of **[Thomas Leonard "Tommy" Eesley](/family/tommy-eesley/)** (b. 1930).

Per Bean's 1985 register he married **Adrienne Woods**, and they had two sons:

- **Gregory Scott Eesley**, b. 22 March 1968
- **Thomas Patrick Eesley**, b. 19 May 1969
