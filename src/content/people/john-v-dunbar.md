John V. Dunbar is the ancestor who carried the Dunbar line **from Maine to West Virginia** &mdash; and the reason the Dunbars, whom [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/) remembered as an established Wood County family, in fact began as New Englanders. He is **Chuck's maternal four-times-great-grandfather** on the Dunbar branch: John V. → [Seth Grosvenor Dunbar](/family/seth-grosvenor-dunbar/) → [Verona Belle (Dunbar) Fleming](/family/verona-sheppard-fleming/) → [Sadye Irene Fleming](/family/sadye-fleming-wildermuth/) → [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/) → [Terrie Lee](/family/terrie-lee-eesley/) → Chuck.

## From Readfield, Maine to Bull Run, Wood County

Born about **1804 in Maine**, John V. Dunbar appears in the **1850 census at Readfield, Kennebec County, Maine**, a **farmer**, age 46, with his wife [Ann](/family/ann-dunbar/) (age 37) and seven children. By the **1860 census** the whole household had moved south to **Bull Run, in the northwest corner of Wood County, (West) Virginia** &mdash; near Waverly and St. Marys, close to the Pleasants County line &mdash; where John is listed as a **steam-mill owner**. He was still there at the **1870 census**, farming, with a property value of $2,500. The family's relocation between 1850 and 1860 is what planted the Dunbars in the Wood County hill country where his granddaughter Verona would be born in 1869.

## A death date, at last

This page long ended with no death recorded. [Robert Earl Wildermuth's 1998 pedigree chart](/archive/pedigree-charts-1998/) supplies one: **22 November 1884, at Parkersburg, West Virginia**. He was about eighty, and he had outlived his son [Seth](/family/seth-grosvenor-dunbar/) by fourteen years.

The same chart gives his birth as **1808**. This archive keeps **c. 1804**, because the **1850 Readfield census** records him as **aged 46**, which the chart cannot outrank. Recorded as a variant.

## Ann and the nine children

With his wife **[Ann Dunbar](/family/ann-dunbar/)** (b. 1812, Maine; maiden name not yet recovered), John had **nine children**, all but the youngest born in Maine before the move:

1. **Vernum Augusteen Dunbar** (b. 5 November 1834; m. Sabina)
2. **[Seth G. Dunbar](/family/seth-grosvenor-dunbar/)** (b. 1837) &mdash; Chuck's direct ancestor, the father of Verona Belle Dunbar by Martha Kelley
3. **Josephine C. Dunbar** (b. 1839)
4. **Virginia W. Dunbar** (b. 1841)
5. **James G. Dunbar** (b. 1843; m. "May")
6. **George H. Dunbar** (b. 1845; m. Annie/Anice; d. 1920)
7. **Mary C. Dunbar** (b. 1847)
8. **Abbee D. Dunbar** (b. 1849)
9. **Thomas I. Dunbar** (b. 1851)
