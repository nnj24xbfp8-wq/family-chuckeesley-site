**周洁** &mdash; [周兆帧](/family/zhaozheng-zhou/)与[Su Ying Xian](/family/su-ying-xian/)之长女，与[周义](/family/yi-zhou/)为双胞胎，**1970年10月18日生**。妹[周善](/family/shan-zhou/)生于1976年。

她出生并成长于父亲被分配去的[阳信](/family/zhaozheng-zhou/)。**1991年全家调回青岛**时她二十岁 &mdash; 这次调动是堂兄[周玲](/family/ling-zhou/)（丽婕之父）多年奔走才办成的。她与姊妹三人此后的人生，皆由此一转。

> *详细生平从略 &mdash; 在世。*

## English

**Zhou Jie** is the eldest daughter of **[Zhou Zhaozheng](/family/zhaozheng-zhou/)** and **[Su Ying Xian](/family/su-ying-xian/)**, and a twin with **[Zhou Yi](/family/yi-zhou/)** &mdash; both born **18 October 1970**. Their younger sister **[Zhou Shan](/family/shan-zhou/)** followed in 1976.

She was born and raised in **Yangxin County**, where the state had posted her father and would not let him leave. She was twenty when the family's transfer back to **Qingdao came through in 1991** &mdash; the transfer her father's nephew **[Zhou Ling](/family/ling-zhou/)**, Lijie's father, spent years arranging. As Lijie put it, translating her father: the daughters were *"very happy to get back, and get better &mdash; both life quality and better education."*

The full account is on **[the dinner-table recording page](/docs/zhou-ling-bringing-his-uncle-home/)**.
