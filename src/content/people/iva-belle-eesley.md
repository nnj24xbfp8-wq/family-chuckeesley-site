**Iva Belle Eesley** was the first child of **[John Franklin Eesley](/family/john-f-eesley/)** and **[Kittie Belle Scott](/family/kittie-belle-scott/)**, born in **1887** — the year her father returned to Plainwell for good and bought the roller rink that became the Sunshine Flour Mill — and dead in **1888**, in infancy. Her given name carries her mother's: Kittie **Belle**.

She is buried in the family plot at **Hillside Cemetery, Plainwell**. Of her parents' three children only her brother **[Harold John](/family/harold-john-eesley/)** reached adulthood; their younger brother **[Franklin R. B.](/family/franklin-rb-eesley/)** died in 1908 at about ten.


> *Source: Find a Grave, Hillside Cemetery, Plainwell, Michigan — family listing on the memorial for [John Franklin Eesley](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/132028385/john-franklin-eesley) (memorial 5884027).*
