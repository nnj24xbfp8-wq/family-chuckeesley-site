**Iva Belle Eesley** was the first child of **[John Franklin Eesley](/family/john-f-eesley/)** and **[Kittie Belle Scott](/family/kittie-belle-scott/)**, born in **1887** — the year her father returned to Plainwell for good and bought the roller rink that became the Sunshine Flour Mill — and dead in **1888**, in infancy. Her given name carries her mother's: Kittie **Belle**.

She is buried in the family plot at **Hillside Cemetery, Plainwell**.

Her short life is part of the answer to a question this archive had left open. John Franklin's grave marker reads **FATHER**, and Dale's tree recorded no children for him, which the [note on his page](/family/john-f-eesley/) flagged as "a wrinkle worth a second look." The Find a Grave record for the Hillside plot names three children — Iva Belle, **[Harold John](/family/harold-john-eesley/)**, and **[Franklin R. B.](/family/franklin-rb-eesley/)** — of whom only Harold reached adulthood. The inscription was accurate; the tree was incomplete.

> *Source: Find a Grave, Hillside Cemetery, Plainwell, Michigan — family listing on the memorial for [John Franklin Eesley](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/132028385/john-franklin-eesley) (memorial 5884027).*
