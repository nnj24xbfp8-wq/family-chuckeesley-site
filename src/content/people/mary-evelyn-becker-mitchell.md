Mary Evelyn (Becker) Mitchell was the **daughter of [Etha May (Fleming) Becker](/family/etha-may-fleming-becker/)** and thus a **granddaughter of [James Wesley Fleming](/family/wesley-fleming/)** &mdash; a grandniece of [Sadye Fleming Wildermuth](/family/sadye-fleming-wildermuth/), on the Fleming side of Chuck's maternal line. She later married into the **Mitchell** family and took the Mitchell name.

She appears as a **young girl of about eight** in the [c. late-1930s three-generation Fleming portrait](/archive/james-wesley-fleming-three-generations-c-late-1930s/), standing in front of her mother Etha at her great-grandfather Wesley's side. That apparent age is the only current anchor for her birth, placing it around **1929–1931**; a firmer date awaits family records.

> *Sources: family-labeled photograph transmitted by Chuck Eesley, 2026; [Etha May (Fleming) Becker](/family/etha-may-fleming-becker/) and [James Wesley Fleming](/family/wesley-fleming/) archive pages.*
