John **"Jack"** Anthony Murdock, Jr. was the son of **[Mary Louise (Davis) Murdock](/family/mary-louise-davis-murdock/)** &mdash; and thus a grandson of [Homer](/family/homer-davis/) and [Bessie Davis](/family/bessie-hill-davis/), and a grandnephew of Chuck's grandmother [Dorothy Davis Wildermuth](/family/dorothy-davis-wildermuth/).

Born in **1946**, he is the **infant in the [1947 five-generation Hill–Davis–Murdock portrait](/archive/hill-davis-murdock-five-generation-1947/)** &mdash; the youngest of the generations gathered in that frame, held in his mother's arms. He died in **1977**, around the age of thirty-one.

> *Source: 1947 five-generation photograph (family-labeled: "John 'Jack' Anthony Murdock, Jr. (1946–1977)"); [Eesley/Wildermuth family GEDCOM](/docs/dale-eesley-familysearch-tree/).*
