**Thomas Bartlett** is Chuck's maternal **6×-great-grandfather** on the Fleming line — the father of **[Nancy Bartlett](/family/nancy-bartlett/)**, who married **[Thomas Bailey](/family/thomas-bailey/)** in Monongalia County, Virginia, on 21 October 1803.

His wife appears in the record **only as "Sarah"** — no surname, no dates. Lewis Fleming's page notes the Baileys and Bartletts as "old Virginia families," but no further Bartlett detail has yet surfaced.

> *Source: the [Bailey Family Group Record](/archive/bailey-family-group-record-0019/), compiled by [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/), c. 1990.*
