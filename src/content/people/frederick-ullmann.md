Frederick Ullmann (1854&ndash;1940) was the father of **[Clara Victoria Ullmann](/family/clara-victoria-ullmann/)** &mdash; the wife of [Harvey Hendershot Hill](/family/harvey-hendershot-hill/) and mother of Chuck's great-grandmother [Bessie](/family/bessie-hill-davis/) &mdash; and of **[Ida Jeanetta "Nettie" Ullman](/family/ida-jeanetta-ullman-hill/)**. He is Chuck's **maternal great-great-great-grandfather** on the Ullmann line.

He was born **28 May 1854** and died **28 October 1940**. With his wife **[Elisabeth (Bode) Ullmann](/family/elisabeth-bode-ullmann/)** (1854&ndash;1930) he had **eight children**, per the family GEDCOM: Magdalena (1871), Minnie Louise (1879), Fredrick William (1881), Henry Edward (1883), [Clara Victoria](/family/clara-victoria-ullmann/) (1885), Laura Carolyn (1887), [Ida Jeanetta "Nettie"](/family/ida-jeanetta-ullman-hill/) (1892), and Bessie Edna (1894).

## The probable family group portrait

Frederick is **probably the bearded elder man seated at the center** of the [c. 1912 Ullmann family group portrait](/archive/ullmann-family-group-probable-c1912/), with Elisabeth beside him and their grown children around them. **This identification is a working guess, not yet confirmed** &mdash; the print carries no label &mdash; but the seated couple's ages fit Frederick and Elisabeth in the early 1910s, and Chuck's read is that it looks like Frederick.

> *Source: [Eesley/Wildermuth family GEDCOM](/docs/dale-eesley-familysearch-tree/) (June 2026 trace) for dates and the eight children; c. 1912 group photograph, identification proposed by Chuck Eesley (unconfirmed).*
