Anna Maria Werner was born **9 June 1739**, the daughter of **[Johann Georg Werner](/family/johann-georg-werner/)** and **[Anna Barbara Phillipp](/family/barbara-phillipp/)** — the young Großaspach mother who [died at thirty-one in 1742](/family/barbara-phillipp/), only three years after Anna Maria's birth. Anna Maria married **[Johann Georg Wolf](/family/johann-georg-wolf/)**, and their daughter **[Catharina Dorothea Wolf](/family/catharina-dorothea-wolf/)** (1766) married **[Adam Wildermuth](/family/adam-wildermuth/)** — the marriage that folds the **Werner–Phillipp** family into the direct Wildermuth line that reaches Chuck.
