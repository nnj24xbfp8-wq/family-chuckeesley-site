**Norma Jean (Wildermuth) Gault** was the **fourth** of [Earl Adam Wildermuth](/family/earl-a-wildermuth/) and [Sadye Irene (Fleming) Wildermuth](/family/sadye-fleming-wildermuth/)'s five children &mdash; born **14 September 1930 in Marietta, Washington County, Ohio**, eight years after her eldest sister [Ruth Irene](/family/ruth-irene-wildermuth-ridenour/), six years after her older brother [Robert Earl](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/) (Chuck's grandfather), and two years after her older brother [Carl Edward](/family/carl-edward-wildermuth/).

She lived through the Marietta-Wildermuth household's 1930s and 1940s arc &mdash; the Depression years, the [1937 flood that came through the family's 413 Harmar Street house](/places/413-harmar-street-marietta/), and the war years that took her brother Robert Earl to the South Pacific on B-24 missions.

She married **Algie Eugene "Gene" Gault** on **2 February 1951** in Marietta &mdash; Gene, born 8 August 1930 in Steubenville and a chemical worker, was the son of Donald M. Gault and Irene Bills. Norma had graduated **Marietta High School in the class of 1948**.

Norma and Gene settled in the Newark&ndash;Hebron area of Licking County and raised three children: **Jay (Denise) Gault** and **John W. Gault**, both of Newark, and **Ann (Michael) Fornataro**, also of Newark. By the time of her 2007 obituary she was a devoted member of the **Hebron United Methodist Church** and a Sunday-school teacher there, a 25-year member of **Eagon Chapter 123, Order of the Eastern Star**, a Past Mother Advisor for the Hebron Assembly of the **Order of Rainbow for Girls**, and a founding member and two-term president of the 19th Hole Club at Harbor Hills Country Club &mdash; as well as *"an avid Ohio State Buckeye fan."* She died **26 May 2007 at Heath, Ohio**, age 76, after an extended illness, survived by Gene and their two granddaughters, Brooke and Brayann; she was buried at **Fairmount Cemetery in Jacksontown**, Pastor Paul Hansen officiating. Her **brother Carl Edward had died in Licking County eighteen years earlier**.
