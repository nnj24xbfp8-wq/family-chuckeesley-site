**John Turner** is a name and a relationship.

He appears on one sheet of [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/)'s [printed ancestor charts](/archive/wildermuth-fleming-ancestor-chart-set/) — the one headed *Ancestors of Martha Turner* — in a single box above his daughter, with **no birth, no death, no place and no wife**. The chart for Martha is the shortest of the fourteen for exactly that reason: it runs one generation and stops.

His daughter **[Martha Turner](/family/martha-turner/)** was born in **Wood County** in March 1832 and married **[Thomas Bailey Fleming](/family/thomas-bailey-fleming/)** at Parkersburg in 1851, so the family was in Wood County, western Virginia, by the early 1830s at the latest.

**Where to look.** Wood County marriage bonds around 1851 would name Martha's father and often a bondsman kinsman; the 1840 and 1850 Wood County censuses would place the household. Neither has been searched.

> *Source: the [printed ancestor charts](/archive/wildermuth-fleming-ancestor-chart-set/), Robert Earl Wildermuth, c. early 1990s.*
