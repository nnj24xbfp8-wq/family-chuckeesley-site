**John D. Turner** is a name and a relationship.

He appears on one sheet of [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/)'s [printed ancestor charts](/archive/wildermuth-fleming-ancestor-chart-set/) — the one headed *Ancestors of Martha Turner* — in a single box above his daughter, with **no birth, no death, no place and no wife**. The chart for Martha is the shortest of the fourteen for exactly that reason: it runs one generation and stops.

## The initial, and the wife he could not name

The [1998 pedigree chart](/archive/pedigree-charts-1998/) adds the one thing anyone has: a **middle initial**. He is entered as **JOHN D. TURNER-0516**, with every other field blank.

Beside him, in the slot for Martha's mother, sits **"WIFE UNKNOWN-0518."** Robert Earl had assigned a record number to a woman whose name he never found — a placeholder held open in his database for years. It is a small, exact picture of where this branch stopped.

His daughter **[Martha Turner](/family/martha-turner/)** was born in **March 1832** and married **[Thomas Bailey Fleming](/family/thomas-bailey-fleming/)** at Parkersburg in 1851, so the family was in western Virginia by the early 1830s at the latest. *(Her birth county is itself in doubt — see her page: one chart says Wood, the 1998 chart says Harrison.)*

**Where to look.** Wood County marriage bonds around 1851 would name Martha's father and often a bondsman kinsman; the 1840 and 1850 Wood County censuses would place the household. Neither has been searched.

> *Sources: the [printed ancestor charts](/archive/wildermuth-fleming-ancestor-chart-set/), Robert Earl Wildermuth, c. early 1990s; the [1998 pedigree charts](/archive/pedigree-charts-1998/), 22 March 1998, for the middle initial and the "WIFE UNKNOWN" entry.*
