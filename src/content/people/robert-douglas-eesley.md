Robert Douglas Eesley is the second of [Donald Stuart "Don" Eesley](/family/don-eesley/) and [Margaret Adelyne "Peggy" Youman Eesley](/family/margaret-youman-eesley/)'s three children, and a younger brother of [Lyle Stuart Eesley](/family/lyle-stuart-eesley/) and older brother of [Marilyn Eesley (Elkins)](/family/marilyn-eesley-elkins/) — Chuck Eesley's first cousin once removed on the paternal Eesley side. Per Bean's 1985 register he married **Roxie Mucklerath**, and their children are **Amelia Eesley** (b. 31 July 1977) and **Douglas Stuart Eesley** (b. 5 November 1979).

Other details withheld — living.
