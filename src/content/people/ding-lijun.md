**丁丽珺**（小名珺珺）&mdash; [李勃](/family/bo-li/)之女、[尚耀真](/family/yaozhen-shang/)（周丽婕之外祖母）的外孙女。因李勃是[李恂](/family/xun-li/)的妹妹，故珺珺是[周丽婕](/family/lijie-zhou/)母系的表亲（表姊妹）。在[青岛约1988年的合影](/archive/lijie-grandparents-early-childhood-qingdao-c1980s/)中，被姨外婆[尚耀香](/family/yaoxiang-shang/)抱在怀里的婴儿即是珺珺（据李恂手记，抱她的是尚耀香，并非外婆尚耀真），身旁坐着的小女孩即[Lijie](/family/lijie-zhou/)。

![约1988年，婴儿丁丽珺（珺珺）被姨外婆尚耀香抱在怀里，左侧坐着幼时的Lijie，青岛。](../../assets/family/originals/shang-yaozhen-grandchildren-qingdao-c1985.jpeg)

*约1988年 &mdash; 婴儿[丁丽珺](/family/ding-lijun/)（珺珺）在姨外婆[尚耀香](/family/yaoxiang-shang/)怀中，左侧为幼时的[Lijie](/family/lijie-zhou/)。*在世；由李恂于2026年指认。

> *注：姓名由家族提供；名字中"丽"字系据拼音推测，"珺"字与小名相合，待家族核定。*

## English

Details withheld &mdash; living. Ding Lijun (childhood name **Junjun** 珺珺) is the child of [Bo Li](/family/bo-li/) (younger sister of [Li Xun](/family/xun-li/)) and a grandchild of [Shang Yaozhen](/family/yaozhen-shang/) (Lijie's maternal grandmother): in the [c. 1988 Qingdao photograph](/archive/lijie-grandparents-early-childhood-qingdao-c1980s/) she is the **baby held by her great-aunt [Shang Yaoxiang](/family/yaoxiang-shang/)** (per Li Xun's note, not her grandmother Yaozhen), with a young [Lijie](/family/lijie-zhou/) seated beside her. As Bo Li's child she is **Lijie's first cousin** on the maternal Li side.

> *Source: identified by Li Xun (Lijie's mother), 2026.*
