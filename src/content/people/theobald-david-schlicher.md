Theobald "David" Schlicher was the **Schlicher patriarch** of Marietta, Washington County, Ohio — a German-immigrant father whose family settled in the same mid-19th-century wave that brought the [Johann Michael Wildermuth](/family/johann-michael-wildermuth/) line to Washington County. The two families merged when his daughter Flora married into the Wildermuths.

Per Dale Eesley's GEDCOM (family record @F56@) he had **six children**: George (b. 8 November 1861), Daniel (b. 8 January 1864), David F (b. 21 February 1866), Edward (b. March 1869), **[Flora](/family/flora-schlicher-wildermuth/)** (b. 24 August 1870 — Robert Earl Wildermuth's paternal grandmother), and **[Emma](/family/emma-schlicher/)** (b. June 1874, the youngest). Only the two daughters, Flora and Emma, have pages in this archive so far; the four sons are named but not yet profiled.

## The hometown his grandson was still seeking

In his [1989 memoir](/docs/robert-earl-wildermuth-memoir/), Theobald's grandson [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/) closed his German-trip travelogue with the research he still hoped to finish:

> *"I hope to return to the home towns of grandma Catherina Boeshar and hopefully that of Johann Peter Schlicher at some later date."*

The Schlicher of that sentence is this family line. The relationship between the "Johann Peter Schlicher" Robert Earl names and Theobald "David" Schlicher — same man, father, or earlier ancestor — is open research.
