Jonas Borror was the husband of [Elsie Chenoweth Borror](/family/elsie-chenoweth-borror/) and the father of [Eugene Everette Borror](/family/eugene-borror/). He was born **25 May 1863 in Jackson Township, Franklin County, Ohio** &mdash; in the countryside southwest of Columbus &mdash; and married Elsie around **1899**.

He appears at center with Elsie, holding their toddler son Eugene, in the [c. 1901 Chenoweth family reunion photograph](/archive/chenoweth-family-reunion-c1901/).

Jonas lived an extraordinarily long life. He died **22 September 1961 in Franklin Township, Franklin County, Ohio, at ninety-eight** &mdash; a span from the middle of the **Civil War to the dawn of the space age**, lived almost entirely within a few townships of his Franklin County birthplace. He was buried at **Concord Cemetery in Grove City**, in the same county where he had been born nearly a century before.
