Jonas Borror was the husband of [Elsie Chenoweth Borror](/family/elsie-chenoweth-borror/) and the father of [Eugene Everette Borror](/family/eugene-borror/). He appears at center with Elsie, holding their toddler son, in the [c. 1901 Chenoweth family reunion photograph](/archive/chenoweth-family-reunion-c1901/).
