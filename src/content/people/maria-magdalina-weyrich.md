Maria Magdalina Weyrich was born in **1807** and married **[Johann Peter Schlicher](/family/johann-peter-schlicher/)** of **Adenbach**, in the Kusel district of the Rhineland-Palatinate. Their son **[Theobald "David" Schlicher](/family/theobald-david-schlicher/)** — born 21 February 1835, still in Germany — became the Schlicher patriarch of Marietta, Washington County, Ohio, and the father of [Flora Schlicher Wildermuth](/family/flora-schlicher-wildermuth/).

She reaches this archive through exactly one source: [Robert Earl Wildermuth's printed ancestor charts](/archive/wildermuth-fleming-ancestor-chart-set/) — his database output, not a document, and unverified against any register. Everything below is from that sheet and should be read as provisional.

## The birthplace that does not resolve

The chart gives her birthplace as **"Marth"**, with no region attached. That is not a village this archive can match to anything, and the charts were photographed at a size where short words are exactly what goes wrong. It is being recorded as written rather than guessed at — including the guess that it must be near her husband's Adenbach, which nothing on the chart supports. The likeliest explanations, in no particular order:

- a **truncation** of a longer name on the printed sheet
- a **misreading** of the photograph on this archive's part
- a name Robert Earl had himself taken down imperfectly from a correspondent

Worth resolving from a flatbed scan of the original before anyone goes looking in German registers for a place called Marth.

## Her parents

The chart carries one generation further back, to **Theobald Weyrich** and **Magdalina Burgey** — and it is presumably from her father that her **son's** name, **Theobald**, descends. An unusual name to carry into an Ohio river town, and it came from her side of the family, not the Schlichers'.

Neither Theobald Weyrich nor Magdalina Burgey is given a page here. They are names on a chart with no dates and no places, and this archive would rather hold them in prose than build pages that say nothing.

She is **Chuck's maternal four-times-great-grandmother**: Maria Magdalina → [Theobald David Schlicher](/family/theobald-david-schlicher/) → [Flora Schlicher Wildermuth](/family/flora-schlicher-wildermuth/) → [Earl Adam Wildermuth](/family/earl-a-wildermuth/) → [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/) → [Terrie](/family/terrie-lee-eesley/) → Chuck.

Whether she made the Atlantic crossing with her husband and son, or died in the Palatinate before they left, is unrecorded. Her husband died at Marietta in 1861; nothing places her there.

> *Source: the [fourteen printed ancestor charts](/archive/wildermuth-fleming-ancestor-chart-set/) from Robert Earl Wildermuth's research papers, c. early 1990s — the "Ancestors of Theobald David Schlicher" sheet. No other record of her is held in this archive.*
