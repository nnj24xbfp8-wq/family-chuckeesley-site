Elizabeth Davis is Chuck's maternal **five-times-great-grandmother** on the Davis line &mdash; wife of **[William Davis](/family/william-davis/)** (1764&ndash;1851) and mother of **[Joseph Davis](/family/joseph-davis/)** (1819&ndash;1896).

She was **born about 1781**. Her **maiden name has not yet been recovered** &mdash; the FamilySearch record carries her only under her married name, "Elizabeth Davis." Recovering her maiden family, her exact dates, and her origin is the open end of this, the deepest couple on the Davis branch.

Through Joseph she is an ancestor of [William Armstrong Davis](/family/william-armstrong-davis/), [Homer Edward Davis](/family/homer-davis/), [Dorothy Marie Davis Wildermuth](/family/dorothy-davis-wildermuth/), and Chuck.

> *Source: [Eesley/Wildermuth GEDCOM tree / FamilySearch](/docs/dale-eesley-familysearch-tree/) &mdash; FamilySearch ID **K4GK-7LX** (Elizabeth Davis, b. ca 1781), linked as the mother of Joseph Davis (GMTB-SJX). Surfaced from the FamilySearch pedigree view, July 2026.*
