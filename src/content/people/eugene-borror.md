Eugene Everette Borror was the first-born son of [Jonas Borror](/family/jonas-borror/) and [Elsie Chenoweth Borror](/family/elsie-chenoweth-borror/), born **1 September 1901**. He is the toddler held at the center of the [c. 1901 Chenoweth family reunion photograph](/archive/chenoweth-family-reunion-c1901/) &mdash; his birth date is what dates that photo to roughly 1901&ndash;1902.
