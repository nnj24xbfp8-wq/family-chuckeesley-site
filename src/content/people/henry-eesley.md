Henry Eesley is the subject of a **printed [50th-wedding-anniversary souvenir dated 2 November 1915](/docs/henry-eesley-50th-anniversary-souvenir-1915/)** — the sole document this archive holds on him. He and his wife, **Clemens (Wilkins) Eesley**, were married in **Blenheim Township, Oxford County, Ontario, Canada, on 2 November 1865**, and by their golden anniversary were living in **Midland, Michigan**, from which they sent their printed greetings *"to our children, grandchildren, brothers, sisters and other kinsfolk."*

## The eight children — a 1915 Midwestern diaspora

The souvenir lists eight children, one already deceased, the rest scattered across the Midwest and Rockies:

- **Edward Eesley** — died 11 December 1879
- **Tressa B. (Eesley) Maddock** — Detroit, Michigan
- **Harry H. Eesley** — Denver, Colorado
- **Mrs. John A. Dillon** — East Tawas, Michigan
- **William J. Eesley** — Steamboat Springs, Colorado
- **Arthur L. Eesley** — Winegar, Wisconsin
- **Mrs. Gus A. Winkler** — Midland, Michigan
- **Lewis E. Eesley** — Midland, Michigan

## How he connects — an open question

Henry's exact relationship to Chuck's documented [Albert Robert Eesley Sr](/family/albert-robert-eesley/) line **is not yet established.** The circumstantial case for a close kinship is strong:

- His **1865 marriage at Blenheim** is in the same **Oxford County, Ontario** region where the [Goldie family of Ayr](/family/jeanie-goldie/) lived — and it came just **four years after** [Albert Robert Eesley](/family/albert-robert-eesley/) and [Jennie Goldie](/family/jeanie-goldie/) married at Ayr in 1861.
- Both men followed the **Canada-to-U.S.-Midwest** path — Henry settling at Midland, Michigan; Albert Robert moving through Michigan mill towns before Toronto.

But Henry does **not** appear in the 1851 Stratford-on-Avon census of Albert Robert Sr's immediate family, so he was not a full brother in that household. A pencil annotation on the souvenir adds the one direct clue: *"Eesley cousin to MaryAnn Harris Cobb"* — placing this branch in some collateral relation via a **Harris-Cobb** connection. The likeliest readings are that Henry was a **cousin of Albert Robert Sr** from a common earlier Oxfordshire/Warwickshire Eesley generation, or a more distant Eesley from another branch of the Hanwell family. His placement here on the paternal Eesley side is provisional pending that reconciliation against Dale Eesley's FamilySearch tree.

> *Source: [Souvenir of the 50th Wedding Anniversary of Henry Eesley and Clemens Wilkins, 1915](/docs/henry-eesley-50th-anniversary-souvenir-1915/), transmitted by Chuck Eesley, 2026. The connection to the Albert Robert Sr line is an open research question.*
