Johann Christian Wildermuth was born **10 October 1794 in [Großaspach](/places/grossaspach/)**, in the Backnang district of Württemberg — the village his father [Adam Wildermuth](/family/adam-wildermuth/) had moved to from Rielingshausen and where Adam died in 1800, when Johann Christian was five.

The [Familienregister](/archive/wildermuth-familienregister-grossaspach/) gives him as **Bürger und Schuhmacher zu Großaspach** — citizen and shoemaker. It is the trade his son [Johann Michael](/family/johann-michael-wildermuth/) would carry across the Atlantic and work in Marietta, Ohio. The same entry gives his father Adam as **Bürger und Weber**, a weaver: three generations, three trades, as the family moved down the valley from Rielingshausen.

## The marriage — 1819, at Rietenau

He married **[Maria Margaretha Schmidgall](/family/maria-margaretha-schmidgall/)** of Röhrach on **25 April 1819** at **Rietenau**, a village a few kilometres from Aspach. She was five years his elder.

> *This page previously carried the marriage as **25 April 1842 at "Ristenau,"** six months before his death — and reasoned from it that Johann Michael, twelve years old in 1842, must be the child of an earlier unrecorded wife. The Familienregister settles it: the year is **1819**, the place **Rietenau**, and the marriage lasted twenty-three years. There was no first wife. The transposed digit had invented one.*

| 18 Aug 1822 | Carolina | died 17 Nov 1827, age five |
| 22 Jan 1825 | Johann Christian | schoolteacher in Switzerland; married at Kapperswyl, 1856 |
| 8 Jun 1828 | **Anna Maria** | *nach Amerika* |
| 23 Aug 1830 | **[Johann Michael](/family/johann-michael-wildermuth/)** | *1847 nach Amerika* |
| 11 Jun 1834 | Carolina | died 9 Jul 1834, four weeks old |

Three of the seven died small, and the name **Carolina** was used twice — given again in 1834 to a daughter born seven years after the first Carolina was buried, who then lived four weeks. Of the four who reached adulthood, one taught school in Switzerland, one married into a neighbouring village, and **two crossed to America**.

## The orphaning

**Maria Margaretha died 30 December 1841.** Johann Christian died **7 October 1842**, ten months later, at forty-eight.

Johann Michael was **twelve**. Five years after that he sailed for New York with the Rossars of Baden — and the reason he went at sixteen, with no parent to hold him, is written plainly in these two dates.
