Stella Anderson Eesley was the mother of [Wilbur "Will" Eesley](/family/wilbur-eesley/) and therefore the mother-in-law of [Peggy McMaster Eesley](/family/margaret-mcmaster-eesley/) &mdash; a paternal-side grandmother of [Charlie Eesley](/family/charles-eesley/). The family record carries a careful note from Peggy: she did not merely *like* her mother-in-law, she *respected* her, and the distinction is meaningful.

> *Sketch &mdash; drawn from the [Peggy-to-Terrie letter on Anne's Wisconsin hospitalization](/docs/letters/peggy-to-terrie-anne-wisconsin-hospitalization/); dates and parentage to be added as they surface.*
