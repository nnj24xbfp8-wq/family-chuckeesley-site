The deepest **Bailey** ancestor the record reaches, and Chuck's maternal **6×-great-grandfather** on the Fleming line. He appears in the Bailey Family Group Record only by initials — entered as **"I.B.Y. OR J.B.Y. Bailey"**, the compiler himself unsure which letter the source document showed. No dates or places are given.

He was the husband of **[Hannah Thornsberry](/family/hannah-thornsberry/)** and the father of **[Thomas Bailey](/family/thomas-bailey/)**, who married [Nancy Bartlett](/family/nancy-bartlett/) in Monongalia County in 1803.

> *A candidate for future research: the initials, and any first name behind them, are unresolved.*
>
> *Source: the [Bailey Family Group Record](/archive/bailey-family-group-record-0019/), compiled by [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/), c. 1990.*
