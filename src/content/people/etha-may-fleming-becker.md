Etha May (Fleming) Becker was the **younger sister of [Sadye Irene (Fleming) Wildermuth](/family/sadye-fleming-wildermuth/)** &mdash; Chuck's maternal great-grandmother &mdash; which makes Etha his **great-grandaunt on the Fleming side**. She was born in **1904 in Wood County, West Virginia**, one of the fourteen children of [James Wesley Fleming](/family/wesley-fleming/) and [Verona Belle (Dunbar) Fleming](/family/verona-sheppard-fleming/), and the **twin of Diatha Fleming**. Robert Earl Wildermuth found the birth records of Sadye's younger siblings, Etha among them, in the Wood County Court House at Parkersburg.

She married a **Becker** and settled in **Columbus, Ohio**. When [Sadye died in 1976](/family/sadye-fleming-wildermuth/), the obituary named just two surviving Fleming sisters &mdash; LaVerna (Fleming) Raison of Newark and **"Mrs. Etha (Fleming) Becker of Columbus"** &mdash; so Etha outlived most of her thirteen siblings.

## In the photographs

Etha stands at her father [James Wesley Fleming](/family/wesley-fleming/)'s right in the [c. late-1930s three-generation Fleming portrait](/archive/james-wesley-fleming-three-generations-c-late-1930s/), her young daughter **[Mary Evelyn](/family/mary-evelyn-becker-mitchell/)** in front of her &mdash; the aging patriarch flanked by two of his daughters and two granddaughters, a few years before his October 1940 death.
