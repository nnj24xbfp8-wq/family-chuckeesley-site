Marilyn (Eesley) Elkins is the youngest of [Donald Stuart "Don" Eesley](/family/don-eesley/) and [Margaret Adelyne "Peggy" Youman Eesley](/family/margaret-youman-eesley/)'s three children, and the younger sister of [Lyle Stuart Eesley](/family/lyle-stuart-eesley/) and [Robert Douglas Eesley](/family/robert-douglas-eesley/) — Chuck Eesley's first cousin once removed on the paternal Eesley side. Per Bean's 1985 register she married **James Elkins**, and their children are **Emily Elkins** (b. 10 February 1977) and **Peter Donald Elkins** (b. 24 March 1979).

Other details withheld — living.
