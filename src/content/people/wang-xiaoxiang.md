## English

Details withheld &mdash; presumed living. Wang Xiaoxiang is the **daughter of [Shang Lingke](/family/lingke-shang/)** (Yaofu Shang's eldest daughter) and a granddaughter of [Yaofu Shang](/family/yaofu-shang/) and [Yunhua Li](/family/yunhua-li/). Because Lingke is a cousin of [Li Xun](/family/xun-li/) (Lijie's mother), Wang Xiaoxiang is Li Xun's first cousin once removed and **Lijie's second cousin**. Born in 1972, she is the little girl held by [Bo Li](/family/bo-li/) in the [c. 1974–75 seaside photograph](/archive/shang-li-family-group-qingdao-c1970s/). Lijie established the connection via FamilySearch in 2026 (PXXV-66K).

> *Source: identified by Li Xun (Lijie's mother), 2026.*
