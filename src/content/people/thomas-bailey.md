**Thomas Bailey** was born in **Virginia** and married **[Nancy Bartlett](/family/nancy-bartlett/)** on **21 October 1803** in **Monongalia County, Virginia**. He is Chuck's maternal **5×-great-grandfather** on the Fleming line.

His daughter **[Synthia Bailey](/family/synthia-bailey/)** married **[Lewis B Fleming](/family/lewis-fleming/)** in 1827 — the marriage that carries this family into the Fleming, and then the Wildermuth, line. His first name descends through her eldest son, **[Thomas Bailey Fleming](/family/thomas-bailey-fleming/)** (b. 1830).

His own parents are recorded as **[J.B.Y. (or I.B.Y.) Bailey](/family/jby-bailey/)** and **[Hannah Thornsberry](/family/hannah-thornsberry/)** — the Thornsberry name reappearing in his eldest son, **Thornsberry Bailey Jr.** (b. August 1804).

## A probable brother, and a second Bartlett sister

The [Bartlett Family Group Record](/archive/bartlett-family-group-record-0094/) shows that Nancy was not the only Bartlett daughter to marry a Bailey. On **24 August 1806**, three years after Thomas and Nancy's wedding, her sister **Mary "Polly" Bartlett** married a **Thornsberry Bailey** in the Glades of Preston County.

That cannot be Thomas and Nancy's own son of the same name, who was two years old at the time — and Robert Earl Wildermuth assigned him a separate ID (0334) from the son's (0310), so he read them as two men as well. The natural explanation is that Mary married **Thomas's brother**: another son of [Hannah Thornsberry](/family/hannah-thornsberry/), carrying her maiden name as a given name, which would also be why Thomas's own boy was entered as *Junior*. The sheets do not state the relationship, so it stands as inference rather than record.

The family's recorded places — Monongalia, Harrison, Taylor, and Preston Counties — are all in the northern Virginia panhandle that became **West Virginia** in 1863.

> *Source: the [Bailey Family Group Record](/archive/bailey-family-group-record-0019/), compiled by [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/), c. 1990.*
