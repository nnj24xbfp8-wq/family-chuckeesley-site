Catherina Boeshar married into the Wildermuth line and was [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/)'s **paternal great-grandmother**. She lived until **1933** and, as the family memory records, was the only *"grandmother"* figure Robert Earl ever knew.
