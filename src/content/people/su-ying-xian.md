**Su Ying Xian**（姓名汉字待家族确认，1947年生）&mdash; 周氏姻亲 &mdash; [周兆帧](/family/zhaozheng-zhou/)（周丽婕的父系叔公，1938年生）之妻。其姓名在[戴尔·伊斯利的FamilySearch家谱](/docs/dale-eesley-familysearch-tree/)中以拼音记录；汉字写法及姓／名顺序尚待家族确认。

育有三女：[周燕](/family/yan-zhou/)与[周伟](/family/wei-zhou/)（1970年生的双胞胎）、[周亮](/family/liang-zhou/)（1976年生），皆生于丈夫被分配去的阳信。

她**毕业分配即在阳信县中医院**，与丈夫同为国家分配到此地的医科毕业生 &mdash; **二人是在阳信认识的**。丈夫1967年秋先分到翟王乡镇医院，**1974年**方调入她所在的县中医院；据其女周亮原话："我妈妈所在的阳信县中医院"。前七年，夫妇二人不在同一所医院。**1991年随夫调回青岛**，夫妇二人其后皆在**青岛市南区浮山所医院**工作 &mdash; 那一纸调令带回青岛的是两份医务工作，不是一份。

## English

Details withheld &mdash; living. Family member by marriage on the Zhou side &mdash; the wife of [Zhaozheng Zhou](/family/zhaozheng-zhou/), Lijie's paternal great-uncle. Her name is recorded in pinyin in [Dale Eesley's FamilySearch tree](/docs/dale-eesley-familysearch-tree/); the Chinese characters and the surname/given split are pending family confirmation.

She and Zhaozheng had **three daughters**, all born in **Yangxin County** during his posting there: **[Zhou Yan](/family/yan-zhou/)** and **[Zhou Wei](/family/wei-zhou/)**, twins born in 1970, and **[Zhou Liang](/family/liang-zhou/)**, born in 1976.

She was a medical professional in her own right, and not a local: she was **assigned to the Yangxin County Traditional Chinese Medicine Hospital on her own graduation**, the same system of state placement that had sent her husband there. **The two of them met in Yangxin.**

That makes this a marriage between two people the assignment system had deposited in the same remote county. She was already at the county hospital when he transferred in from the township hospital at Zhaiwang in **1974** — in her daughter's phrasing, *"我妈妈所在的阳信县中医院"*, the county hospital **where my mother was**. For the first seven years of his time in Yangxin they worked at different hospitals.

She came back to Qingdao with the family in **1991**, when the transfer her husband's nephew [Zhou Ling](/family/ling-zhou/) had spent years arranging finally came through. Both she and her husband then worked at the **Fushansuo Hospital in Shinan District** (青岛市南区浮山所医院) &mdash; a detail worth stating plainly, because it means the transfer moved **two medical careers** back to the city, not one. See [the dinner-table recording](/docs/zhou-ling-bringing-his-uncle-home/).

> *Source for the 1991 return, both hospitals, the 1974 transfer and the daughters' birth dates: [Zhou Liang](/family/liang-zhou/), by WeChat, 20 and 21 September 2026.*
