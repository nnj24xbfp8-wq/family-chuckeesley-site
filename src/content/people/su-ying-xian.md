**Su Ying Xian**（姓名汉字待家族确认，1947年生）&mdash; 周氏姻亲 &mdash; [周兆帧](/family/zhaozheng-zhou/)（周丽婕的父系叔公，1938年生）之妻。其姓名在[戴尔·伊斯利的FamilySearch家谱](/docs/dale-eesley-familysearch-tree/)中以拼音记录；汉字写法及姓／名顺序尚待家族确认。

育有三女：[周洁](/family/jie-zhou/)与[周义](/family/yi-zhou/)（1970年10月18日生的双胞胎）、[周善](/family/shan-zhou/)（1976年4月3日生），皆生于丈夫被分配去的阳信。

她本人即在**阳信县中医院**工作；丈夫1967年秋先分配到翟王乡镇医院，**1974年**方调入她所在的县中医院 &mdash; 据其女周善原话："我妈妈所在的阳信县中医院"。前七年，夫妇二人不在同一所医院。**1991年随夫调回青岛**，夫妇二人其后皆在**青岛市南区浮山所医院**工作 &mdash; 那一纸调令带回青岛的是两份医务工作，不是一份。

## English

Details withheld &mdash; living. Family member by marriage on the Zhou side &mdash; the wife of [Zhaozheng Zhou](/family/zhaozheng-zhou/), Lijie's paternal great-uncle. Her name is recorded in pinyin in [Dale Eesley's FamilySearch tree](/docs/dale-eesley-familysearch-tree/); the Chinese characters and the surname/given split are pending family confirmation.

She and Zhaozheng had **three daughters**, all born in **Yangxin County** during his posting there: **[Zhou Jie](/family/jie-zhou/)** and **[Zhou Yi](/family/yi-zhou/)**, twins born 18 October 1970, and **[Zhou Shan](/family/shan-zhou/)**, born 3 April 1976.

She was a medical worker in her own right, and the archive can now place her before her husband: she was already at the **Yangxin County Traditional Chinese Medicine Hospital** when he transferred in from the township hospital at Zhaiwang in **1974**. In her daughter's phrasing it is *"我妈妈所在的阳信县中医院"* — the county hospital **where my mother was**. For the first seven years of his time in Yangxin, in other words, they were at different hospitals.

She came back to Qingdao with the family in **1991**, when the transfer her husband's nephew [Zhou Ling](/family/ling-zhou/) had spent years arranging finally came through. Both she and her husband then worked at the **Fushansuo Hospital in Shinan District** (青岛市南区浮山所医院) &mdash; a detail worth stating plainly, because it means the transfer moved **two medical careers** back to the city, not one. See [the dinner-table recording](/docs/zhou-ling-bringing-his-uncle-home/).

> *Source for the 1991 return, both hospitals, the 1974 transfer and the daughters' birth dates: [Zhou Shan](/family/shan-zhou/), by WeChat, 20 and 21 September 2026.*
