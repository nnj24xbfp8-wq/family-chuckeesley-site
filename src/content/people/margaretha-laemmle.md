Margaretha Lämmle was born **7 March 1746 in Rielingshausen**, in Württemberg, and married [Andreus Wildermuth](/family/andreus-wildermuth/) on **19 July 1768** in the same village &mdash; a link in the deep eighteenth-century Wildermuth line that the family traces back through [Rielingshausen](/places/rielingshausen-church/).
