William Thomas "Bill" Bean was born **15 November 1909**. He married **[Mary Elizabeth Eesley](/family/mary-eesley-bean/)** (b. 25 August 1913, Shelby, Michigan) &mdash; the daughter of Charles Leonard and Lillie Dale Eesley who, in 1985, compiled the [Eesley Family History](/docs/eesley-family-history-1985/) that is the relationship backbone of the paternal line in this archive. The couple lived in **Narberth, Pennsylvania**, the address from which Mary wrote and mailed the register.

Bill stands behind his wife Mary in the late-1940s/early-1950s family group photo.

## Children

Per Mary's own register, the Beans' daughter was:

- **Margaret Louise Bean**, b. 11 December 1941; married **Jay Albert Kirkpatrick**, with three children &mdash; William Jay Kirkpatrick (1969), Mary Margaret Kirkpatrick (1974), and Laura Lynn Kirkpatrick (1978).

> *Source: Mary Eesley Bean, *[Eesley Family History](/docs/eesley-family-history-1985/)*, 1985 (descendant register and address appendix).*
