Johann Georg Wolf married **[Anna Maria Werner](/family/anna-maria-werner/)** (b. 1739) in the Aspach villages of Württemberg; their daughter **[Catharina Dorothea Wolf](/family/catharina-dorothea-wolf/)** (1766) married **[Adam Wildermuth](/family/adam-wildermuth/)**, carrying the Wolf and Werner–Phillipp lines into the Wildermuth descent. His own birth, death, and parentage are not yet recorded here.

> *Source: GEDCOM — Dale Eesley / FamilySearch (Johann Georg Wolf, LYTV-Y54).*

## The shoemaker the trade came from

Robert Earl's sketch of him carries five words the archive had missed: ***"Johann was an early German shoemaker."*** The [1988 Ahnentafel](/archive/ahnentafel-wildermuth-grossaspach-1988/), compiled in Germany from the Großaspach parish registers, gives the same thing in the original: ***Schuhmacher in Großaspach***.

That relocates the origin of the family's long shoemaking trade. It has been treated as a Wildermuth trade running from [Johann Christian](/family/johann-christian-wildermuth/) (b. 1794) to his emigrant son [Johann Michael](/family/johann-michael-wildermuth/), who worked at it in Marietta for fifty years. But Johann Christian's father [Adam](/family/adam-wildermuth/) was a **weaver**, and Adam's father [Andreas](/family/andreus-wildermuth/) a **vine-dresser**. The shoemaking comes in through Johann Georg — Johann Christian's **maternal grandfather**, by way of his daughter [Catharina Dorothea Wolf](/family/catharina-dorothea-wolf/).

Four generations of shoemakers, and the line runs **Wolf → Wildermuth**, not down the Wildermuth male line at all.

His own parents were **[Balthus Wolf](/family/balthus-wolf/)** (1710–1787) and **[Magdalena Ubele](/family/magdalena-ubele/)** (1717–1769), both of Großaspach. The Ahnentafel adds his death — **28 April 1816 at Großaspach** — which his sketch left as an age only, and gives his birthplace as **Rohrbach**.
