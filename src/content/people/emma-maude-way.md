Emma Maude Way was a daughter of [Edward E. Way](/family/edward-e-way/) and a younger sister of [Belsora "Belle" Way Wickens](/family/belsora-way-wickens/). Born in **1883**, she appears with her sisters &mdash; [Nora Angie](/family/nora-angie-way/) (b. 1876) and Victoria Anna (b. 1874) &mdash; in the family record around [Belle's 1901 wedding](/archive/belle-way-howard-wickens-wedding-1901/).
