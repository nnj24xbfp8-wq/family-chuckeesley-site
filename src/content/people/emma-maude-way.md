Emma Maude Way was the youngest of the Way sisters &mdash; born **3 July 1883 in Jackson Township, Noble County, Ohio**, a daughter of [Edward E. Way](/family/edward-e-way/) (1851&ndash;1924) and [Tacy Elizabeth Matthews](/family/tacy-elizabeth-matthews/) (1848&ndash;1902). She was a younger sister of [Belsora "Belle" Way Wickens](/family/belsora-way-wickens/) and of **[Victoria Anna Way Davis](/family/victoria-anna-way-davis/)** &mdash; Chuck's maternal great-great-grandmother &mdash; which makes Emma a **great-great-aunt of Chuck's** and an aunt of the toddler [Homer Edward Davis](/family/homer-davis/) in the family's [1901 wedding portrait](/archive/belle-way-howard-wickens-wedding-1901/). She stands among her sisters in that photograph; the numbered key labels her, from the vantage of a later hand, **"Emma Way Parker."**

## Marriage and the move west

On **24 December 1912** she married **Harry H. Parker**. Sometime after, the couple went **west to Wyoming** &mdash; and there Emma died young. She died **9 November 1917 at Lost Springs, Converse County, Wyoming**, at only **thirty-four**. Her body was brought back the length of the country to Ohio: she was buried in **1917 at Crooked Tree Cemetery, Noble County** &mdash; the Way family ground, beside her parents and the sisters she had stood with at Belle's wedding sixteen years before.
