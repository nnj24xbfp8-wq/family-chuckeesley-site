Maria Catharina Hoffmann was born **14 March 1781 in Frohnhofen**, in the **Kusel district of the Rhineland-Palatinate** (the **Pfalz**), and was christened there four days later. She married **[Johannes Jung](/family/johannes-jung/)** (1776–1844) of the same village, and they raised **ten children** in Frohnhofen — among them **[Maria Margaretha Jung](/family/maria-margaretha-jung/)** (b. 1814), the future mother of the emigrant [Catharina Boeshar](/family/catharina-boeshar-wildermuth/). She **died 16 April 1847 in Frohnhofen**, age 66, and was buried at Altenkirchen — outliving her husband by about two and a half years.

Her own parents in the tree are **Jakob Hoffman** and **Maria Elisabetha Böhnlein** — Chuck's six-times-great-grandparents, documented so far only by name.

She is **Chuck's five-times-great-grandmother** on the maternal Wildermuth side, through Margaretha → Catharina → [William Clifford Wildermuth](/family/william-wildermuth/) → Earl Adam → Robert Earl → Terrie → Chuck.
