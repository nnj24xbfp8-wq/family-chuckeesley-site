Edwin William Burnes was the husband of **[Helen Louise "Little Helen" (Eesley) Burnes](/family/helen-burnes/)** — the youngest of [Charles Leonard](/family/charles-leonard-eesley/) and [Lillie Dale Chenoweth Eesley](/family/lillie-dale-chenoweth/)'s eight children — which makes him Chuck's **great-uncle by marriage** on the paternal Eesley side.

He was born **27 April 1924 in Newaygo, Michigan**, married Helen in **1946**, and the couple made their home in **Worthington, Ohio**. He died in **2007 in Bushnell, Florida**.

## His children

Edwin and Helen had **seven children — a son and six daughters**:

- **[Brian Carlson Burnes](/family/brian-burnes/)** (b. 1948) — the eldest and only son
- **[Barb (Burnes) Hester](/family/barb-burnes-hester/)** — the oldest daughter, keeper of the photocopied set of Charles Leonard's 1940s letters
- **[Shelley Burnes](/family/shelley-burnes/)**
- **[Cindy Burnes](/family/cindy-burnes/)**
- **[Liz Burnes](/family/liz-burnes/)**
- **[Roberta Burnes](/family/roberta-burnes/)** — keeper of the 1940s Eesley letter collection and one of the archive's principal paternal-side sources
- **[Katie (Burnes) McKeel](/family/katie-burnes/)**

Through his daughter Roberta especially, the Burnes household became one of the main conduits by which the Charles Leonard Eesley documentary record reached this archive.
