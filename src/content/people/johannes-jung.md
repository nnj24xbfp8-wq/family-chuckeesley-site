Johannes Jung was born **13 January 1776 in Frohnhofen** — the same Kusel-district village in the **Pfalz** his descendants came from — and was **christened the following day, 14 January 1776, at the Altenkirchen parish church** (Bezirksamt Homburg, in what was then the Kingdom of Bavaria). He lived his whole life in Frohnhofen and **died there on 3 October 1844**, age 68, buried two days later at Altenkirchen.

He married **[Maria Catharina Hoffmann](/family/maria-catharina-hoffmann/)** (1781–1847), also of Frohnhofen, and they had **ten children**. Among them was **[Maria Margaretha Jung](/family/maria-margaretha-jung/)** (b. 1814), who married the Breitenbach miner [Christian Boeshar](/family/christian-boeshar/) and became the mother of the emigrant [Catharina Boeshar](/family/catharina-boeshar-wildermuth/) — Chuck's great-great-great-grandmother.

His own parents in the tree are **Theobald Jung** and **Maria Elisabetha Becker** — Chuck's six-times-great-grandparents, documented so far only by name.

He is **Chuck's five-times-great-grandfather** on the maternal Wildermuth side, through Margaretha → Catharina → [William Clifford Wildermuth](/family/william-wildermuth/) → Earl Adam → Robert Earl → Terrie → Chuck. He is one of the deepest documented ancestors on the whole maternal line.
