Maria Margaretha Jung was born **27 February 1814 in Frohnhofen**, a village a few miles from Breitenbach in the **Kusel district of the Rhineland-Palatinate** (the **Pfalz**). She married the Breitenbach miner **[Christian Boeshar](/family/christian-boeshar/)** on **8 December 1836**, and they had two children — **Jacob** (b. 1837) and **[Catharina](/family/catharina-boeshar-wildermuth/)** (b. 1840).

## Widowed young

When Christian **died in May 1843**, Margaretha was about **29**, left with a five-year-old and a not-yet-three-year-old in a Pfalz mining village. Her own later life — whether she remarried, whether she stayed in Breitenbach, and whether she was still living when her daughter Catharina left for America — is **not recorded in the family tree**, and her death date is unknown. She is one of the deepest-reaching names on the maternal Wildermuth line, and the point at which the documentary trail on this branch currently thins to a single vital record.

Her name survives in this archive largely because of one document: the **German-script birth certificate** that [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/) tracked down and had translated for his [*"Little Histories"*](/docs/robert-earl-wildermuth-little-histories/) — it named both Christian Boeshar's trade and **"Margaretha Jung"** as Catharina's mother.

Her own parents in the tree are **Johannes Jung** and **Maria Catharina Hoffmann** — Chuck's five-times-great-grandparents, documented so far only by name.

She is **Chuck's four-times-great-grandmother** on the maternal Wildermuth side, through Catharina → [William Clifford Wildermuth](/family/william-wildermuth/) → Earl Adam → Robert Earl → Terrie → Chuck.

> *Source: [Dale Eesley / FamilySearch — the Boeshar family record](https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/KLPR-G45); the German birth certificate reproduced in [Robert Earl Wildermuth's "Little Histories"](/docs/robert-earl-wildermuth-little-histories/).*
