Adam Archboe Fleming was born in **1849 in Doddridge County**, then still part of Virginia (West Virginia statehood was fourteen years away). Per a comment recorded in the [Wildermuth family book](/docs/wildermuth-family-of-marietta-book/), he was a son of **James and Frances Fleming** — the **fourth of five children and the second son**, his siblings in birth order being Emily, John, Rosetta, and Benjamin F. His unusual middle name **"Archboe"** may be a misspelling honoring a grandfather or great-uncle, **Archibald Fleming**, or his father's business partner, **Samuel Archbold**.

## The boy soldier of the 4th West Virginia Cavalry

The heart of Adam's record is his Civil War adolescence. When Virginia passed its **ordinance of secession on 17 April 1861**, the counties west of the Alleghenies — Doddridge foremost among them — rose against it, sending delegates to the **Wheeling Convention** and enrolling their sons as **soldiers for the Union**. The family-book narrative continues:

> *"And so it was, that young Adam, at the tender age of 13 or 14, joined the 4th Regiment West Virginia Cavalry along with cousins Matthew, Aaron, and A. H. Fleming, and neighbors Ephraim Bee and the Dotson brothers. Adam fudged his age as 18 and was mustered in August 19, 1863."*

He was mustered into the **4th West Virginia Cavalry on 19 August 1863** — a thirteen- or fourteen-year-old passing for eighteen — and served in the theater around Stonewall Jackson's operations in the region. (The surviving book excerpt breaks off mid-sentence at this point; the fuller account of his service is still to be transcribed.)

## Marriages and children

Adam married three times, per the book's Fleming section:

- **First marriage** (wife not named in the excerpt) — two daughters: **Alice May Fleming** (b. 11 November 1874) and **Frances LueCena Fleming** (b. 10 February 1876, m. Thomas Jefferson Hicks on 11 September 1892, d. 28 October 1940). The 1880 Doddridge County census records "A. A. Fleming," age 31, a laborer, with wife "M. E." and these two daughters.
- **Mary Ellen Smith** — married 9 March 1879 (Doddridge County marriages, per Wes Cochran, p. 80); she was born ca. 1862 in Upshur County, daughter of Johnson and Elizabeth Smith, and died in April 1898. Their children: **James W.**, **Dora Elizabeth**, **Johnson R.**, **Ruehamy** (b. 29 August 1886), and **Lucretia Oley Fleming** (b. 25 June 1892).
- **Minna Chennault** — married 8 June 1899; one child, **Martha Ellen Fleming** (b. 8 June 1900).

He **died 14 May 1902**, recorded in the family book as Lewis County, Tennessee.

## How he connects — an open question

Adam appears in the Fleming section of the [Wildermuth family book](/docs/wildermuth-family-of-marietta-book/) that [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/) compiled with Benjamin F. Bain — which places him within the family's orbit. But his **exact link to [Sadye Irene Fleming](/family/sadye-fleming-wildermuth/)'s direct line is not yet established.** Sadye's father, [James Wesley Fleming](/family/wesley-fleming/) (b. 1855), was a son of **Thomas Bailey Fleming and Martha Turner** per the GEDCOM — a different Fleming couple from Adam's parents, James and Frances. The two men were near-contemporaries in the same county, most plausibly **cousins**, but no documented link between the two Fleming households has yet surfaced in this archive. His placement here on the maternal Fleming side is provisional pending that connection.
