William Eesley was a son of [John Eesley](/family/john-eesley-1800/) and [Susannah Bubb](/family/susan-babbs/), born **1834** in Warwickshire &mdash; older brother of [Albert Robert Eesley](/family/albert-robert-eesley/) (b. 1837, the Canadian emigrant whose line runs through Chuck) and of [George Bubb Eesley](/family/george-bubb-eesley/) (b. 1836, the U.S.S. Constitution chaplain).

## The Australia migration

[Mary Eesley Bean's 1985 family history](/docs/eesley-family-history-1985/) preserves the one story this archive has about him:

> *William Eesley b. ___. Migrated to Australia at LaSalle near Melbourne. He was in the shoe business. No record of his family.*

The **LaSalle near Melbourne** reference is to one of the working-class districts of the Victorian gold-rush boomtown that Melbourne had become in the 1850s &mdash; possibly **La Salle** or a phonetically close place name in greater Melbourne. The shoe-making and shoe-repair trades were among the immigrant working-class trades the gold-rush economy created and sustained.

For William to have arrived in Australia would have meant a long voyage by sail or early-steam from England in the 1850s or 1860s &mdash; possibly as part of the wave of immigrants the **Victorian gold rush (1851 onward)** drew from England, Scotland, and Ireland. By the time Bean was writing in 1985, no record of his descendants had reached the family.

## The three-direction Eesley diaspora

William's Australia migration sits alongside two other Eesley emigrations in the same generation:

- **[Albert Robert Eesley](/family/albert-robert-eesley/) to Canada** &mdash; the line that runs through Charles Leonard, Will, Charlie, and Chuck.
- **The Eesley line to Elizabeth, New Jersey and Newark in 1858** &mdash; documented through the 1858 farm-and-bakery arrival and the [Newark NJ census record](/family/susan-babbs/), including their brother [George Bubb Eesley](/family/george-bubb-eesley/) and probably their sister Hannah and a younger John.

The Old Stratford / Charlecote household of John and Susannah Bubb scattered its sons across **three continents** in a single generation &mdash; Australia, Canada, and the United States. The Australia branch is the one whose descendants this archive has not traced.

## Open

- **Australian shipping records** &mdash; the British and Australian National Archives both hold the passenger lists for the 1850s/60s emigration. A William Eesley on a Melbourne-bound vessel would be findable.
- **Victorian shoemaker directories** &mdash; the Melbourne city directories of the 1860s-1890s and the Victoria state directories would record a William Eesley in the shoe trade.
- **Any surviving descendants** &mdash; the Australian Eesleys (if any) would be the cousins-many-times-removed of the Canadian Eesleys, the Newark NJ Eesleys, and Chuck. None have surfaced.

> *Sources: [Mary Eesley Bean, *Eesley Family History*, 1985](/docs/eesley-family-history-1985/) (page 1a); migration context from gold-rush-era Victoria.*
