Jane Armstrong is Chuck's maternal **three-times-great-grandmother** and the source of the **"Armstrong"** in her son [William Armstrong Davis](/family/william-armstrong-davis/)'s name &mdash; the kind of maiden-name-as-middle-name that keeps a mother's family visible a generation on.

She was born in **1816 in Jefferson County, Ohio**, and married **[Joseph Davis](/family/joseph-davis/)** there on **26 May 1842**. She died **6 November 1881 in Washington County, Ohio**, and was buried at **Beverly Cemetery**, Beverly &mdash; the same Muskingum-riverfront burying ground where her husband would be laid fifteen years later.

## The Scottish thread

The GEDCOM records Jane's mother as **Jannet McCalmont**, born **14 April 1784 in New Luce, Wigtownshire, Scotland** (d. 1869) &mdash; a **Scottish immigrant** whose family crossed to the upper Ohio Valley. She is, so far, the deepest and one of the few non-German, non-English immigrant threads on Chuck's maternal side, and a lead worth confirming against the [Wildermuth &amp; Bain book's Davis section](/docs/wildermuth-family-of-marietta-book/). Jane's father is not yet recorded.
