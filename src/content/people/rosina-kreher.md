Rosina Kreher was born **2 June 1721 in Kirchberg an der Murr**, a village in the Marbach district of Württemberg a few miles from the Wildermuths' [Rielingshausen](/places/rielingshausen-church/) &mdash; the daughter of **Michael Kreher**. She married **[Johann Adam Wildermuth](/family/johann-adam-wildermuth-1720/)** (1720&ndash;1768) and raised a large family in Rielingshausen; the tree records seven children, among them **[Andreus (Andreas Adam) Wildermuth](/family/andreus-wildermuth/)** (b. ~1745), the *Gartner* (vineyard gardener), along with several Johann-named sons and a daughter, Anna Barbara.

She is **Chuck's six-times-great-grandmother** on the maternal Wildermuth line.

Page is a stub. Her death date and her mother's name are pending the next layer of Rielingshausen-area parish-record research.
