**Hannah Thornsberry** is Chuck's maternal **6×-great-grandmother** on the Fleming line — wife of **[J.B.Y. Bailey](/family/jby-bailey/)** and mother of **[Thomas Bailey](/family/thomas-bailey/)**.

Her surname did not disappear when she married into the Baileys: her grandson, Thomas's eldest son, was named **Thornsberry Bailey Jr.** (b. August 1804, d. 25 May 1857 in Preston County) — the same pattern of carrying a mother's family name forward as a given name that appears one generation later in **[Thomas Bailey Fleming](/family/thomas-bailey-fleming/)**. Twice in this line, a woman's surname survives as a man's first name.

No dates or places are recorded for her.

> *Source: the [Bailey Family Group Record](/archive/bailey-family-group-record-0019/), compiled by [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/), c. 1990.*
