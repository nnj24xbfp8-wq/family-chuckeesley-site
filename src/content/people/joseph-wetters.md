Joseph "Joe" Wetters was [Anne Eesley](/family/anne-eesley/)'s chosen-family partner. His daughter Stephanie &mdash; "Stephie" &mdash; appears in the family correspondence (she was the niece who, the family noted, had taken to calling Terrie "Aunt Terrie").

> *Sketch &mdash; drawn from the [Peggy-to-Terrie letter on Anne's Wisconsin hospitalization](/docs/letters/peggy-to-terrie-anne-wisconsin-hospitalization/); more to come as it surfaces.*
