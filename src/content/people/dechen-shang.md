**尚德臣**（姓名汉字已由家族确认）&mdash; 周丽婕的外曾祖父，尚氏一脉。[谭春娥](/family/chune-tan/)之夫，育有四子女：[尚耀真](/family/yaozhen-shang/)（周丽婕之外祖母）、[尚耀香](/family/yaoxiang-shang/)、[尚耀福](/family/yaofu-shang/)、[尚耀礼](/family/yaoli-shang/) &mdash; 子女名字中的"耀"字很可能是尚氏字辈诗中的本代用字。其中两位子女与李家结亲：女儿[尚耀真](/family/yaozhen-shang/)嫁[李仲初](/family/zhongchu-li/)，子[尚耀福](/family/yaofu-shang/)娶[李蕴华](/family/yunhua-li/)（仲初之姐）&mdash; 尚、李两家由此在同代结成[双重姻亲](/family/yaofu-shang/)。

> *注：人名汉字已由家族确认。*

## English

DeChen Shang is the father of the four Shang siblings born in early-20th-century Qingdao — [Yaozhen](/family/yaozhen-shang/) (Lijie's grandmother), [Yaoxiang](/family/yaoxiang-shang/), [Yaofu](/family/yaofu-shang/), and [Yaoli](/family/yaoli-shang/). Two of them married into the Li family in the same generation: his daughter [Yaozhen](/family/yaozhen-shang/) married [Zhongchu Li](/family/zhongchu-li/), and his son [Yaofu](/family/yaofu-shang/) married Zhongchu's sister [Yunhua Li](/family/yunhua-li/) — the [Li–Shang double marriage](/family/yaofu-shang/). His own birth and death are not yet recorded in this archive. See [The Qingdao Families — Zhou, Li, and Shang](/docs/qingdao-families-zhou-li-shang/) for how the three families fit together.
