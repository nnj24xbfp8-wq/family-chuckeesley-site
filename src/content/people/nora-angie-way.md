Nora Angie Way was one of the older Way sisters &mdash; born **23 February 1876 in Noble County, Ohio**, a daughter of [Edward E. Way](/family/edward-e-way/) (1851&ndash;1924) and [Tacy Elizabeth Matthews](/family/tacy-elizabeth-matthews/) (1848&ndash;1902). She was a sister of [Belsora "Belle" Way Wickens](/family/belsora-way-wickens/) and of **[Victoria Anna Way Davis](/family/victoria-anna-way-davis/)** &mdash; Chuck's maternal great-great-grandmother &mdash; making Nora a **great-great-aunt of Chuck's** and an aunt of the one-year-old [Homer Edward Davis](/family/homer-davis/) in the family's [1901 wedding portrait](/archive/belle-way-howard-wickens-wedding-1901/), where the numbered key lists her first, as **"Nora Way Hutchens."**

On **19 June 1900**, the year before that wedding, she had married **John Ernest Hutchins**. Unlike her sister [Emma](/family/emma-maude-way/), who went west and died young in Wyoming, Nora stayed in the country she was born in. She died **30 December 1939 in Noble County, Ohio**, at sixty-three, and was buried at **Crooked Tree Cemetery** &mdash; the same Way family ground that holds her parents and sisters, a life lived nearly whole within a single Ohio county.
