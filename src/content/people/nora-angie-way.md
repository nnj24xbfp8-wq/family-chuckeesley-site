Nora Angie Way was a daughter of [Edward E. Way](/family/edward-e-way/) and one of the older sisters of [Belsora "Belle" Way Wickens](/family/belsora-way-wickens/). Born in **1876**, she appears among her sisters &mdash; Victoria Anna (b. 1874) and [Emma Maude](/family/emma-maude-way/) (b. 1883) &mdash; in the family record around [Belle's 1901 wedding](/archive/belle-way-howard-wickens-wedding-1901/).

> *Sketch &mdash; drawn from the [Belsora Way Wickens](/family/belsora-way-wickens/) entry and the Way family material; more to come as it surfaces.*
