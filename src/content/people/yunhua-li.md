## English

Yunhua was an elder sibling on the Li side at this generation, an elder sister of [Zhongchu](/family/zhongchu-li/) (Lijie's grandfather). Her **birth date is incorrect and pending correction** (the tree records 30 June 1911). She married **[Yaofu Shang](/family/yaofu-shang/)** &mdash; brother of [Yaozhen Shang](/family/yaozhen-shang/) (Lijie's grandmother) &mdash; making her also Lijie's great-aunt by marriage on the Shang side. This is the [Shang-Li double intermarriage](/family/yaofu-shang/) at the great-aunt/uncle generation.
