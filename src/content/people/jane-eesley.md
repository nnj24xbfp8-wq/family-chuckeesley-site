Jane Elda Eesley was the daughter of **[George Edburn "Bus" Eesley](/family/george-e-eesley/)** and **[Edla "Eddie" (Scaife) Eesley](/family/edla-scaife-eesley/)**, and the younger sister of Richard and **[Daniel Edburn "Dan" Eesley](/family/daniel-edburn-eesley/)**. She was born **21 March 1938 in Buffalo, New York**, where her father was working in the wartime-aircraft industry; she was four when he died in 1942.

## Killed at twelve — Lakewood, June 1950

On a Thursday afternoon in June 1950, Jane was **killed at twelve** when the bicycle she was riding swerved into the side of a truck at Lakewood, Ohio. Her Fremont hometown paper carried the news two days later, on 16 June 1950, under the headline *"Eesley Child Mishap Victim."* It records that she *"was the first girl to captain the safety patrol at Lincoln elementary school, Lakewood, which she attended"* — a small, aching detail given how she died.

She was survived by her mother, Edla Eesley, of Rosalia Avenue in Lakewood, and her brother Richard; her maternal grandparents Mr. and Mrs. Arthur Scaife of Florida; her aunt Bess Eesley and grandmother Annie Eesley of Fremont; her aunt Mrs. Jack Cassells of Toronto; and her uncle Albert Eesley of Youngstown. Her funeral was held at the Lakewood Methodist Church.

Jane is the *"died tragically in her youth"* named, without detail, in her brother [Dan's 2018 obituary](/docs/daniel-edburn-eesley-obituary-2018/) &mdash; the loss that had shadowed the family for nearly seventy years. She is **Chuck's second cousin once removed** on the paternal Eesley side.

> *Source: "Eesley Child Mishap Victim," Fremont News-Messenger, 16 June 1950 (family papers, transmitted by Chuck Eesley 2026); [Daniel Edburn Eesley obituary, 2018](/docs/daniel-edburn-eesley-obituary-2018/).*
