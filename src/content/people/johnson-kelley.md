Johnson B. Kelley was the father of [Martha Kelley](/family/martha-kelley/) and the eldest son of [Ebenezer Kelley](/family/ebenezer-kelley/) and Rachel Johnson. He was born **25 July 1810 in Randolph County, Virginia** &mdash; in the mountain country that would become **West Virginia** in 1863.

On **17 January 1830**, not yet twenty, he married **Sarah Yoak** in Randolph County. Together they had a large family &mdash; the GEDCOM records ten children, among them **[Martha Kelley](/family/martha-kelley/)** along with Deborah Ann, John Wesley, Melvina, Amanda, Almira, Isaiah, Hester Ann, Alta California, and Rebecca. (He later married a second time, to Nancy A. Stevens.) The daughter named **Alta California** is a small marker of the era's westward pull &mdash; a mid-century Appalachian family naming a child for the far Pacific coast.

He lived his whole life in the western-Virginia / West Virginia highlands, dying **24 December 1886 &mdash; Christmas Eve &mdash; in Taylor County, West Virginia**, at seventy-six. Through his daughter Martha, this branch of the Kelley line runs forward into the family's later Appalachian and Ohio generations.
