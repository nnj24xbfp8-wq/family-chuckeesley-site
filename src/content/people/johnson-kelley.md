Johnson B. Kelley was the father of [Martha Kelley](/family/martha-kelley/) and the son of [Ebenezer Kelley](/family/ebenezer-kelley/). Born **25 July 1810 in Randolph County, Virginia** &mdash; in the part of the state that would become West Virginia in 1863 &mdash; he carried the Kelley line into Appalachia.
