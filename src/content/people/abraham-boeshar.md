Abraham Boeshar is, so far, **a name without a record**. In the family tree he is the father of **[Christian Boeshar](/family/christian-boeshar/)** (1810–1843), the Breitenbach miner, and the husband of **[Maria Lehman](/family/maria-lehman/)** — but no birth, death, or place is recorded for him. Given his son Christian was born in Breitenbach in 1810, Abraham was presumably a Breitenbach-area man of the late 18th century, in the same **Kusel district of the Rhineland-Palatinate** (the **Pfalz**) the rest of this branch comes from.

He is **Chuck's five-times-great-grandfather** on the maternal Wildermuth side, through Christian → [Catharina Boeshar](/family/catharina-boeshar-wildermuth/) → [William Clifford Wildermuth](/family/william-wildermuth/) → Earl Adam → Robert Earl → Terrie → Chuck. Recovering his dates from the Breitenbach parish registers would be the way to give this name a life.
