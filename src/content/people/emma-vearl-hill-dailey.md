Emma Vearl (Hill) Dailey was the **second of the four Hill children** and a younger sister of **[Bessie Marie (Hill) Davis](/family/bessie-hill-davis/)** (1905&ndash;1950), Chuck's great-grandmother &mdash; making her **Chuck's great-grandaunt on the maternal Hill side**.

She was born **25 March 1907 at Rinard Mills**, in Washington Township, Monroe County, Ohio, a daughter of **[Harvey Hendershot Hill](/family/harvey-hendershot-hill/)** (1882&ndash;1951) and **[Clara Victoria (Ullmann) Hill](/family/clara-victoria-ullmann/)**. Her siblings were [Bessie](/family/bessie-hill-davis/) (1905), Theodore Roosevelt Hill (1912), and [Louella Virginia (Hill) Beardmore](/family/louella-hill-beardmore/) (1917). She married **Everett Fulton Dailey** on **3 June 1950**, and died **4 December 1989 at Marietta**, age 82 &mdash; back across the county line from the Monroe County hill country where the Hills began.

> *Source: [Eesley/Wildermuth GEDCOM tree](/docs/dale-eesley-familysearch-tree/), which records the four Hill children of Harvey Hendershot Hill and Clara Victoria Ullmann.*
