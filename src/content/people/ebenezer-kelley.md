Ebenezer Kelley was the grandfather of [Martha Kelley](/family/martha-kelley/) and the father of [Johnson B. Kelley](/family/johnson-kelley/). Born **2 October 1763 in Somerset County, New Jersey**, he is part of the New-Jersey-to-Appalachia migration that carried the family into what would become West Virginia.
