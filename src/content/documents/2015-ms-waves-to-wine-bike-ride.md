## The ride

The 2015 MS Society **[Waves to Wine](https://www.nationalmssociety.org/get-involved/events-and-fundraisers)** &mdash; the two-day Bay Area ride from San Francisco through Sonoma wine country. This is Chuck and Lijie's **second** documented MS ride in the archive, in the same year [Charlie Eesley](/family/charles-eesley/)'s death from diabetes complications would set the [ADA Tour de Cure](https://diabetes.org/tour) anchor in motion.

## Why MS, when no family member has it

Chuck and Lijie's path into the MS Society's annual ride came not from family loss but from friendship. Their friend **Paul Twohey** got Chuck started on the Waves to Wine ride the first year. **Across the years of riding, Chuck and Lijie met many people living with MS** &mdash; the disease's broad reach made itself visible through the riding community itself &mdash; and the cause stayed with them on its own merits.

The MS rides are therefore the **non-family-anchored half of the bike-rides corpus**: a practice that started as a friend's invitation and grew into its own multi-year commitment. The ADA Tour de Cure rides ([Charlie](/family/charles-eesley/) anchor) and the MS Society Waves to Wine rides (Paul Twohey + friendship-and-community anchor) run as the two parallel threads of the [annual cycling practice](/docs/charity-bike-rides/).
