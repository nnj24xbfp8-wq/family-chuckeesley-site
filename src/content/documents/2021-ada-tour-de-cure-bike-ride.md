## Five years in

The 2021 ride sits at roughly the **five-year mark** of the post-Charlie ADA Tour de Cure practice that began c. 2016, the year after [Charlie Eesley](/family/charles-eesley/)'s November 2015 death from diabetes complications. Five years of an annual practice through a global pandemic is the point at which the response to a single loss has become a steady-state arrangement &mdash; the [Family Threads #7 (Showing up)](/docs/family-threads/) pattern's whole point.
