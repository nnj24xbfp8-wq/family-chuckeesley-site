## How the MS rides began — Paul Twohey

Chuck and Lijie do not have an immediate family member with MS &mdash; **the path into the MS ride was friendship.** Their friend **Paul Twohey** got Chuck started on the Waves to Wine ride in its first year, and Chuck and Lijie have continued ever since. Across the years of riding they have met many people living with multiple sclerosis, and the cause has stayed with them on its own terms.

This is one of the distinguishing pieces of the [bike-rides corpus](/docs/charity-bike-rides/): the **ADA Tour de Cure** anchor is in family (Chuck's father [Charlie](/family/charles-eesley/) died of diabetes complications in 2015) but the **MS rides anchor is in friendship and community** &mdash; an extension of the showing-up practice outward beyond the family network. Both threads run through the same annual cycling life.
