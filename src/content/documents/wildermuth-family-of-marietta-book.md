**"Wildermuth Family of Marietta, Ohio"** is the book-length genealogy that [Robert Earl Wildermuth](/family/robert-earl-wildermuth/) — the family's own retired genealogist — compiled with **Benjamin F. Bain II** between **19 October 2005 and 31 March 2009**. Where his [memoir](/docs/robert-earl-wildermuth-memoir/) and [combat log](/docs/the-big-one/) are first-person narrative, this volume is the systematic descendant-and-associated-family record on the maternal Wildermuth side.

Its title page lists the **major associated families** it traces alongside the Wildermuths:

> Bailey · Bartlett · Dunbar · Dotson · Fleming · Gain · Kelley · Schlicher · Seyler

and opens with the epigraph the family motto could be built on:

> *"Can one tell where he's going until he knows where he's been?"*

The book interweaves **census abstracts, county marriage records, and transcribed family letters** — so it is both a genealogy and a small letter archive in its own right. Pages surfaced so far include the **Fleming section** (with the [Adam Archboe Fleming](/family/adam-archboe-fleming/) Civil War account) and transcriptions of family letters — among them a [letter from Peggy Eesley to Charles and Terrie](/docs/letters/peggy-to-charles-and-terrie-wedding-aftermath/), [Chuck's 2007 letter to his mother](/docs/letters/chuck-to-terrie-2007-12-28/), and a [2010 letter from Jo Haddox to Terrie](/docs/letters/jo-haddox-to-terrie-2010-11-12/).
