## 2025 — the most-documented year

The Asti Tour de Vine is the **fourth 2025 ride documented in this archive**, alongside:

- [Waves to Wine + Tour de Cure combined recap](/docs/2025-ms-waves-to-wine-and-tour-de-cure-bike-ride/) — the two anchored annual rides
- [Mo'omomi Beach Molokai](/docs/2025-moomomi-beach-molokai-bike-ride/) — the corpus's first non-mainland entry

Four documented rides in one calendar year is the densest single-year section of the [bike-rides corpus](/docs/charity-bike-rides/) so far &mdash; a year that saw the practice extend from its standard Bay Area routes through Sonoma's wine country to the Hawaiian islands.
