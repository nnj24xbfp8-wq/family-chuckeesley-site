## Two anchors, one cycling year

The 2025 recap is significant because **the same video carries both threads of the charity-rides corpus**:

- The **family-anchored half** &mdash; the ADA Tour de Cure for diabetes, anchored in [Charlie Eesley](/family/charles-eesley/)'s November 2015 death from diabetes complications, by 2025 in its ninth or tenth year as a continuous standing arrangement
- The **friendship-anchored half** &mdash; the MS Waves to Wine for multiple sclerosis, anchored in Paul Twohey's [2014 introduction-year](/docs/2014-ms-waves-to-wine-bike-ride/) and extended through years of meeting people living with MS in the riding community

2025 is also the year of the [Mo'omomi Beach Molokai ride](/docs/2025-moomomi-beach-molokai-bike-ride/), which makes it the year **three rides documented** &mdash; the year the corpus's geographic reach first extended beyond mainland California, with the Molokai trip echoing the deeper Hawaii thread that the 1953 Eesley anniversary trip set running through the archive.
