## Six years in — the practice as practice

By 2024 the post-Charlie ADA Tour de Cure rides had reached **roughly the eight-year mark** since the practice began c. 2016, a year after [Charlie Eesley](/family/charles-eesley/)'s November 2015 death from diabetes complications. The 2024 ride is the **third ADA ride documented in this archive** (after the [2018 ride](/docs/2018-ada-tour-de-cure-bike-ride/) and the various intermediate-year rides whose videos are pending in the corpus).

Eight years of an annual practice is the point at which a thing is no longer a response to a single event but a **continuous standing arrangement** &mdash; the [Family Threads #7 (Showing up)](/docs/family-threads/) pattern's whole point. The 2024 entry's significance is its very ordinariness: the same ride, the same cause, the same two riders, the same five-minute video format, recorded again.
