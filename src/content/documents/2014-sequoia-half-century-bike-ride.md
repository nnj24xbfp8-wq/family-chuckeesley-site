## A note on dating — the corpus is older than 2015

The 2014 ride pre-dates [Charlie Eesley](/family/charles-eesley/)'s **26 November 2015 death** from diabetes complications &mdash; the event that anchored Chuck and Lijie's subsequent annual [ADA Tour de Cure](https://diabetes.org/tour) and [MS Society Waves to Wine](https://www.nationalmssociety.org/get-involved/events-and-fundraisers) charity rides. **The cycling practice itself is therefore older than the charity-rides corpus**: the 2014 Sequoia documents the Bay Area-cycling-club piece of Chuck and Lijie's life that was already in place a year before the loss that organized the charity rides around it.

The parent [bike-rides document](/docs/charity-bike-rides/) collects both threads &mdash; the long-running club rides (the Sequoia entries from 2014 onward) and the post-2015 anchored charity rides &mdash; under the same heading, since they are the same cycling practice in two registers.
