## What the letter is

8 December 1970, from the comfortable rear. The letter's spine is a plan: he hopes to place a phone call to Terrie the following Monday, 13 December &mdash; the call the [14 December letter](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1970-12-14/) confirms he made (*"As you know I called you today"*). Around the plan is the good life of the rear-area posting, the PX, the mess hall, the courts, the daily movie, the cheap booze, set down with a short-timer's satisfaction: *"so I'm happy."* It is the Camp Eagle rear at its most livable, weeks before the field would call again.

## Transcription

> *Dec 8*
>
> Dear Terrie,
>
> As you probably know by now, I hope to call you next Monday night, on Dec 13. If I don't get through, at least I will have tried. It will be expensive for you, so don't be stubborn and use the money I gave you. I hope you are eating enough anyway.
>
> *[A short family-health passage is held private at the family's discretion.]*
>
> The PX prices here are really good. The mess hall is great. They even have Vietnamese girls to clear the tables so I don't even have to carry my tray anymore after I'm through eating. There are tennis, basketball and handball courts plus a movie which changes pictures every day. [Booze] is just a little over $2.00 a bottle so I'm happy.
>
> Well I guess I'll close for now. I hope everything is alright with you and I'll try to write in a day or so.
>
> Love
> Charles

## What the letter records

**The phone call, planned.** *"I hope to call you next Monday night, on Dec 13... don't be stubborn and use the money I gave you."* The MARS radio-telephone call from Vietnam to the U.S. that the [14 December letter](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1970-12-14/) reports he made two days later, billed to the recipient at long-distance rates &mdash; here anticipated, and pre-paid out of the money he'd sent her, over her likely objection to the cost.

**The rear-area good life.** *"The PX prices here are really good. The mess hall is great... tennis, basketball and handball courts plus a movie which changes pictures every day."* The rear cantonment amenities the [16 December enlisted-men's-club letter](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1970-12-16-em-club-photo/) also describes &mdash; the comfortable counterweight to the field months, listed with the small pleasures of a soldier who had been in the mountains and was, for now, not.

**"Vietnamese girls to clear the tables."** The Vietnamese-civilian labor around American bases, the same base-economy the [em-club letter](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1970-12-16-em-club-photo/)'s hostesses belong to &mdash; here the mess-hall staff who cleared the trays.
