## What the letter is

An undated letter — placed here at **c. September 1971**, in the weeks after [Charlie](/family/charles-eesley/) and [Terrie](/family/terrie-lee-eesley/)'s 1971 wedding — from **[Peggy McMaster Eesley](/family/margaret-mcmaster-eesley/)** to the newlyweds, preserved as a transcription in the [Wildermuth family book](/docs/wildermuth-family-of-marietta-book/). The canning season and the plan to drive [Anne](/family/anne-eesley/) "up to Madison" (the University of Wisconsin) both point to late summer / early fall 1971.

## Transcription

> Dear **Charles & Terrie**,
>
> I'm sending the newspaper picture & account — it came out ok., I think.
>
> From **Mrs. Morris** (who sent the gorgeous plate) in church today. She said that the wedding was simply perfect and in such good taste. This is what we hear on all sides — so you can feel that you did yourselves proud.
>
> I'm sending the one glossy print or news account to your \_\_\_\_\_. There's a second glossy print which the paper will return. I would like to give it to **Kay & Ted** [?]. We ordered a portrait for you which I'll send over when it is finished.
>
> We had our party for the **Danders** on their 25th anniversary. It was at Murray's **"Crow's Nest"** which is located by **Devol's Dam**. He's got it fixed up really nice. Everyone enjoyed the food & the company — big evening. Next weekend we plan to go to Columbus to visit **Schramms** — I can use a change of pace.
>
> Have been canning like mad — tomatoes, beans, peaches. This afternoon I made jam, spiced peaches, sugar canned & sugar substitute canned ones. \_\_ \_\_\_\_\_.
>
> The next project is to get **Anne** to **Madison**. It seems that I'm selected to drive her up — do hope she makes it this time.
>
> Are you reducing your insulin dose, **Charles**? When the brewer's yeast pills come, & you run out of magnesium (or multi-\_\_\_\_) pills — just try the yeast tablets alone for a while — see if your insulin dose (small as possible) will hold you — Much \_\_\_\_\_ to you both — **Mother**

## Notes

The letter is a small portrait of the Eesley household the season after the wedding: the newspaper write-up circulating, the compliments "on all sides," the anniversary party at a local supper spot by the Muskingum's Devol's Dam, and Peggy's canning marathon. It closes on the note that recurs across this family's correspondence — **Charlie's diabetes** — with Peggy, ever practical, coaching him to hold the insulin dose "small as possible."
