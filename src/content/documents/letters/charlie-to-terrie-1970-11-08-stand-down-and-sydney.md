## What the letter is

Early-to-mid November 1970, written in pencil from a firebase in the mountains. It sits a few days after the [5 November letter](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1970-11-05-extension-plan-and-congressman/), the mail-gap count having climbed from fourteen days to sixteen. The texture is the same field misery, rain, no mail, the slow grind, but the horizon is opening: a stand-down on the 13th, a move to the lowlands, and the first floated R&R idea of the tour, Sydney in the southern summer. The day-count, "day 180 over here. Just 217 more to go," is the arithmetic that the extension letters would soon sharpen into a fixed out-date.

## Transcription

> Dear Terrie,
>
> Well after the 1 day [pause] on the firebase it seems we will now be going in on Nov 13 for 1 week stand-down. I'm sorry if you haven't received any mail but it has been 16 days now since I or anybody else out here has received any mail and I will probably stretch to 21 days without mail. I know they are having problems but they couldn't be that bad in the rear. At least after the stand-down we are scheduled to go to the lowlands and some other poor battalion is supposed to come up to these mountains.
>
> The days are dreary but seem to go very fast. It was sunny for 3 days but is now raining again and we are socked in. Today is
>
> [page 2:] day 180 over here. Just 217 more to go. Heavens I'm not even half way yet!
>
> I think I'll go to Sydney for R&R sometime in Jan or early Feb. It will be summer down there and at least the beach and food should be good for a change. That too is still a long ways off.
>
> Well, no contact with the enemy now for nearly a month thank heavens. If it can just keep up everything will be fine. There is nothing I need right now except a few letters. I think I'll close now and add more tomorrow or so.
>
> Love
> Charles

## What the letter records

**The mail gap, growing.** Sixteen days now, maybe stretching to twenty-one, up from the fourteen in the [5 November letter](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1970-11-05-extension-plan-and-congressman/). The apology to Terrie, "I'm sorry if you haven't received any mail," is for a silence he can't help; the mail simply wasn't moving to the firebase.

**Day 180.** "Just 217 more to go. Heavens I'm not even half way yet!" The mid-tour low point, named in the arithmetic. The 217 days from here land in mid-June 1971, the out-date the [5 November extension plan](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1970-11-05-extension-plan-and-congressman/) had just laid out.

**The Sydney idea.** The first R&R destination Charlie floats, Sydney in the southern summer, before the plan settled on the April 1971 Hawaii trip with Terrie. A glimpse of the road not taken in the R&R thread.
