## What the letter is

15 February 1971. A waiting letter, written in the gap between the [clerk job coming through](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1971-02-08-clerk-job-camp-eagle/) and Charlie actually [reporting to Camp Eagle](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1971-02-21-camp-eagle-finally-here/) a week later. He is in Da Nang, orders not yet cut, so unsettled that he can&rsquo;t tell Terrie where to send his mail. The register is loose and rear-area &mdash; a beach, a sunburn, a ridiculous birthday yo-yo &mdash; and the real work of the letter is arithmetic: how to get Terrie to Hawaii in April on a private&rsquo;s pay.

<aside class="not-prose my-8 border-l-4 border-amber-700 bg-amber-50/60 dark:bg-amber-950/20 px-6 py-5 rounded-r">

**Mid-February 1971 &mdash; the rear, while Laos heats up**

By 15 February, [Operation Lam Son 719](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Lam_Son_719) &mdash; the ARVN incursion into Laos, launched 8 February with US air and artillery support &mdash; was a week old and intensifying; Charlie&rsquo;s *&ldquo;the activity is picking up over here&hellip; a lot of guys are going to get messed up&rdquo;* is that campaign seen from the rear. He himself is out of the field, waiting on the clerk-typist orders that had cleared the day before in the [14 February valentine](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1971-02-14-valentines-clerk-orders/) but had not yet physically moved him; his worry about being *&ldquo;screwed again&rdquo;* and sent back out is the fear that the reprieve won&rsquo;t hold.

The Hawaii budget is the same machinery tracked across the winter: R&amp;R allocations claimed in advance for a set window (here 9&ndash;19 April), the serviceman&rsquo;s pay locked until departure, and the wife-or-fianc&eacute;e&rsquo;s airfare the single largest cost. The *&ldquo;Australia or Thailand&rdquo;* fallback names the other common R&amp;R destinations of the period, held in reserve in case Terrie couldn&rsquo;t make the trip.

*Sources: [Wikipedia: Operation Lam Son 719](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Lam_Son_719); [Wikipedia: R&amp;R (military)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%26R_(military)).*

## Transcription

> *Feb 15*
>
> Dear Terrie,
>
> I really wouldn't know where to tell you to send my letters. I'm still here in Danang awaiting orders. The army is very slow. I'm having a good time here. I was at the beach the other day and have a fairly good burn. I guess I don't tan very well as I've been burned several times, almost every time I'm out in the sun. Over all I'm probably darker than I've ever been. I'm still light though.
>
> It seems the activity is picking up over here. A lot of guys are going to get messed up. If I get screwed again and go back to the field it will be a while after we get back that we get out to the field again. It seems they have given all our equipment out to new GI's just in country so we will all have to get new equipment. They will have a mess on their hands once again
>
> [page 2:] all this activity should be quieted down again by May or late April.
>
> I will try to get some material on R&R in Hawaii for you. Also I'll try for April 9-19 (a week in there some place) and let you know. I guess it would be best for you to save all your questions & comments til I find out my address for certain. It seems the highest cost will be your plane fare. The rest will cost between $200-$400 for room, food & entertainment. We could always eat hot dogs and stay in bed for the week to save money.
>
> If by some chance you can't come I would then either go to [Hawaii — struck through] Australia or Thailand.
>
> My parents and Anne sent me a yo-yo, paddle ball, and top for my birthday. Really ridiculous but I've had fun with it so far. Please don't send anything as I don't really need it. Save your money&hellip;
>
> [The page carrying the sign-off is not located.]

## What the letter records

**Not knowing his own address.** *&ldquo;I really wouldn&rsquo;t know where to tell you to send my letters&rdquo;* is the whole administrative limbo of mid-February in one line: the clerk job approved, the orders not yet cut, Charlie physically in Da Nang but institutionally nowhere. It resolves within the week when he reaches [Camp Eagle](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1971-02-21-camp-eagle-finally-here/).

**The Hawaii arithmetic.** The trip is the organizing hope of the spring, and here it is at its most concrete &mdash; a dated window (9&ndash;19 April), a cost breakdown, and the tender joke about hot dogs. The same budget hardens into a deadline in the [24 February letter](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1971-02-24-five-days-in-and-rr-cost/) (the 5&ndash;6 March allocations) and, by spring, into the [second R&amp;R booking](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1971-03-29-second-rr-booking/).

**The yo-yo.** The gifts from his parents and his sister [Anne](/family/anne-eesley/) &mdash; a yo-yo, a paddle ball, a top &mdash; are the family reaching across the distance with the smallest, most childish things, and Charlie, twenty-one and counting down a combat tour, playing with them anyway.
