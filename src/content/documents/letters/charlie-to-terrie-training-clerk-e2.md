## What the letter is

A short letter from **clerk training** &mdash; the Army teaching Charlie to type and to fill out its forms *"just so."* It is a small, funny, exasperated page (*"I care but I don't care"*), and it matters mostly for where it points: the clerk skills he is failing to love here are the ones that, a year later, land him the [Camp Eagle clerk desk in Vietnam](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1971-02-21-camp-eagle-finally-here/) &mdash; the rear-area job that took him off the line. The complaint is the origin of the reprieve.

<aside class="not-prose my-8 border-l-4 border-amber-700 bg-amber-50/60 dark:bg-amber-950/20 px-6 py-5 rounded-r">

**Undated — date on the envelope**

No date on the page. The postmark survives on the envelope and will be added later. It is placed provisionally in the training run: Charlie is at a training post (*"just waiting here"*), learning clerical work, at the rank of **E-2** &mdash; one grade up from the E-1 of induction, the junior rank of the earliest service months.

</aside>

## Transcription

> Dear Terrie,
>
> Got your letter today. One mistake, I'm not a PFC. A PFC is the rank of E-3 and I'm only an E-2 up from E-1.
>
> I type too slow and make too many mistakes to be a good clerk I believe. They keep throwing things at me. I guess it is because it is all new to me and their forms have to be just so. I care but I don't care. At least now we get time to ourselves. It is very boring just waiting here. I've seen quite a few very bad movies.
>
> I hope you do better this semester with 18 hours. Too bad Sandy isn't going full time. I just hope you finish. It hinted in your letter you would be staying at Maryland. Don't work too hard.
>
> Love
> Charles

## What the letter records

**Clerk training, and the seed of the Camp Eagle job.** *"I type too slow and make too many mistakes to be a good clerk... their forms have to be just so."* The Army clerical training Charlie is unimpressed by here is the exact skill set that would later matter enormously: the [February 1971 move to the Camp Eagle clerk desk](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1971-02-21-camp-eagle-finally-here/) &mdash; *"I'm in a relatively safe area"* &mdash; was the rear job that pulled him out of the field for the back half of his tour. He learned it grumbling.

**"I care but I don't care."** The exact register of the whole training correspondence: the divided attention of a drafted man doing what the Army requires without letting it own him. It's the same voice as the *"it is all so petty"* of the [NCO-school letter](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1969-nco-school/) and the *"beginning to hate the army"* refrain.

**"E-2 up from E-1."** The rank correction dates the letter early: E-2 (Private) is one grade above the E-1 of the first months of service, well before the Spec 4 (E-4) he would make in Vietnam by [November 1970](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1970-11-15-spec-4-and-inquiry-over/).

**Terrie's 18 hours.** *"I hope you do better this semester with 18 hours... I just hope you finish."* The steady thread of him tracking and pushing Terrie's schooling at the University of Maryland &mdash; the *"keep studying"* that runs from the training letters through the [field letters](/docs/letters/charlie-to-terrie-1970-10-19-160-days/) a year later.
