## What this is

A single leaf — the second page, marked *"– 2 –"* at the top — of a family letter that otherwise has not survived in this archive. It surfaced among unplaced scans during the July 2026 QA pass and was placed once [Chuck identified *"Aunt Ota"* as his great-great-aunt **Dr. [Scioto Mafry (Ota) Chenoweth Smith](/family/scioto-mafry-chenoweth/)**](/family/scioto-mafry-chenoweth/). It is written in the hand of **[Lillie Dale Chenoweth Eesley](/family/lillie-dale-chenoweth/)** (see the attribution note below).

## Transcription (page 2)

> *…I know I have given you their address before, but in your shuffling about so thought it possible you might have lost it. **Uncle Howard** was here a short time after dinner & he & **Aunt Ota** & I sorted our pictures to put in the albums for **Chauncey & Ada** so you [could] see all them & tell about them. Want to get it off tomorrow. You are a long ways off now & you can imagine how glad you'd be to see somebody from home if you had been out there **nineteen or twenty years as Cousin Ada & Chauncey have**. I think but—*

*(The page breaks off mid-sentence; a page 3 is not held in the archive.)*

## Who the letter names

- **"Aunt Ota"** — **[Scioto Mafry "Ota" Chenoweth Smith](/family/scioto-mafry-chenoweth/)** (1871–1930), the physician; [Lillie Dale](/family/lillie-dale-chenoweth/)'s older sister. *"Ota"* was her lifelong family nickname, from *Scioto*.
- **"Uncle Howard"** — **Howard Glen Chenoweth**, Ota and Lillie Dale's eldest brother, a son of [Joseph Hill Chenoweth](/family/joseph-hill-chenoweth/) and [Mary O. Timmons](/family/mary-ohio-timmons-chenoweth/). (Not yet a person entry in this archive.)
- **"Cousin Chauncey" and "Ada"** — **[Chauncey "Chant" Chenoweth](/family/chauncey-chenoweth/)**, the cousin who had moved to California, and Ada (his wife or companion; not yet separately documented). The letter's *"nineteen or twenty years"* out there points to a move around 1901–1903 and a letter written around **1921–1922**.

That the writer calls her own siblings *"Uncle Howard"* and *"Aunt Ota,"* and her own cousin *"Cousin Chauncey,"* is the ordinary way a mother refers to relatives when writing **to her child** — using the child's terms, not her own.

## Attribution — the hand is Lillie Dale's

This page carries no signature or salutation, so the writer is established by **handwriting**, not by a signed name. Set beside Lillie Dale's confirmed [1939 letter to Leonard and Helen](/docs/lillie-dale-1939-letter-after-dales-death/) — a securely-identified sample of her hand — the two match closely: the same moderate right slant, the same constantly-looped ampersand, the same distinctive rendering of *"you"* (written so it reads almost as *"yau"*), and the same habit of **underlining a word or phrase for emphasis** (*"nineteen or twenty years"* here; *"lots"* in 1939). The hand is Lillie Dale Chenoweth Eesley's.

The **recipient** is not named on this page, but is very likely her son **[Leonard David Eesley](/family/leonard-david-eesley/)**: the letter is written to someone *"a long ways off… in your shuffling about,"* and it coordinates family photographs for **Chauncey and Ada in California** — matching the summer **1922** in which Lillie wrote Leonard, then on his solo cross-country car trip west, and told him *"to be sure to visit Chant"* (see [Chauncey's page](/family/chauncey-chenoweth/) and the [Charles Leonard Eesley letter collection](/docs/charles-leonard-letter-collection/)). Recipient and exact date remain inferred pending page 1 or a matching envelope.
