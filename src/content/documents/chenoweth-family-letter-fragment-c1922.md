## What this is

A single leaf — the second page, marked *"– 2 –"* at the top — of a Chenoweth family letter that otherwise has not survived in this archive. It surfaced among unplaced scans during the July 2026 QA pass and was placed once [Chuck identified *"Aunt Ota"* as his great-great-aunt **Dr. [Scioto Mafry (Ota) Chenoweth Smith](/family/scioto-mafry-chenoweth/)**](/family/scioto-mafry-chenoweth/).

## Transcription (page 2)

> *…I know I have given you their address before, but in your shuffling about so thought it possible you might have lost it. **Uncle Howard** was here a short time after dinner & he & **Aunt Ota** & I sorted our pictures to put in the albums for **Chauncey & Ada** so you [could] see all them & tell about them. Want to get it off tomorrow. You are a long ways off now & you can imagine how glad you'd be to see somebody from home if you had been out there **nineteen or twenty years as Cousin Ada & Chauncey have**. I think but—*

*(The page breaks off mid-sentence; a page 3 is not held in the archive.)*

## Who the letter names

- **"Aunt Ota"** — **[Scioto Mafry "Ota" Chenoweth Smith](/family/scioto-mafry-chenoweth/)** (1871–1930), the physician; identified by Chuck in 2026. *"Ota"* was her lifelong family nickname, from *Scioto*.
- **"Uncle Howard"** — **Howard Glen Chenoweth**, Ota's elder brother and the eldest of [Joseph Hill Chenoweth](/family/joseph-hill-chenoweth/) and [Mary O. Timmons](/family/mary-ohio-timmons-chenoweth/)'s children. (Not yet a person entry in this archive.)
- **"Cousin Chauncey" and "Ada"** — **[Chauncey "Chant" Chenoweth](/family/chauncey-chenoweth/)**, the cousin who had moved to California, and Ada (his wife or companion; not yet separately documented). Chauncey's page records him settled on the West Coast by 1922 — and the letter's *"nineteen or twenty years"* out there points to a move around 1901–1903 and a letter written around **1921–1922**.

## What is not yet known

The **writer** and the **recipient** are not identified from this page. Two internal clues place them: the writer speaks of *"Uncle Howard," "Aunt Ota,"* and *"Cousin Chauncey,"* which puts the writer in the generation of [Lillie Dale Chenoweth Eesley](/family/lillie-dale-chenoweth/)'s children (Ota and Howard's nieces and nephews); and the recipient is away from home and *"shuffling about,"* like someone traveling. That is consistent with the 1922 cross-country correspondence surrounding [Leonard David Eesley](/family/leonard-david-eesley/)'s solo car trip to California — the same trip in which his mother Lillie Dale asked him *"to be sure to visit Chant"* (see [Chauncey's page](/family/chauncey-chenoweth/)) — but this page carries neither a signature nor a salutation, so the identification of writer and recipient is left open pending page 1, a matching hand, or family memory.
