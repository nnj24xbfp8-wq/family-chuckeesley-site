## Ten years in &mdash; and a recurring riding partner

The 2024 entry sits **roughly a decade into the MS ride practice**, which began with the [2014 Waves to Wine](/docs/2014-ms-waves-to-wine-bike-ride/) ride that Chuck's friend Paul Twohey got him onto. Over the years the practice has continued and accumulated &mdash; the [2015 ride](/docs/2015-ms-waves-to-wine-bike-ride/) is the previously documented Waves to Wine entry on this archive.

**[Vasyl Dub](https://vasyldub.com/)** is a recurring cycling partner of Chuck and Lijie's, also appearing on the [2026 Sequoia Half Century Stanford team](/docs/2026-sequoia-half-century-bike-ride/). His presence on the 2024 Waves to Wine ride is part of the **growing circle of friends-on-the-bike** that the riding life has built up across the years. The MS rides anchored in friendship (Paul Twohey's introduction-year) are now being extended through the friendships built on the rides themselves.
