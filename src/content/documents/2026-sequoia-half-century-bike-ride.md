## The ride

The **[Western Wheelers Bicycle Club](https://westernwheelers.org/)** of Palo Alto runs an annual signature century each summer in the Santa Cruz Mountains and South Bay foothills called the **[Sequoia Century](https://westernwheelers.org/event/sequoia-century/)**. The full course offers a 100-mile century, an 82-mile century, a 100-kilometer (62-mile) **metric century** / *"Half Century,"* and shorter options. The 2026 edition is the ride this entry documents.

Chuck and Lijie rode the **metric century / Half Century route** with a **Stanford-affiliated team** of five:

- **Chuck Eesley**
- **Lijie Zhou**
- **[Vasyl Dub](https://vasyldub.com/)**
- **Anton**
- **Andrew**

The route ran through the redwoods, oak woodland, and small-road climbs the South Bay foothills are known for &mdash; the kind of long-distance group ride that the Bay Area cycling community has run for half a century, with the Western Wheelers as one of its central long-running clubs.
