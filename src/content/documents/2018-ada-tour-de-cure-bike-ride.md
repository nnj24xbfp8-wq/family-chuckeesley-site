## Why ADA Tour de Cure — Charlie Eesley

The ADA Tour de Cure is the load-bearing ride of the entire [charity-rides corpus](/docs/charity-bike-rides/) on this archive. **Chuck's father [Charlie Eesley](/family/charles-eesley/) died on 26 November 2015 of complications from diabetes**, after carrying Type 2 diabetes for years. The years of small daily diabetic toll on heart, kidneys, eyes, and feet ended in the cause of death recorded on his certificate.

Chuck and Lijie began riding in the ADA Tour de Cure in the first year or two after Charlie's death. The 2018 ride is the **earliest of those ADA rides documented in this archive**, but the practice itself began earlier in 2016-2017. The ride is the **direct organized response** to the loss &mdash; raising money for the institutional ADA infrastructure (research, awareness, patient care) that addresses the disease that killed Charlie.

Parallel to the riding runs **Chuck's venture investment in [Empo Health](https://www.empohealth.com/)**, the company building diagnostic technology for **diabetic foot ulcers** &mdash; one of the most under-treated late-stage complications of advanced diabetes and a leading cause of lower-limb amputation. The Tour de Cure rides fund the research-and-awareness layer; the Empo Health investment funds the specific clinical-technology piece. **The same impulse runs through both.**
